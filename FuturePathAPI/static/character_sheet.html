Standard Conditions
Total concealment. Takes 3Ad (Disadvantage) on melee attacks, DEX, and Perception checks. Cannot read or perform visual tasks.
Takes 1Ad (Disadvantage) on Initiative and Perception checks. Passive Perception reduced by 1/2 WIS modifier (round down).
Mentally befuddled; treats all targets as enemies. Takes 1Ad (Disadvantage) on INT and WIS checks. If attacked, must attack last attacker. Otherwise, acts randomly.
Takes 1Ad (Disadvantage) on Attack Rolls and DEX saves. Immobile if anchored; half speed and no running/charging if unanchored. Attackers gain 1Ad (Advantage) against target.
Caught unaware or unable to react. Loses Dexterity bonus to AC.
Paralyzed, sleeping, or unconscious. Effective AC = 5 + size mod. Vulnerable to Coup de Grace. Passive Perception reduced to 1/4 (round down), no skill checks.
Standing up is a Simple Action (prevents Bonus Action). Melee attacks take 1Ad (Disadvantage), no techniques. Gains 1Ad (Advantage) on Ranged attacks against targets > 10ft away.
Triggered at 0 HP. Roll 1d4 CON damage each turn in combat (every 20 mins out of combat). Move walk speed only. Stabilized by DC 20 Medical check.
Status Tracks
Fatigue Track:
Winded: Fatigued penalty, removed by 1-min breather. Winded + Winded = Fatigued.
Fatigued: Half speed. 1Ad (Disadvantage) on STR/DEX saves & Combat Melee Tech. -1 Melee attacks, temp -2 DEX. Needs 8 hrs rest.
Exhausted: No Bonus Actions, Run, Sprint, or Melee Tech. 1Ad (Disadvantage) on Melee, -2 Ranged. Temp -2 STR, -4 DEX. Needs 8 hrs rest.
Fear Track:
Frightened: 1Ad (Disadvantage) on all actions.
Cowering: Frozen. Lose DEX to AC/saves/skills, 2Ad (Disadvantage) on all actions. DC 13 WIS check each turn.
Panicked: Flees by most direct route. Can only defend, cannot attack.
Grapple Track:
Grappled: Attack only with bare hands, light weapon, or escape. Lose DEX to AC (except vs grappler).
Pinned: Immobile. Lose DEX to AC against all attackers.
Nausea Track:
Nauseated: Cannot gain Advantage. 2Ad (Disadvantage) on attention-requiring actions.
Sickened: Treated as Nauseated. Lose 1 CON per day (rolled) until cured via Medical check or death.
Shaken Track:
Shaken: 1Ad (Disadvantage) on attacks, saves, and skill checks.
Dazed: Cannot gain Advantage, 2Ad (Disadvantage) on all actions. Retains normal AC (1 rd).
Stunned: Lose DEX to AC, drop held items, cannot attack/move (1 rd).
Paralyzed: Cannot move or act. Considered Helpless.
