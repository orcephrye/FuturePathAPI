# Character Creation & Advancement Summary

## 1. Character Creation Steps
1. **Species:** Grants Size (default Medium), Speed (default Walk 15 ft, Run 30 ft, Swim/Climb 10 ft, Burrow 5 ft), Tech Level (default 2), and Traits.
2. **Proficiency Levels:**
   - **Tech:** Set by species.
   - **Melee/Ranged Weapons:** Choose starting split of 2/2, 3/1, or 1/3 levels.
   - **Armor:** Default level 2. **Power Armor:** Level 0 (requires Power Armor Expert feat). **Item Use:** Level 0.
3. **Path:** Provides Advantage Die, Ability Affinity, Hit Die (HD), Favored Skills, Skill Points/lvl, and Level 1 Feat/Talent choices.
4. **Ability Themes:**
   - *Standard Spread:* 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8.
   - *Point Buy:* 15 points (base 10 in all stats; point cost table: 7 = -4 pts up to 18 = 17 pts).
   - *Dice:* Roll 4d6, drop lowest (6 times, re-roll totals < 7).
   - *Modifier Formula:* `(Score / 2) - 5` (round down).
5. **Calculated Stats:**
   - **Level 1 HP:** Max HD value + CON Modifier.
   - **Passive Perception:** `10 + WIS Modifier`.
   - **Initiative:** `DEX Modifier + WIS Modifier`.
   - **AC:** `10 + Armor Bonus + DEX Mod + Size Mod + Natural Armor + Misc Mod`.
   - **Flat-Footed AC:** AC without DEX Mod. **Touch AC:** AC without Armor Bonus.
6. **Skills:**
   - Favored Skills cost 1 pt/rank; non-favored cost 2 pts/rank.
   - Max Rank = `1/2 Level` (round up). Language skill has no level limit.
7. **Feats & Talents:**
   - 1 Path Talent at Level 1.
   - 1 General Feat at Level 1 (+1 Bonus Feat for Humans or via Path option).
8. **Occupation & Wealth:**
   - *Occupation:* Credit Score = Min Credit + WIS Mod (ignore negatives). ISK = Monthly Income × WIS Mod (ignore negatives).
   - *Default:* $4,000/level, Credit Score = `4 + WIS Mod`.
9. **Equipment:** Starter Packs grant up to 15 non-masterwork items ≤ Tech Level without subtracting ISK. Weapons & Armor require spending starting ISK.

### Level Up Checklist
1. **HP Gain:**
   - *Default:* `(HD / 2) + 1 + CON Mod`.
   - *Roll:* `1dHD + CON Mod`.
   - *Low Fantasy:* `CON Mod` (min 1).
   - *High Fantasy:* `Max HD + CON Mod`.
2. **Skill Points:** `1 + INT Mod` or `2 + INT Mod` (based on Path).
3. **Feats & Talents:** Gain Path Talent at 1st, 6th, 12th, 18th. Gain Feat on odd levels (alternating Tactical / Utility).
4. **Custom Bonus (4th, 8th, 16th, 20th):** +1 to two Ability Scores OR 1 Misc Feat (+1 to two Ability Scores at 12th).
5. **Professions (Level 2+):** Gain Advanced Class talents, features, and techniques.
6. **Proficiency:** Gain +1 Training Point.
7. **Companions:** Level scales relative to owner level.
