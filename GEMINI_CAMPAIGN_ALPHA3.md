# FuturePath Alpha 3 Campaign: Shadows of the DMZ

## Campaign Background
Located in the Orion-Cygnus arm, Earth sat inside the **A.P.E.Z.** (Jovian Advancing People's Exclusion Zone)—a protected buffer zone established by the robotic **Jove** between the rival Cryous and Lepidonan empires. Blocked by the zone, the two empires clashed directly, sparking a rebellion that saw two subjugated species (the Peoples of Sayor and the Kurgian Republic) break away to form an independent Federation, later legitimized by a peace treaty arbitrated by the neutral Graylings.

When humanity reached sub-light spaceflight (0.05–0.10c), the Jove granted humanity ownership of the resource-rich, 250-light-year territory, allowing alien interaction but strictly forbidding forced conquest. Before any faction could convince humanity to ally or cede the land, a devastating world war erupted on Earth—triggered in part by the shock of alien contact and advanced technology. With no unified human government left to sign treaties, the Jove refuse to recognize any foreign claims, freezing out the desperate empires and leaving open the question of whether humanity destroyed itself or was pushed.

## Modern Events
Seventy years after Humanity's catastrophic System War 1 (WW3)—and a century after the Federation's hard-won peace with the Lepidonians—the border has plunged back into chaos.

Desperate to prevent total destabilization, the Federation has rushed its cutting-edge Tech 3 flagship, the *FSS Vanguard* (or player-chosen name), to the front lines before completing its scheduled shakedown cruise. As the Federation's elite naval crew, your mission is to restore order to a volatile border on the brink of war and uncover the shadowy plots threatening the peace.

## Campaign Continuity: The Legacy of 'The Claws' (Prequel Context)
*Conspiracy at the Border* is the direct narrative continuation of the Level 1–5 campaign, *The Claws*, but experienced from an entirely different perspective. While the previous adventure followed chaotic rogues and mercenaries fighting through the underworld, this campaign is a Star Trek-inspired, naval command sci-fi campaign.

### What Happened in 'The Claws' (Level 1–5 Prequel Summary)
- **The Setup & Amnesia:** The players began as members of an infamous mercenary band known as **The Claws**. They awoke inside a high-security penal facility with their memories wiped, knowing only that a prominent magistrate named **Judge Carthic** had sentenced them without a public trial.
- **The Acbar Devastation:** Escaping custody, The Claws tore through the neutral trade metropolis of **Acbar**, leaving catastrophic destruction, scorched infrastructure, and civil panic in their wake—the very crisis that has now forced Acbar into total quarantine and system-wide blackout.
- **The Uncovered Conspiracy:** In their search for answers, The Claws uncovered smoking-gun evidence proving Judge Carthic was taking astronomical bribes, exposed corrupt Human corporate oligarchs scheming to exploit the turmoil to crown themselves Humanity's new rulers, and revealed that the **Lepidonian Empire** was orchestrating the entire border destabilization.
- **The Clone Double-Cross:** Fearing an inevitable Lepidonian double-cross, the original Claws had secretly cloned themselves, wiped the clones' memories, and arranged for their amnesiac duplicates to take the fall. This gave the original mercenaries the cover to assume new identities and vanish. The Lepidonians paid Judge Carthic lavishly to convict and bury the "Claws" swiftly in kangaroo proceedings to keep their operations buried.
- **The Climax & Departure:** In the end, the clones discovered the truth, tracked down their original counterparts, and fought a brutal mirror-match. The victorious survivors decided they wanted nothing more to do with this sector's escalating war; they abandoned the region entirely to seek fortune elsewhere in the galaxy.
- **The Claws Are No More:** The Claws are gone and play no further role in this story. However, the wake of their chaos set the entire sector ablaze.

### Tone & Perspective Shift: From Scoundrels to Starfleet
- **The Perspective:** Instead of chaotic rogues, swindlers, and mercenary outlaws, players now step into the boots of disciplined commissioned officers in the **Federation Navy** (the joint alliance of the Peoples of Sayor and the Kurgian Republic).
- **The Tone:** High-stakes naval military sci-fi. Players command an advanced capital flagship, manage bridge stations, navigate tense diplomatic crises, conduct tactical away-team operations, and wage tactical starship combat to uphold Federation law.
- **The Mandate:** The Claws' rampage exposed Carthic and set off riots on Sawn Prime, while Acbar was plunged into darkness and Tarienth into rebellion. The Federation must now pick up the pieces, de-escalate the border, and expose the Lepidonian plot before the Grayling Envoy rules against them.

### The New Mercenary Threat: The Iron Mandible
With The Claws having fled the sector, the Lepidonians have hired a new, ruthless proxy mercenary syndicate: **The Iron Mandible**.
- **Carthic's Connection:** Judge Carthic personally knows of The Iron Mandible and knows the Lepidonian handlers who finance their contracts.
- **Their Mission in Escort the Judge:** The Lepidonians want Judge Carthic **alive** to interrogate him and recover his encrypted data manifests before Federation Intelligence can crack them. The Iron Mandible's assault on the *FSS Vanguard* is strictly an **extraction ("rescue") mission**. They will only attempt to execute Carthic or destroy the ship if they realize they cannot get him off alive.
- **Beaming Extraction Dynamic:** The mercenaries use Shield Piercers to drop the flagship's shields. Once inside Cell Block B, if Carthic is extracted while the ship's shields remain down, the mercenaries will attempt to **beam off with him**. If the player crew restores shields in time, beaming is jammed, trapping the strike team and forcing a desperate, lethal standoff.

## From the Eyes of the Federation
From the Federation's perspective, the Lepidonians are laying the groundwork for war by manufacturing treaty violations. They have formally accused the Federation of breaching their century-old peace pact on three specific counts:
1. Abandoning commitments to terraform **Tarienth**.
2. Covertly manipulating Humanity toward Federation membership.
3. Sabotaging the neutral trade hub of **Acbar**.

To validate these claims, the Lepidonians have summoned a Grayling Envoy to inspect the border. The stakes are existential: if the Graylings rule the treaty breached, they will revoke diplomatic protection, leaving the Federation exposed to unprovoked Lepidonian aggression. With the border currently in chaos, the Federation must restore order immediately before the arriving Envoy mistakes unrest for guilt.

## Campaign Rules & Player Options

- **Missions**: The general mission is to bring order to this sector of space and stop a war from happening. Your team will undertake 3 major missions:
  1. **Escort the Judge** *(Must be done first)*: The ‘Honorable’ Judge Carthic has been accused of collusion with Lepidonians, and his alleged behavior has sparked violent riots in Sayor space.
  2. **Control of Tarienth**: Go to Tarienth in the DMZ and restore Federation rule of law. Clear the Federation of any wrongdoing.
  3. **Acbar in the Dark**: Go to Acbar and investigate why the planet and the entire system have gone completely dark under sudden quarantine.

- **Races**: All playable races except the Lepidonians (the primary antagonists). If you play as an Ovex, you must disguise yourself as either a Human, Sayor, or Kurgian.
- **Classes**: All classes are available to play.
- **Alignment**: Characters serve in the Federation naval hierarchy, so **Chaotic alignments are prohibited**. If you play an evil character, you *must* be **Lawful Evil**. All other alignments are welcome.
- **Starting Character Level**: All Characters start at **Level 5**!
  - *Special Note*: Players are encouraged to try being a Cyborg, Mutant, or Psion (social penalties are waived for this elite task force).
  - *Bonus Feat*: All Characters receive one free Feat from the **Spaceship Operations** subcategory!
  - *Secondary Characters*: Players may create a second character to explore different roles or tactical duties.

- **Available Bridge & Ship Positions**:
  - **Ship’s Captain** *(Must have a Lawful alignment)*
  - **Second in Command (Commander / Captain's #1)**
  - **Chief Science Officer** *(Any scientific field of study)*
  - **Chief Medical Officer**
  - **Chief Engineer**
  - **Chief Tactical / Weapons Officer**
  - **Ranking Helmsman**
  - **Sensors and Comms Operator**
  - **Lt. Commander of Space Marine Squad Alpha** *(Default: "Hell's Hammer")*
  - **Lt. Commander of Space Marine Squad Beta** *(Default: "Void-Walkers")*
  *(Unassigned positions are handled by competent NPCs whom players may temporarily direct or control in encounters).*

- **Optional Character Traits**:
  1. *Old Comrade*: Longtime close friend and confidant of the Captain.
  2. *Black Mark*: Troubled or controversial service record with the Federation; this mission is redemption or ruin.
  3. *Pulled from Retirement*: Veteran recalled to active service due to specialized border experience.
  4. *Lepidonian Scarred*: Survivor scarred by past trauma or atrocities involving the Lepidonian Empire.
  5. *Diplomatic Conscience*: Sworn pacifist assigned to the crew for sensitive political or diplomatic oversight.
  6. *Ties in the DMZ*: Romantically involved with or closely related to someone recently reported missing in the Demilitarized Zone (near Tarienth or Acbar).

## GM Campaign Notes: The Triple Crisis

1. **Trouble in Our Own Backyard**:
   The planet **Sawn Prime**, capital of the Sayor peoples and founding member of the Federation, is in open chaos. The ‘Honorable’ Judge Carthic stands accused of treason and treasonous collusion with Lepidonian syndicates after evidence uncovered on Acbar exposed his corruption. Disillusioned human refugees and enraged Sayor citizens are rioting in the capital city of Swan.
2. **The Shadow of The Iron Mandible**:
   A ruthless proxy mercenary outfit known as **The Iron Mandible**, operating on Lepidonian coin, has seized installations across the DMZ surrounding **Tarienth**. Tarienth is in open revolt, and the Lepidonians accuse the Federation of sabotaging joint terraforming obligations.
3. **The Silence of Acbar**:
   The neutral trade world **Acbar** has gone dark. Following the devastating rampage of the rogue mercenary outfit "The Claws", Grayling authorities declared a total planetary quarantine and ordered all outbound shipping impounded, yet they have revealed no explanation. The Federation needs the Grayling Ambassador on Acbar rescued or contacted before the inspection Envoy rules against the Federation.
