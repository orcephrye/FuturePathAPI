The Sayor are one of the two major species within the Federation. The
Federation is a young coalition of species that borders the Lipdonians
and Cryous. It was formed as Sayor and Kurgian rebels took advantage of
a devastating war between Lipdonians and Cryous. The Sayor were once
occupied by the Lepidonains, but since the Federation formed, they have
been free of their oppressors. The Sayor are a troubled species. Their
relationship with the major species in the galaxy has made them
distrustful and wary of most of them.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html} Sayor\'s skin tone is
light blue. Some in the species will have white spots of different
shapes and sizes. All the eyes of Sayor are different shades of grey.
Their hair grows slowly and is short for both males and females, and is
white. However, because it is extremely easy to die, many Sayor have
wildly different hair colors.

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html} Sayor are deep friends and allies
of the Kurgian as they are both the leading and founding members of the
new Federation and both suffered under the thumb of Cryous and
Lepidonains. They have a shaky relationship with Humans, as they do
share a border with them. Many humans left their poisoned world to seek
new life, and most of the first stop and often stay in Sayor space. They
hate the Lipdonians and have only a slightly better relationship with
the Cryous.
