This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons, go
[here](Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.

A Holster is used to hold small firearms on a person. It acts as a
single Quick Access Slot that can hold a Small, Single-Handed weapon.
This allows the weapon to be stored on the person in an easily
accessible way, enabling them to \'Draw\' the weapon as a Simple Move
Action rather than a Standard Move Action, which would require pulling
it from a bag or case.

Benefit
: 1 Quick Access Slot that can hold a Small, Single-Handed weapon. Can
  only have two of these on a Character.
