With years of experience and special training, you are able to lead a
bridge crew and assist other players through your experience and
leadership skills.

**Prerequisites**: [Leadership](Leadership "Leadership"){.wikilink}

**Benefits**: Allows the Captain to use [Advanced Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Advanced Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Normal**: Normally the Captain would only be able to use [Standard
Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Standard Officer Functions"){.wikilink}

**Special**: -
