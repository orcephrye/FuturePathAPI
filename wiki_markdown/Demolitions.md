The professional setting and disarming of explosives. Often used to
achieve an extremely specific outcome. NOTE: To determine the Skill
Ranks of the Demolition Skill, the Player must pick 2 of the following 3
skills \[**Craft Chemical**, **Knowledge Science**, **Disable Device**\]
and use their Rank values as the Demolition Rank. This total is not
limited to the normal restrictions put on Skill Rank levels. This skill
can be either associated with Wisdom **-OR-** Intelligence. When the
Skill is acquired, the Player must choose. The Player cannot spend skill
points on this skill. To improve its Rank, the Player must spend points
on the chosen skills that make up Demolitions.

## Check

Setting a simple explosive to detonate at a specific spot doesn't
require a check, but connecting and setting a detonator does. Also,
placing an explosive for maximum effect against a structure calls for a
check, as does disarming an explosive device. Without this skill,
Characters use their Craft Chemical to make and set explosives.
Demolition skill demonstrates professional-level expertise, allowing a
Character to perform tasks involving explosives of greater difficulty.

### Craft Explosive {#craft_explosive}

This allows the Character to craft Explosives and their individual parts
found [here](Weapons#Explosives_(Grenades/Traps) "here"){.wikilink}.

### Place Explosive Device {#place_explosive_device}

Carefully placing an explosive against a fixed structure (a stationary,
unattended inanimate object) can maximize the damage dealt by exploiting
vulnerabilities in the structure's construction.

If the Explosive is meant to damage a structure or massive object,
normally a [Knowedge
Science](Knowledge#Science "Knowedge Science"){.wikilink} check would be
used to determine the optimal location. This can now be done with
Demolitions. The GM should set the DC to determine not only success but
also if extra damage is dealt or 1/2 damage is dealt.

### Disarm Explosive Device {#disarm_explosive_device}

Normally, this requires a [Disable
Device](Disable_Device "Disable Device"){.wikilink} check. But if
Demolitions is higher, the Character can utilize their Demolitions skill
to disarm explosives or disable a trap. When using Demolitions, the
Character cannot fail a critical check.

## Try Again {#try_again}

A character can take 10 when using the Demolitions skill, but can't take
20. Setting a detonator is usually a full-round action. Placing an
explosive device takes 1 minute or more, depending on the scope of the
job.

## Special

- **IMPORTANT**: The Rank of this skill is the Rank of Craft Chemical
  and Knowledge Science combined and doesn\'t follow the normal Skill
  Rank level restrictions.
- **IMPORTANT**: This skill can be either associated with Wisdom **OR**
  Intelligence. When the Skill is acquired, the Player must choose.
- **IMPORTANT**: The Player cannot spend skill points on this skill. To
  improve its Rank the Player must spend points on Chemical or Science.
- A character with the Cautious feat and at least 1 rank in this skill
  gets a +2 bonus on all Demolitions checks.
