## Campaign Background {#campaign_background}

The spiral galaxy of the Milky Way has 4 stellar arms. Between two of
these arms, the Perseus and Carina-Sagittarius, there is a smaller arm
called Orion-Cygnus. Near the middle of Orion is a system called Sol
with a Planet called Earth. Many years before the current events, a
Robotic Species called the Jove discovered humans and declared this
sector of space the Jovian Advancing People\'s Exclusion Zone, or
A.P.E.Z. This was between two growing empires. The Lepidonains and the
Cryous. Unable to expand in that direction, these great nations
eventually came head-to-head in a terrible conflict. Both empires had
subjected different species, and two of those species rebelled together
during a time of weakness and formed a Federation. The Peoples of Sayor
and the Kurgian Republic declared themselves a Federation and
independent. The Cryous relented and recognized this new nation. But the
Lepidonains didn't. However, in an embarrassing defeat, the Lepidonains
and Federation met under a flag of truce and hammered out a new Treaty
of Peace almost 100 years ago. A neutral party, the Graylings, who were
interested in the region for economic reasons, helped arbitrate this
peace.

However, not long after, humanity proved itself capable of Spaceflight.
Not faster than light, but still able to reach speeds between 5% and 10%
of the speed of light. The Jove declared that humanity\'s protections
were reduced and that other Alien species were allowed to interact. The
APEZ still existed, but it was now 'gifted' to Humanity by the Jove to
do with as they pleased. Both the Cryous and Lepidonan empires looked
eagerly at a sphere with a diameter of over 250 light-years, a
resource-rich space sitting between their borders. But they couldn't
simply declare it theirs. The Jove still protected Humanity in this.
Humanity would have to be 'willing' to give it to them. Who would
Humanity side with? Lepidonan? Cryous? Or even the young Federation?

Before this could be answered, humanity blew itself up. Waged a great
war, caused in part by the shock of alien species and their technology.
Without a unified government to speak for humanity, the Jove would not
recognize any treaty, and thus Cryous and Lepidonan could not acquire
what they most desired. Was this on purpose? Did the ember of war get
stoked into a fire by a foreign wind, or was it really just Humans being
human, as the rest of the universe saw it?

## Modern Events {#modern_events}

It has been 70 years since Humanity\'s WW3/System War 1 (SW1) and nearly
100 years since the signing of the Lepidonian peace treaty, which
recognized the Federation. Chaos, confusion, and accusations have spread
across the Federation and Lepidonan! It seems that some mercenary group
called 'The Claw' was possibly behind a lot of recent events. Who paid
these people? In desperation, the Federation has sent its new
state-of-the-art Tech 3 warship, the \<insert player\'s desired name
here\>, on orders ahead of the scheduled shake-down cruise. Its mission
is to bring order to the border. You are the best of the best the
Federation has to offer on their new flagship.

## From the Eyes of the Federation {#from_the_eyes_of_the_federation}

The Lepidonians are up to no good. They claim the Federation is
violating a nearly 100-year-old treaty. If true, the Graylings who had
arbitrated the Treaty would nullify it and refuse aid if the Lepidonians
decided to attack. The Lepidonians claim the Federation has skirted its
commitment to terraforming Terienth, manipulated the Humans into
possibly joining the Federation, and deliberately disrupted the neutral
trade planet of Acbar. They have requested a Grayling Envoy to observe
the situation as they gather evidence for their case. The situation on
the Federation border is chaotic. If the Grayling Envoy sees this, they
may be inclined to believe the Lepedonion lies.

## Mission Summaries {#mission_summaries}

- **Missions:** The general mission is to bring order to this sector of
  space and stop a war from happening. Your team will undertake 3 major
  missions:

:# **Escort the Judge:**(Must be done first) This mission involves
traveling to Sawn Prime to pick up Judge Carthic and take him to the
Federation Capital, which is temporarily on Kurgian Prime. There he will
stand trial, and hopefully justice will help calm the citizens of Sawn.

:# **Control of Tarienth:** It is a race for time. A Grayling Envoy,
escorted by a Jovian ship, is speeding toward Tarienth, both to
determine the truth behind these claims and because a science team will
divert to Acbar. This Envoy from the Graylings is new and unknown to the
Federation. The Federation officially cares about restoring order and
resolving conflict, but behind the scenes, government officials make it
clear that the only priority is keeping the Graylings from nullifying
the treaty. The Federation military is worried about reports of
terrorists in the DMZ. And it seems that some politicians, listening to
their constituents, want answers to the supposed worker abuse that is
happening.

:# **Acbar in the Dark:** Determine the cause of the planet-wide
quarantine and assist if necessary. The Federation\'s official statement
is that it is sending a science vessel to assess the situation. The real
reason is deeper. Many politicians want the Grayling Ambassador to the
Federation freed so they can visit Tarienth. She should arbitrate this
dispute, and many politicians at home would feel better if she did.

## Campaign Current Events {#campaign_current_events}

- The planet of Sawn Prime, the capital of the Sayor peoples and one of
  the founding members of the young Federation, is in chaos. The
  'Honorable' Judge Carthic has been accused of treason and collusion
  with the Lipedoneans. Human refugees on the planet, along with many of
  the native populace, have been rioting in the capital city, Swan.
- A paramilitary Lipedonean terror organization known as Dark Claw,
  which swears it acts to propel the Lipedonean Empire into the future,
  has been seen moving into the DMZ near the planet of Tarienth.
- Tarienth is in open revolt, and the Lipedoneans claim the Federation
  has failed to uphold its responsibilities in the joint terraforming
  project. A special Grayling Envoy was supposed to come from the
  neutral planet of Acbar to help determine the truth of these claims.
- The planet Acbar has gone dark. An official statement from the
  Grayling authorities has requested a full quarantine and the holding
  of any ship that left Acbar until further notice. Yet they have
  revealed no information and appear to have made no progress in
  understanding why the planet has suddenly stopped responding.
