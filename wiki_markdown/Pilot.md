You can use the Pilot skill to fly in 3 dimensions or any type of
steering in 2 dimensions. This can be used for both driving a
[vehicle](Vehicles "vehicle"){.wikilink} on planet side or flying a
[space craft](Space_Ships "space craft"){.wikilink} or plane. For more
explanations of traveling, see [here](Transportation "here"){.wikilink}.

## Check

Typical piloting tasks don't require checks. Checks are required during
combat, for special maneuvers, in other extreme circumstances, or when
the pilot wants to attempt something outside the vehicle\'s normal
parameters. When flying, the character can attempt simple maneuvers and
stunts (actions in which the pilot performs something complex very
quickly or in limited space).

Each vehicle's description includes a maneuver modifier that applies to
Pilot checks made by the operator of the vehicle.

### Space Ships Checks {#space_ships_checks}

During combat, the Pilot of the ship makes a Pilot check at the start of
each round. All active Pilots perform this check at the start of the
round, simultaneously. Each Pilot declares what type of action they wish
to do. Examples include Feint, Defensive, Offensive, and Flee. Your
action is successful towards whatever ship\'s rolled lower.

Learn more about Space Ship combat in the [Space
Combat](Combat#Space_Combat "Space Combat"){.wikilink} section.

Other Pilot checks are necessary when piloting through extreme
circumstances. Such as a gravity wave, a plasma storm, or some other
space-related phenomenon. Failure could result in damage to the ship
and/or injury to the crew.

To chart a course is a different Skill check, however. That uses the
[Survival](Survival "Survival"){.wikilink} skill check.

### Ground Transport Vehicles Checks {#ground_transport_vehicles_checks}

Routine tasks, such as ordinary driving, don't require a skill check.

This acts just like Space Ship Pilot checks, and the same 4 actions are
available. Feint, Defensive, Offensive, Flee.

Any other checks are only necessary when some unusual circumstance
exists (such as inclement weather or an icy surface), or when the
character is driving during a dramatic situation (the character is being
chased or attacked, for example, or is trying to reach a destination in
a limited amount of time). When driving, the character can attempt
simple maneuvers or stunts.

## Try Again {#try_again}

No. You cannot attempt this again until the next round. A character can
**not** take 10 or 20 when making a Pilot check.

## Time

A Pilot check is always a Full Round action.

## Special

- Feats:

1.  The [Spaceship Helm
    Officer](Spaceship_Helm_Officer "Spaceship Helm Officer"){.wikilink}
    Feat allows a Character to use their Advantage Die when rolling for
    the Pilot check.
2.  The [Guide](Guide "Guide"){.wikilink} Feat provides a Misc +1 bonus
    to both the Pilot and Survival skills.
