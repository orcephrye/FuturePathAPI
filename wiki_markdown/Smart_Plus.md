Advancements in science coupled with your drive to excel enable you to
progress faster toward becoming a truly intelligent hero.

**Prerequisites**: Your Character must have an Affinity with the
Intelligence ability this Affinity can be gained through an Advance
Class meaning that the character doesn\'t have to have the Intelligence
Hero Base Class.

**Benefit**: You gain two talents from the Smart hero's talent trees.

**Special**: You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you
select this feat, you must choose a different talent.
