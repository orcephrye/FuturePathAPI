Man the guns! Fire and will!

## Description

This is available to any crew member who has access to a console and
chooses to act in the **Action Phase** of SpaceShip combat. With the
exception of the Weapon\'s Officer.

This allows the Player to roll an accuracy check against the target\'s
Tracking DC. This is only possible if the Ship Sensors can detect the
ship or the Sensor Officer has already successfully scanned it. This
check by default uses the Character\'s Dexterity Modifier as a bonus.
However, if the Character has taken any of the **Space Ship Operations**
[Feats](Feats "Feats"){.wikilink}, then they can choose to use their
Intelligence or Wisdom Modifiers instead.

If successful, then once the Battle Pool has been rolled by the
Weapon\'s Officer but **before** re-rolls, the assisting Player can
choose one d10 from the pool that has failed and either add their
Character\'s chosen Ability Modifier (ie: Dex/Int/Wis) to turn it into a
successful hit, or if that isn\'t enough, they can try to re-roll the
d10 to see if it succeeds, adding their modifier on top of any the die
already benefits from.

If the Die becomes a success because of the Weapons Assist function, it
cannot be \'Exploded\' or the target of a re-roll.
