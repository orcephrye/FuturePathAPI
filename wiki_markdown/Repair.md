## Description

The DC is based on the Damage that was given via a 2d6 roll noted in
[Battle Damage
Chart](Battle_Damage_Chart "Battle Damage Chart"){.wikilink}. This is a
Craft check for a Craft Structure, with bonuses from the Ship\'s Repair
check.

Damage](Giving_and_Receiving_Damage_in_Space_Ships#Repairing_Damage "Repairing Damage"){.wikilink}
