The Galatic Guide is a collection of knowledge from different peoples
who explored The Great Ring navigating around the dangers of the galaxy.
This can be considered a **Besteriary** or **Monster Manual** of sorts.
However, it also has knowledge about all sorts of peoples, culters,
organizations that are capable of doing violence.
