#### This is a helpful guide for new players of FuturePath and TTRPGs. {#this_is_a_helpful_guide_for_new_players_of_futurepath_and_ttrpgs.}

Below is a list of actions organized by Abilities and Skills that a
Character can take in different situations.

What is an Ability? Abilities are divided up into 6 themes, and each
represents the aptitude the Character has for each specific theme. This
level of aptitude is based on its score. The themes are:

- Strength (Str) = Power, Muscle, Capacity
- Dexterity (Dex) = Quickness, Finesse, Subtlety
- Constitution (Con) = Vitality, Durability, Toughness
- Intelligence (Int) = Comprehension, Brilliance, Industrious
- Wisdom (Wis) = Intuition, Focus, Awareness
- Charisma (Cha) = Glamour, Dominating, Presence

If the GM asks to perform an Ability check, then roll a d20 and add the
Ability Score found at the top left of your Character sheet. If the
Ability is a favored ability, then also add the Character\'s advantage
die to the total.

Most actions fit under \'Skills\' however. What are Skills? Skills are
actions that Characters can do in the game that require confirmation, as
well as having defined outcomes. While a generic action can be loosely
associated with an Ability, a Skill is a more specific action. IE:
Talking nicely to an NPC (Charisma check) vs attempting to convince the
NPC of something (Diplomacy check). Below are the Skills and a quick
explanation of their theme.
