Advancements in science coupled with your drive to excel enable you to
progress faster toward becoming a truly strong hero.

**Prerequisites**: Your Character must have an Affinity with the
Strength ability this Affinity can be gained through an Advance Class
meaning that the character doesn\'t have to have the Strength Hero Base
Class.

**Benefit**: You gain two talents from the Strong hero's talent trees.

**Special**: You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you
select this feat, you must choose a different talent.
