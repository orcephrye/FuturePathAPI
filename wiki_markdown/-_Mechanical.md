This skill allows a character to build mechanical devices from scratch,
including engines and engine parts, weapons, and other gadgets.

If the Player wants to build a mechanical device from scratch, the
Player describes the kind of device he or she wants to construct; then
the GM decides if the Player can even craft the item based on there Tech
Level and then what the DC is, usually 10 or 15 + Procure Diff.
Otherwise, there are plenty of useful items in the game that require the
Mechanical crafting skill to make and/or repair.

The main categories from the [General
Equipment](General_Equipment "General Equipment"){.wikilink} page that
require Mechanical are:

- [Profession
  Kits](General_Equipment#Profession_Kits "Profession Kits"){.wikilink}
- [Survival
  Gear](General_Equipment#Survival_Gear "Survival Gear"){.wikilink}
- [Miscellaneous
  Equipment](General_Equipment#Miscellaneous_Equipment "Miscellaneous Equipment"){.wikilink}
- [Weapons](Weapons "Weapons"){.wikilink} (By default however, some
  weapons may require additional skills)
- Space Ships and some Space Ship Accessories

Identify
: If a Character wants to attempt to identify the Mechanical item, then
  they can follow the rules for identifying technology with their Tech
  rating.

Either check takes only 1 min. If possible, a Take 10 or Take 20 is an
automatic success.

A Mechanical Tool Kit from the [Profession
Kits](General_Equipment#Profession_Kits "Profession Kits"){.wikilink}
page is necessary to craft Mechanical items. An advanced kit provides an
Advantage when crafting or repairing.

Mechanical follows the core rules for Crafting with no modifications
except for Identifying as noted above. More rules on crafting, costs,
and time can be found [here](Craft "here"){.wikilink}.
