A Grenade Pouch or bandolier is used to store explosive devices for
quick deployment. It provides Quick Access Slots for grenades or other
small explosives, allowing them to be retrieved and ready for throwing
as a Simple Move Action.

Benefit
: 2 Quick Access Slots for Grenades or small explosives.
