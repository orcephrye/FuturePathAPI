**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefits**: The Character doesn\'t take a disadvantage when using
exotic weapons (both melee and ranged). This is similar to the Archaic
Weapons Feat. Some weapons are just down right weird. So new and
different that they are not intuitive to even the practiced gun smith.
Taking this Feat means your Character has spent time getting use to
different kinds of weird and uncommon weapon configurations.

**Normal**: Normally a Character would take a disadvantage when using
exotic weapons.
