**Prerequisite**: [Medium Firearms
Training](Medium_Firearms_Training "Medium Firearms Training"){.wikilink}.
If you pick this, you **cannot** also have [Heavy Contact Weapons
Training](Heavy_Contact_Weapons_Training "Heavy Contact Weapons Training"){.wikilink}

**Benefit**: Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 6 and higher for only
1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Ranged Weapon Branch.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 6 or
higher in the Ranged Weapons Proficiency Tree.
