**Prerequisites**: Spaceship Operations, Spaceship Weapons Officer

**Benefits**: A Spaceship have 4 different main types of weapons.
Projectile, Laser, Plasma, Missile. When a Player picks this Feat they
can pick a type and gain a +2 to accuracy for that type of Weapon.

**Normal**: -

**Special**: This can be picked up to 4 times each time the Player can
pick a different type.
