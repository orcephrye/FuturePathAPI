This is a [Campaign](Campaigns "Campaign"){.wikilink} for level 5
Characters/Heroes. It is meant to take Player Characters from level 5 to
level 10. This follows the events of the [The
Claws](The_Claws "The Claws"){.wikilink} campaign; however, it expects
different characters and doesn\'t require the Players to know about the
previous campaign.

  Federation of Free Peoples. Onboard the Federation Star Ship (FSS)
