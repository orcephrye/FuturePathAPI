This is a Power Armor Mod. Please review the [Power
Armor](Armor#Power_Armor "Power Armor"){.wikilink} rules for more
information.

Makes the Wearer immune to Environmental conditions. They do not need to
make checks when dealing with Zero G. Or when dealing with Extreme Heat,
Cold, Radiation, Chemical Hazards and so on. They are also not slowed
down by uneven terrain.
