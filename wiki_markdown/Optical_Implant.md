This page is associated with the [Cybernetics
Enhancement](Cybernetics#Enhancements "Cybernetics Enhancement"){.wikilink}
rules and covers special Optical Implant rules.

Any Implant that has the word Optic or Optical in its title is
considered an Optical implant. All-Optical implants can be \'upgraded\'
to include other implants. You can do this by spending a Masterworked
point on the initial Implant. The new implant is installed without
costing a Slot but does cost +1 power usage unless 2 Masterwork points
are spent and then the implant is installed without any additional power
or slot usage.

The number of Optical upgrades is limited only by the number of
Masterwork points available.

Once an Upgrade happens the Optical implant can no longer be stealth and
protrudes in an unnatural way from the recipient\'s eye socket.

These rules are adjusted for the Tech 4 **Universal Modular Optics**
implant.
