**Prerequisite**: Requires at least 5 levels in Armor Proficiency.

**Benefit**: Unlocks the Power Armor Proficiency for further
advancement. Also, immediately gain a point in this tree.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character cannot train in their Power Armor
Proficiency Tree. This Feat allows a Character access to the use of
Power Armor/Mechs.
