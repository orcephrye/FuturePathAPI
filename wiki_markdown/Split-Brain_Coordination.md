**Prerequisites**: A Class that supports the use of Techniques and the
Feat [Technique Novice](TechniqueNovice "Technique Novice"){.wikilink}.

**Benefits**: When using Techniques that are lower than the max level
techniques the Class/Character is currently able to perform, the
Character gains a Bonus Attack Action, if they have an off-handed weapon
or other qualified weapon option ready.

**Normal**: Techniques normally do not grant Bonus Actions.
