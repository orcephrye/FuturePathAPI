**Prerequisite**: [Medium Contact Weapons
Training](Medium_Contact_Weapons_Training "Medium Contact Weapons Training"){.wikilink}.
If you pick this, you **cannot** also have [Heavy Firearms
Training](Heavy_Firearms_Training "Heavy Firearms Training"){.wikilink}.

**Benefit**: Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 6 and higher for only
1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Melee Weapon Branch.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 6 or
higher in the Melee Weapons Proficiency Tree
