**Prerequisite**: -

**Benefit**: The Character can ignore the no advantage penalty for using
their Off-Handed weapon. They do not simply get an advantage because the
weapon is in their off-hand. Also, this only counts for valid off-hand
weapons. IE: Any weapon that is not considered \'heavy\', \'clunky\',
\'two-handed\' cannot qualify. **Also**, they can target a different
opponent if they wish.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character can use a second weapon in their
off-hand, but they gain no advantages for using it. There are additional
penalties depending on whether the weapon is ranged or melee. However,
this rule applies only to the no advantage penalty. Also, normally, a
Bonus Attack Action must target the same opponent as the Action that
earned the Bonus Attack. Usually, the target of the Standard Attack
Action.
