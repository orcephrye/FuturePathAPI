Campaign Main Page. Nothing here yet.

I have run several test games following a campaign I have noted in
Google Docs. I will eventually combine all of this into a starter
Campaign that takes players from level 1 to 5.

It starts with all the characters in prison together, all with amnesia,
and all told they just arrived and are part of a notorious mercenary
group. The prison is also a labor camp that disassembles old spaceships
and other equipment and stores the parts in a junkyard. Thus their ship
is sent there to be scaped as well. The prison is also in an asteroid
belt, on a massive asteroid with artificial gravity.

The players have to escape, then track down who they really are, how
they were imprisoned, and, finally, why. They learn quickly that a human
woman is frozen in statis in their ship. They learn the name of the
judge who imprisoned them, and about the last couple of jobs they did.

The players can choose which mystery and which information to
investigate first as they jump around space, trying to clear their name,
get revenge, or both.
