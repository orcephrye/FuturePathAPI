This is a Power Armor Mod. Please review the [Power
Armor](Armor#Power_Armor "Power Armor"){.wikilink} rules for more
information.

Allows the Wearer to preform on the fly repairs to objects even during
combat. Including on the hull of a ship during combat. The Suit acts as
a Electrical, Mechanical, Demolitions Tool Kit. With 50 uses that can be
recharged. If they can already preform a repair on the fly or as a
Simple or Free action then they can now preform two repairs in the same
turn as long as it doesn\'t require moving.
