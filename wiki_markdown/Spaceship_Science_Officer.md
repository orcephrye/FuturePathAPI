**Prerequisites**: [Study](Study "Study"){.wikilink} 2 Ranks

**Benefits**: You can add your affinity die to rolls for multiple
science-related skills while on board your Spaceship. The list of skills
is as follows: Computer Use, Decipher Script, Disable Device, Knowledge
Science, and Study. They can also use [Advanced Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Advanced Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character cannot add their affinity die to any
skill check rolls, regardless of whether they are on a ship. You can
only use [Standard Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Standard Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Special**: -
