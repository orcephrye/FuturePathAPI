This is a Power Armor Mod. Please review the [Power
Armor](Armor#Power_Armor "Power Armor"){.wikilink} rules for more
information.

Allows the Wearer to do on-the-fly (as a simple action) Medical skill
checks even during combat. The Suit acts as a First Aid, fast-use
Medkit, Surgery, and Pharmacist Kit. With 50 uses and rechargeable. If
they can already perform a Medical Skill check as a Simple or Free
action, they can now perform two Medical checks in the same turn, as
long as they don\'t require moving.
