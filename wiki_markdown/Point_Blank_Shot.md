**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefit**: The Character takes no disadvantage penalties for aiming at
targets within 10ft and gains a +1 to all damage dice for the weapon if
the target is within 10ft. Also, the Character does not cause the target
to gain a Tactical Opportunity against them for close-quarters use of a
Ranged weapon.

**Normal**: Firing a Ranged weapon at a Targe within 10ft comes with a
disadvantage. Also, if the target is able to hit the Character with a
melee weapon, that target gains a Tactical Opportunity against them.
