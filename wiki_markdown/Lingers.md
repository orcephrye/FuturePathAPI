Lingers

- This means that the effect will stay within the range specified by the
  [Charge](Weapons#The_Charge "Charge"){.wikilink} for a certain amount
  of time. The time is generally specified after the word \'Lingers\' in
  parenthesis. IE: (1d4 rnds). This assumes that they are in combat.
  Each around is 3 seconds if the explosive is used outside of combat
  time.
