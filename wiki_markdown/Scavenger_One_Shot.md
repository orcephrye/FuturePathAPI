## Scavenger One-Shot: Derelict Delve on Aethelgard-7 {#scavenger_one_shot_derelict_delve_on_aethelgard_7}

This is a tactical and exploration-based one-shot adventure for a crew
of Level 1 or 2 characters. It focuses on Ground Combat and various
aspects of dungeon crawling, e.g., dealing with traps, figuring out how
to proceed through difficult spaces, etc.

### Adventure Premise {#adventure_premise}

The players are a crew of contract scavengers specializing in \"Station
Stripping.\" The Players do not play as the Captain. A high-paying
contract (100,000 ISC) is offered by **Dr. Valen Vance**, an executive
from the Grayling biotechnology corporation **Xeno-Synth Systems**. The
mission is to board **Aethelgard-7**, a decommissioned communications
and monitoring outpost at the system\'s edge. The players may keep and
sell all salvaged metal and equipment, but they must retrieve specific
\"cryo-containers\" from the cargo bay and download all research data
from the mainframe.

In truth, Aethelgard-7 was purchased by the Xeno-Synth Corp to operate a
secret laboratory researching a forbidden bio-mechanical nanite
pathogen: the **Cybertaker Strain**. The pathogen escaped, assimilating
the staff and even the machines. Xeno-Synth Systems abandoned the
station to cover up their illegal research. Now, they want their asset
recovered using the expendable scavengers.

### Key Characters {#key_characters}

1.  **Dr. Valen Vance (Grayling Corp Rep):** Cold, demanding, and
    communicates only via encrypted hologram. He warns the crew not to
    inspect the research files and to use a special data drive that can
    plug into the system to extract the data. \"\...Because you would be
    unable to otherwise.\"
2.  **Mother-7 (Corrupted Station AI):** The station\'s central
    computer. Infected by the nanites, she oscillates between hostile
    defense subroutines and fragmented, desperate warnings to the crew
    to escape.
3.  **Dr. Sarah Lin (Assimilated Station Lead):** Vance\'s former head
    researcher, now fully merged with the reactor core and serving as
    the primary hive host.

## Opening Story Summary {#opening_story_summary}

*Read the following aloud to the players to start the adventure:*

> \"Space is vast, cold, and quiet---until the Captain\'s voice barks
> over the ship\'s comms. The Captain signed the contract before any of
> you could voice an objection. A whopping 100,000 ISC from **Xeno-Synth
> Systems** to salvage and secure **Aethelgard-7**, a decommissioned
> communications and monitoring outpost drifting at the very edge of the
> system.
>
> The official corporate brief says the station was decommissioned and
> abandoned. But the pay is far too high for a standard
> station-stripping run, and the corporate executive, **Dr. Valen
> Vance**, was far too eager to hand over a custom, encrypted data drive
> to extract the mainframe logs---warning you not to look at the files.
> He has given specific instructions to recover both the data and some
> \'supplies\' left behind when the station was abandoned.
>
> Through the viewport, **Aethelgard-7** looms---a dead, silent
> leviathan of steel drifting in silence. No beacons flash, no docking
> return signal, no lights shine through the small circular windows.
> Only a faint red glow from some radiator panels indicates that the
> station still lives.
>
> Your salvage vessel maneuvers close, aligning with the external
> docking ring. The thrusters roar one last time, followed by the heavy,
> shuddering clunk of the clamps locking onto the station\'s docking
> port. The airlock cycle begins, and your ship\'s cargo bay doors hiss
> open. Instantly, sirens blare; you feel air whooshing past you for a
> quick second before the emergency pressure doors slam shut with a
> violent clang. Your body flashes in discomfort at the quick change in
> pressure.
>
> You look through a window to survey the problem. Before you lies the
> station\'s docking bay---a narrow metal walkway hangs suspended over a
> cavernous, dark bay. Sparks shower from failing overhead conduits,
> casting erratic shadows across the rusted deck plates. On the opposite
> side of the walkway, the manual override console for the docking
> clamps blinks a weak amber warning light, indicating the dock is
> unsecured. Beyond it, the main hangar doors are jammed half-open,
> revealing the cold void of space.

## Rooms

### Room 1: The Docking Bay {#room_1_the_docking_bay}

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Read the following aloud to the players when they are ready for Room
1:*

> The intercom buzzes. The captain\'s voice is barely heard over the
> popping in your ears. \"The main hangar door is jammed! You grunts
> will have to seal this place, or the station-side airlock won\'t open.
> Use the pressure suits and get to those controls.\" Another buzz
> sounds from the intercom as the captain dismissively hangs up.

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Close the main hangar doors to complete the docking and
  allow access to the rest of the station.
- **The Situation:** The power is fluctuating, and the main hangar doors
  are jammed half-open. When the players try to manually override the
  docking clamps, the station\'s security grid misidentifies them as
  intruders and deploys a patrol of **[Corp Security
  Droids](Scavenger_One_Shot#Corp_Security_Droids "Corp Security Droids"){.wikilink}**.
- **Special System Mechanics:**

:\* **Vacuum and Bulky Suits:** Characters without vacuum-sealed armor
must wear **Emergency Pressure Suits** (AC +0, imposes **1Ad
(Disadvantage)** on Dexterity-based skill checks)
[Advantage](Advantage "Advantage"){.wikilink}.

:\* **Suit Puncture:** Taking a **Major Hit** punctures a suit. A
character must spend a Simple Action to apply a patch, or take 1d6
Thermal (Cold) damage at the start of each of their turns
[Conditions](Conditions "Conditions"){.wikilink}.

GM Notes
: Inform the players that in the airlock of their ship are pressure
  suits for all the players. As well as some basic gear and equipment
  that is available for any Character to pick up and add to their
  inventory. This should include a couple of Frag Grenades, a Medical
  Kit, and some Healing Concoction (1d8) Syringes 5 Pack.

<!-- -->

Details

: 

- **Perception Check** - If the Players make a
  [Perception](Perception "Perception"){.wikilink} check of the room
  while they cross the walkway:

:\* DC 11 - (GM Note: This can be freely provided through Passive
Perception if you desire) The player notices damage all over the place.
There are many cargo boxes clogging the hangar doors in the second half
of the cargo bay, and it appears that a ship ripped itself free of its
clamps.

:\* DC 15 - They determine from afar that the console looks damaged but
functioning, and that there may be blood on it. This can be seen without
a check when a player reaches it.

:\* DC 17 - The player notices a dead Grayling that is still clinging to
the metal structure of the station. The player also notices security
droids. They appear damaged, and many of them are piled up with boxes
that appear to be jamming the door; others are on the walkway on the
hangar\'s other side, slumped over and apparently off.

- **The Console:** - This console looks damaged but is still
  functioning. A player who approaches it will notice blood that has
  boiled dry on the console due to rapid decompression.

:\* Hacking the console requires a [Computer
Use](Computer_Use "Computer Use"){.wikilink} check (DC 12).

::\* **Success** causes the doors to reset. They first open all the way,
causing many of the containers to float out into space. Then the doors
close, forming a seal.

::\* **Failure** causes all the security droids to activate.

:\* Regardless of the result, the security droids activate, but if it is
a success, most of the droids are flushed out the airlock. Only two
damaged droids remain. On a failure, many of the droids try to escape
the pile-up at the hangar bay door, while some droids (half of the total
player count, rounded down) get out and attempt to fly up to the
walkway. The rest are pushed out of the hangar bay, and the bay doors
close. **Note**: If a player has a Passive Perception of 14, they can
automatically detect that the robots are activating. Otherwise, a player
must state that they are watching out for the bots, or the bots get a
surprise round.

*Read the following aloud to the players after combat is finished:*

> Another buzz sounds off, but to your surprise it isn\'t the captain\'s

### Room 2: Station Foyer {#room_2_station_foyer}

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Read the following aloud to the players when they enter Room 2:*

> \"As you step through the station\'s heavy inner airlock, the door
> seals behind you with a reassuring thump, and the atmospheric cyclers
> groan to life, filling the room with thin, freezing air. You find
> yourselves in the station\'s foyer. Flickering, dim red emergency
> lights cast long, nervous shadows across the room. Directly ahead, the
> primary corridor is blocked by a massive, reinforced blast door,
> locked down tight.
>
> The only way forward is a maintenance access corridor to the side, but
> it has become a death trap. A breached coolant pipe is venting thick,
> swirling clouds of freezing cryogenic nitrogen gas across the floor.
> To make matters worse, a severed overhead conduit has fallen into the
> breach, sending dangerous, blue arcs of electricity dancing across the
> metal grates. The path forward is blocked by ice and high voltage.\"

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Clear the side corridor of hazards to proceed deeper
  into the station.
- **The Situation:** The main blast doors are locked. The side corridor
  coolant loop is breached, venting freezing gas, and exposed wiring is
  electrifying the floor.

Details

: 

If the Players do not make a Perception check, simply inform them of the
general problem and remind them that
[Perception](Perception "Perception"){.wikilink} and
[Study](Study "Study"){.wikilink} checks may be necessary to discover
solutions. [Knowledge
Science](Knowledge#Science "Knowledge Science"){.wikilink} can also be
used in place of [Study](Study "Study"){.wikilink}. If a Player has both
[Study](Study "Study"){.wikilink} and [Knowledge
Science](Knowledge#Science "Knowledge Science"){.wikilink}, they can
roll for both and take the highest.

- **Perception Check** - If the Players inspect the room to find a way
  through the coolant loop:

:\* DC 11 - They notice the gas is venting from three distinct valves.
To shut it off mechanically, the valves must be turned simultaneously,
meaning they must coordinate their efforts.

:\* DC 15 - They notice the electrical conduit is sparking in a slow,
rhythmic 3-second cycle. Timing their movement grants Advantage on the
Athletics check to leap past it.

:\* DC 17 - They spot a secondary electrical junction panel hidden
behind a loose bulkhead plate. A character can attempt to cut the power
to the severed conduit from here, resolving the electrical hazard
entirely.

- **Study Check** - The Players can study the environment and the
  situation to try to discover new ways to deal with the situation. (A
  [Knowledge Science](Knowledge#Science "Knowledge Science"){.wikilink}
  check can also be used in place of [Study](Study "Study"){.wikilink}.)

:\* DC 12 - The players note that they can fix the valves so they are
easy to turn and that it will require a [Craft
Mechanical](-_Mechanical "Craft Mechanical"){.wikilink} check.

:\* DC 14 - If they haven\'t noticed with Perception, they will now
notice the electrical conduit is sparking in a slow, rhythmic 3-second
cycle. Timing their movement grants Advantage on the check to leap past
it.

:\* DC 18 - A Player thinks they have pinpointed the correct wiring to
disable the electricity in the corridor.

- **Note On Take Ten/Twenty**: Since the foyer itself is safe,
  characters can Take 10 or Take 20 on their Study checks to analyze the
  hazard before entering it. Doing so automatically succeeds on all
  Study check potential information. However, taking 20 minutes allows
  time to pass, which causes the station\'s reactor to warm up: the
  difficulty to cross the electricity increases by +4, and the damage
  from the coolant leak increases by 1 damage die. Please read the
  flavor text below if they Take 20, or if the players take too long at
  **Resolving the Hazards** (see below).

*Read the following aloud to the Players if they take too long*

> The station rumbles beneath your feet as if some great beast were
> awaking within. It stops, and only the sounds of electric sparks and
> the hissing of coolant can be heard once again. A buzz in your ear
> precedes the Captain\'s next announcement over the comms, \"Something
> just happened. One of the reactors just went extra hot! What are you
> guys doing in there? Get a move on!\" You notice that the electrical
> sounds are now at a different frequency, and the coolant is hissing
> even louder than before!

- **Resolving the Hazards:**

: To clear the coolant leak, the crew has a few options:
  - **Coordinated Shutoff (Valves):** Requires three players to turn the
    valves in sync. The valves appear jammed and even partially frozen
    over. The characters make a [Cooperative
    Check](SkillsBasics#Cooperative_Checks "Cooperative Check"){.wikilink}
    Strength DC 18 (requiring a majority of participating characters,
    i.e., 2 out of 3, to succeed). A failure with a NAT 1 on any check
    will cause that player to be
    [Fatigued](Conditions#Fatigue_Track "Fatigued"){.wikilink} and the
    Players must wait a minute before trying again. Each subsequent
    attempt grants **1Ad (Advantage)** to the check. On the 3rd try,
    they succeed no matter the result. However, time has passed, and the
    electricity trap has become fiercer. If the Players had to try all 3
    times, then read the text for Take Ten/Twenty.

  :\* **Valve Repair (DC 12 Study required):** The players realize that
  they can repair the valves with a [Craft
  Mechanical](-_Mechanical "Craft Mechanical"){.wikilink} DC 14 check.
  Each successful repair on a valve reduces the Strength check DC by 4.

  - **Brute Force Run:** This is extremely ill-advised. Players can try
    to run through the corridor. An
    [Athletics](Athletics "Athletics"){.wikilink} DC 25 is necessary to
    get through without the cold damaging the character. For every 4
    points by which the DC is missed, the character must roll a CON save
    (DC 15) or take 1d4 cold damage (up to a maximum of 3 times). When
    doing this, they take **1Ad (Disadvantage)** on passing through the
    electrical hazard and ignore any benefit provided by the Perception
    and Study checks.
: To deal with the electrical hazard:
  - **Leap Across:** A player can leap across the electrified grates
    with a DC 14 [Athletics](Athletics "Athletics"){.wikilink} or
    [Acrobatics](Acrobatics "Acrobatics"){.wikilink} check. Succeeding
    at the DC 15 Perception or DC 14 Study check grants **1Ad
    (Advantage)** on this roll. Failure causes 1d6 (E) damage.
  - **Junction Panel Disable (DC 17 Perception required):** A character
    can disable the power at the junction panel with a DC 15 [Sleight of
    Hand](Sleight_of_Hand "Sleight of Hand"){.wikilink} check, turning
    off the electrical arcs safely. A failure results in 1d3 (E) damage.
  - **Cutting the Wires (DC 18 Study required):** A character can simply
    cut the correct wiring to disable the electricity, resolving this
    trap. The GM can optionally require a DC 5 check (Sleight of Hand or
    Disable Device) to perform the cut safely.

### Room 2B: Central Hub {#room_2b_central_hub}

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Read the following aloud to the players when they enter Room 2B:*

> \"Already tired and shivering from the hazards, you scramble out of
> the maintenance corridor and stand in the station\'s Central Hub, your
> breath pluming in the cold.
>
> The hub is a circular chamber with four heavy blast doors lining the
> walls. Overhead, the main lights are dead, leaving the room
> illuminated only by the faint, amber glow of emergency backup
> terminals. The silent, empty station stretches out from here in every
> direction:
>
> The main corridor behind you is fully sealed---a thick steel blast
> door blocking your way back to the Foyer. The door to the Core Lab is
> locked down tight. Its access terminal is smashed and burnt out,
> leaving only a keycard slot blinking a slow, red warning light. The
> door to the Command Bridge is dark and cold, its control panel
> completely unpowered and dead. The Cargo Bay door is the only one
> showing signs of life. Its terminal glows with a steady green light
> and hums softly, unlocked and ready to open.
>
> Suddenly, without warning, a faint crackling laugh echoes in the
> chamber. The metallic, malicious sound echoes around this circular
> room, and you are all unable to determine its source. A cruel voice
> speaks; \"You should have listened to Mother\... Mother knows best.\"
> More laughter echoes and then silence.\"

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Determine the direction to take. This room has 4 doors.
  One leads back to the Foyer, which is unreachable. Then there is a
  door leading to the Core Lab, another leading to the Command Bridge,
  and lastly a door leading to the Cargo Bay.
- **Foyer Door:** It has already been determined that this way is
  completely blocked and cannot be, nor should it be, opened.
- **The Core Lab Door:** This door is completely locked down. The
  terminal used to access it has been destroyed, so only the keycard
  port appears to be working.
- **The Command Bridge:** This door is also locked down. The power to
  this door has been disabled. Its terminal is dead. The Players can try
  to force entry. The door is tough and cannot be blasted through
  easily. However, nothing is holding the door down. A Strength check DC
  25 could succeed at forcing the door open. A portable power pack could
  also provide power to the door. At that point, a Computer Use DC 12 is
  necessary to make the door recognize you and open.
- **The Cargo Bay:** Here, the bay door is both powered and unlocked.
  This should force the Characters to have to go through the Cargo Bay.

Details

: 

- **Perception Check:** A perception check reveals nothing new.
- **Study Check:**

:\* DC12 - The Players notice that a key card terminal for the Core Lab
Door still appears functional.

:\* DC18 - The Players realize that a portable power station could be
jerry-rigged to power the Command Bridge door.

- **Knowledge Mechanic or Electrical:**

:\* DC16 - The Players realize that a portable power station could be
jerry-rigged to power the Command Bridge door.

- **Computer Use:(Requires Study Check DC12)** This is to try to hack
  the Key Card Terminal. This is a DC25 Computer Use \[Expert\] check
  and should be nearly impossible for the players to succeed on.

:\* **NOTE:** Stating a check\'s competency level, i.e., Computer Use
\[Expert\] or Computer User \[Novice\], is a way to convey to the
Players just how hard an action is. You do not necessarily have to say
the DC value. Only that it would require an \'Expert\' to succeed at the
listed DC. The lower the competency level of a player\'s Computer Use
Skill, the higher the DC is for them. These players should be level 1 or
2. Meaning they are novice. So this adds to the DC, making this
virtually impossible for them. More on calculating the DC based on
competency level
[here](SkillsBasics#Skill_Competency_Levels "here"){.wikilink}.

- **Taking Ten or Twenty:** If the Players decide again to take ten or
  twenty, the GM should describe the same rumbling feeling and have the
  captain get on the comms to demand they hurry up in securing the
  station. However, nothing notable happens. If the GM hasn\'t used the
  text for Take 10/20 from the previous section, feel free to use it
  here instead.

### Room 3: Cargo Bay {#room_3_cargo_bay}

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Read the following aloud to the players when they enter Room 3:*

> \"As the door slides open and you step into the vast, silent cavern of
> the station\'s main Cargo Bay, the air hits you---stale and thick with
> dust. Massive cargo containers and stacks of metal crates tower around
> you in the darkness like rusted monoliths. Overhead, high walkways
> hang silently, casting long, stark shadows.
>
> Arriving at the coordinates listed on the manifest on your datapad,
> your eyes trace the rows of shipping containers until they lock onto a
> specific pile in the corner. Stamped with the blue-and-silver
> corporate seal of Xeno-Synth Systems are the cargo containers you were
> sent to retrieve. Nearby, a heap of discarded scrap metal and a
> deactivated maintenance droid chassis lies slumped in the dark; a
> cargo management terminal blinks a weak, amber status light in the
> gloom.\"

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Retrieve the client\'s containers from their listed
  manifest location.
- **The Situation:** The crew finds the containers stamped with
  corporate logo seals in the Cargo Bay.
- **The Twist:** The containers are empty decoys rigged with **Electric
  Arc Traps** and **Gas Grenades**. When triggered, the bay doors lock,
  two corp security droids and and two corrupted Grayling scientists
  rise from scrap piles to attack.
- **The Resolution:** Players can hack the local manifest terminal, DC
  10 Computer Use, to discover that the real biological research
  containers were moved to the Core Lab.

Details

: 

- **Perception Check:** If the Players choose to perform a Perception
  check

:\* DC11 - The Player notices a manifest terminal that is operational.
Passive Perception would also allow the player to notice this.

:\* DC15 - The Players notice a Trap set on the containers marked on
their manifest.

:\* DC17 - They notice two Corp Security Droids are actively hiding in
the pile of rubble.

:\* DC19 - They notice two corrupted Graylings hiding behind containers,
waiting to ambush the players. Upon discovery, they attack along with
the Corp Security Droids..

:\* DC20 - Before or after the battle, this reveals a Mobile Power
Station usually used for in-the-field repairs and work. This can be
jerry-rigged with Craft Electronics DC11, Knowledge Science DC12, or an
Intelligence check DC13 to successfully power the Command Bridge door.

- **Study or Perception Check on the Containers:**

:\* If the Players choose to specifically check the Containers, a DC15
Perception check or DC13 Study check will reveal the Trap on the
containers.

:\* DC13 Study check of the Console will show the Players the manifest,
and also reveal the controls for the Maintenance Droid.

- **Disable Device (Unlock if a Player discovers the trap):** A [Disable
  Device](Disable_Device "Disable Device"){.wikilink} check on the trap
  with a DC14 will safely remove the trap but also trigger the encounter
  if it hasn\'t already.
- **Triggering the Trap and/or Combat:**

*Read the following aloud to the players when combat is about to start*

> (If they trigger the trap) \"Suddenly, grenades fly into the air from
> the box and a bright blue flash erupts as a bolt of electricity shoots
> from the container.\"
>
> (If combat starts) \"Just then, out of the rubble pile shoot two Corp
> Security Droids; they look like their companions from the hangar bay.
> If that sight wasn\'t bad enough, an even more bizarre sight catches
> your eye: two Graylings in science uniforms pop out of containers in
> opposite corners of the hangar bay. They point pistols at you. You
> notice just then that their skin glistens in an unnatural way.\"

- **Trap Details**: The closest person to the container, or the one
  opening it, must make a Dex saving throw DC 14 or take 1d4 + 1 (E)
  damage. The gas grenades go off at the end of the Players\' Vanguard
  phase. The grenades have a diameter of 20ft and automatically cause
  all people within that range to roll a Conn Save DC 12 or take 1d4
  Conn damage. The gas dissipates by the end of the round.

Combat Details

: 

- **Note:** During combat, the first time a Player succeeds at damaging
  a Grayling, the GM should inform them that it appears that the damage
  dealt is slowly healing, that the glistening skin seems to move around
  and over the wound. A Knowledge Science or Tech check DC15 will tell
  the person that this is some sort of hybrid nano/bio technology. It
  should be obvious to the Players that the Grayling is regenerating
  lost Hit Points.
- **Round 1/Surpise Round:** During the first round for the enemies,
  whether it\'s a surprise round or a standard one, the Droids will use
  their round to get close to the players, while the Graylings will not
  move but will fire their laser pistols.

*At the beginning of Round 2, read the following to the players*

> Suddenly, a scratching of metal can be heard over the sounds of the
> battle. A rumbling as boxes and metal containers of scrap fall down
> off the rising behemoth of the Maintenance Droid. Its control chair
> and console glisten with the same strange substance that coats the
> Graylings\' skin. The Maintenance Droid is clearly booting up. It will
> take a few more seconds, but you get an odd feeling that this mindless
> machine seems eager to join the battle.

::\* **Note:** The Maintenance Droid is slow and will not enter combat
until the next round. GM Note: If the combat is not going well for the
enemy and the player is easily winning, have the Droid enter combat at
the end of the Rearguard phase of Round 2.

- **Overall Tactics:** The Corp Security Droids will try to do melee
  attacks with their knives while the Graylings will not move even if an
  enemy gets right on top of them. Once the Maintenance Droid enters
  combat, it only wants to smash the players. If it cannot do melee, it
  will pick up a chunk of debris and throw it at the party.
- **Perception Check During combat:** If the Players choose to check out
  their surroundings during combat.

:\* DC12 - They notice that one of the Graylings has a Key Access Card
on their lapel.

:\* DC15 - They notice that the maintenance console lights have changed,
likely because the Maintenance Droid is being controlled through the
console.

- **Computer Use:** In combat, if the Players choose to hack into the
  console, it is a Computer Use DC14 check. They can choose to disable
  the maintenance droid. By successfully rolling a Computer Use DC 18 or
  more, they can reprogram the Maintenance Droid to attack enemies, if
  they are still alive. If not in combat, it\'s DC 10, and they can

### Room 4: The Core Lab {#room_4_the_core_lab}

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Read the following aloud to the players when they enter Room 4:*

> \"The heavy blast door slides aside to reveal the station\'s Core Lab,
> but the sterile laboratory you expected is gone. Instead, you face a
> pulsing, bio-mechanical nightmare. Thick, black cables and metallic
> vines sprawl across the floor, walls, and ceiling like frozen roots,
> dripping with a glistening, iridescent fluid. The air is warm and
> smells faintly of ozone and burning synthetic flesh.
>
> At the center of the chamber, bathed in the blinding, erratic glow of
> the exposed fusion reactor core, stands a horrific silhouette. The
> upper body of a woman in a shredded research coat is fused directly
> into the main power junction. Wires and nanite-laced conduits weave
> through her skin, and her head hangs limp until you step inside. She
> snaps her head up, her eyes glowing with a cold, artificial light as a
> massive energy shield crackles to life around her. In the shadows of
> the machinery, several mutated, jerky figures---assimilated crew
> members---begin to step forward while the door slams shut behind you\"

![](Scavenger_One_Shot_Boss.jpg "Scavenger_One_Shot_Boss.jpg"){width="1408"
height="768"}

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Defeat the assimilated Dr. Sarah Lin and secure the
  true cryo-containers.
- **The Situation:** The Core Lab has been transformed into a
  bio-mechanical nest. Dr. Sarah Lin has been fused into the station\'s
  main power grid, drawing energy from the energy reactor.
- **The Encounter:** **The Cybertaker Host (Dr. Lin)** and a swarm of
  assimilated crew thralls attack. The Host sweeps the area with
  mechanical tentacles. If her Shield is disabled, she also uses a
  breath weapon.

Details

: 

- **Perception Check:** If the Players choose to perform a Perception
  check

:\* DC 12 - The player notices large shield emitters behind the mangled
body of Dr. Sarah Lin. These are plugged directly into the reactor and
are vulnerable to attack.

:\* DC 14 - The player notices a lapel security key badge hanging off
what is left of the Doctor\'s lab coat.

:\* DC 16 - The player notices that a control panel operated by one of
the corrupted people is active and displays information about the
reactor status. It is the reactor control computer!

- **Computer Use (Requires Perception DC 16):** A player can use
  [Computer Use](Computer_Use "Computer Use"){.wikilink} to access the
  terminal and start turning off the reactor. There are 3 reactors. Each
  reactor shutdown will disable one of the tentacles Dr. Lin controls
  and weaken the shield by removing 2 DR and 1 point of HP regeneration
  from Dr. Lin. After the final reactor is shut down, Dr. Lin\'s shield
  drops completely, all the mechanical tentacles fall still, and her
  regeneration stops.

Tactics

: 

The minions all have ranged laser pistols in one hand and a knife in the
other. If the players get close for melee, the minion will use the
knife. If not, they will use the pistol. They will target whoever is
closest without any tactics or care for the environment; they appear
mindless.

Dr. Sarah Lin will use her mechanical tentacles to do ranged touch
attacks. These act like a whip, dealing 1d4 - 1 (K) + 1d4 (E) damage.
The tentacles are weak and not able to do much, as they were not meant
for combat; their main purpose is to electrify the opponent. The Doctor
is a boss and thus gains special boss features. They can act in both
Vanguard and Rearguard phases. They can also choose to sacrifice the
round to act at the top of the round before anybody else. In each phase
of combat, Dr. Lin uses 1 of the 4 tentacles to attack. She will focus
first on anyone trying to access the console. Second, on anyone who has
succeeded at dealing her damage. And lastly, the person who has done the
most damage to one of her minions. If someone is at the console, at the
start of a round she can choose to act first to use all 4 tentacles to
grapple the target. For every tentacle lost, she takes disadvantage. If
it looks like she is going to be defeated (all her minions are dead, she
has lost her shield, and has less than 1/2 health while the Players are
better off), she will try to convince the players that assimilation is
better, while secretly enabling a self-destruct. A
[Perception](Perception "Perception"){.wikilink} or [Sense
Motive](Sense_Motive "Sense Motive"){.wikilink} check (DC 14) reveals
the deception.

**Note:** If the GM is so inclined to make combat more difficult, or to
account for when the players take too long (such as choosing to Take 10
or Take 20), they can add extra buffs to Dr. Sarah Lin, as if the
reactors powering up are enhancing her. Perhaps adding an extra tentacle
to destroy or another shield emitter that must go down before the shield
drops.

#### Shield Emitters {#shield_emitters}

These are Items and take Battle Scars under the [Item
Damage](Item_Damage "Item Damage"){.wikilink} rules. They have R2/H5/AC
10, and by default there are 2 of them. Remember that an Item can only
take 1 Battle Scar per Phase of combat. And that damage is accumulated
between players for that Phase.

#### Dr. Sarah Lin\'s Mechanical Tentacles {#dr._sarah_lins_mechanical_tentacles}

These are also Items and can take Battle Scars under the [Item
Damage](Item_Damage "Item Damage"){.wikilink} rules. They have R2/H5/AC
15, and by default there are 4 of them.

#### Dr. Sarah Lin Corrupted Human Scientist {#dr._sarah_lin_corrupted_human_scientist}

- **Appearance**: If you only saw her upper body, you would find her
  surprisingly beautiful in an almost artificial way. The glistening
  skin shines and reflects patterns from the light cast all around the
  room by the reactors. She doesn\'t appear as malnourished as the
  others around her. Her hair of a black/brown color surprisingly still

- **Size**: Medium

- **Initiative**: 0
- **Attacks**:

range must succeed on a
[Wisdom](SkillsBasics#Ability_Checks "Wisdom"){.wikilink} save (DC 11)
or be blinded for 1 round. Anyone within 20ft (including the control

:\* **Melee**: Can attack both phases of combat. Per phase, one Tentacle
Whip Ranged 50ft Touch Attack x2/20 1d4 - 1 (K) + 1d2 (E) \[Roll:

[grapple](Techniques#Combat_Techniques "grapple"){.wikilink} any person

Overview

: 

- **Objective:** Download the station\'s research log, salvage remaining
  high-tech equipment, and escape before the reactor collapses.
- **The Challenges:**

:\* If Dr. Lin **hasn\'t** been defeated yet, the console is locked out.
It is locked out by **BOTH** Dr. Lin and **Mother-7** the AI system. If
asked, Mother-7 explains that she doesn\'t want these evil experiments
set free. To obtain the data, 2 different players will need to succeed
on [Computer Use](Computer_Use "Computer Use"){.wikilink} checks (DC 25)
simultaneously. If even one fails, the system completely locks up and
cannot be tried again. Also, the players cannot take 10/20. If the
players somehow succeed, Mother-7 will plead with them not to go through
with it and deploy security droids to stop them (4 undamaged droids
appear through a heavy security door; this is the door to the armor; see
below for more details).

:\* If Dr. Lin **has** been defeated, special protocols have been
activated because of the reactor failure. The consoles are open. When
the players try to extract the data, Mother-7 pleads with them not to
proceed. The security door to the room with the droids is also open, but
the droids do not attack if someone enters; they simply politely ask the
players not to panic and to find the nearest available exit for their
own safety.

- **Mother-7 (Her story):** The AI system is called Mother-7. The
  station is named Aethelgard-7 because it is the 7th Generation of this
  station type, and this AI is the 7th-generation model. The company
  that built the station built it for the local government as a comms
  and observation facility. Its time has passed, and it was sold to
  Xeno-Synth Corp, who simply considered the AI part of the purchase and
  thus didn\'t replace it with their own. This AI, however, was built
  for the local government and had the local citizens in mind, as they
  were also the ones who operated it during its initial service. She/It
  sees these people as under her care, even though they no longer live
  on the station. She hates what the Xeno-Synth Corp has done here and
  will attempt to stop anyone who tries to take the research off
  station, especially if it ends up back in the Xeno-Synth Corp\'s
  hands.
- **The Revelation:** The downloaded files reveal that Xeno-Synth Corp
  under Dr. Vance\'s administration had taken on a new medical contract
  to expand a nanite healing system. However, this was just a cover
  story for further research into advancing a new type of highly
  integrated cybernetic implant. You also learn that Dr. Vance hired a
  human scientist named Dr. Sarah Lin and her team to lead the illegal
  work, possibly for plausible deniability. At some point, Dr. Lin began
  infecting the station\'s staff with the nanomachines. It is unknown if
  this happened before or after she herself was infected. A [Knowledge
  Culture](Knowledge#Culture "Knowledge Culture"){.wikilink} check
  (DC 12) reveals that advancing cybernetics beyond medical application
  is illegal in the Grayling government. A [Knowledge
  Culture](Knowledge#Culture "Knowledge Culture"){.wikilink} check
  (DC 14) reveals that Graylings take a lot of pride in their
  intellectual abilities; it would be extremely scandalous for a
  Grayling scientist to admit to having alien help, especially from a
  human. A [Computer Use](Computer_Use "Computer Use"){.wikilink} check
  (DC 15) reveals that Dr. Vance was not only aware of but also
  encouraged Dr. Lin\'s unorthodox and highly illegal research methods,
  which was another reason for placing the station so far out of reach.

Details

: 

- **Escaping Aethelgard-7:** The players will either need to override
  the locks in the computer system or use
  [Diplomacy](Diplomacy "Diplomacy"){.wikilink} or
  [Bluff](Bluff "Bluff"){.wikilink} (Intimidate will not work). The AI
  will know if the players take the research, so if they wish to take
  the research, they must explain it will be used only to incriminate
  Xeno-Synth Corp.
- **Perception Check:** DC14 reveals a Station map on display showing
  all the exits and the quickest way to get to each possible exit. A
  quick glance reveals to the players that there is a 2nd Hangar Bay
  highlighted. But further questioning of the AI or review of the
  computer systems will be necessary to determine whether that is an
  actual way out.
- **Computer Use:**\\

:\* (Requires Perception DC14) DC5: The Players learn about the private
hangar gay detailed in the DC 12 section below.

:\* DC 14: A player learns the full layout of the station, including a
secondary private hangar bay accessible through the Cargo Bay. In the
hangar is an experimental prototype Cruiser-class Grayling civilian
personal luxury transport, upgraded with corporate security in mind. The
player learns that this ship is the personal property of Dr. Valen
Vance, who loaned it to Dr. Sarah Lin as a corporate perk. The ship
requires a full crew of players to activate and fly, and it also
requires Dr. Sarah Lin\'s personal keycard, which she drops when she
dies.

:\* DC 17 is necessary to override the lockdown. The players can try
twice before they cannot try again.

- **Diplomacy:**

:\* DC 22 to allow the players to leave with the research to be used to
incriminate the Xeno-Synth Corp.

- **Bluff:**

:\* An opposing [Bluff](Bluff "Bluff"){.wikilink} check against the
AI\'s [Sense Motive](Sense_Motive "Sense Motive"){.wikilink} check to
swear you will not sell the research but send it to the local government
to incriminate them.

- **The Salvage (Reward):** In the bridge\'s secure armory, the players
  find:

:\* A [Tactical Laser
Pistol](Laser_Pistol#Tactical_Laser_Pistol "Tactical Laser Pistol"){.wikilink}

- **Moral Dilemma & Endings:**

:\* **The Way Out:** If the players discover the prototype cruiser ship,
they now have a second means of escape. They no longer need to escape
with the scavenger ship and free the captain. If the team cannot unlock
the hangar bay doors or convince the AI to do so, they can take this
route, but at the cost of the captain\'s life. However, a player may
convince the AI to let the ship go with a
[Diplomacy](Diplomacy "Diplomacy"){.wikilink} DC 10, arguing that the

::\* **Take the Ship:** Dr. Vance will want the ship back. If the
players choose to deliver the containers, he offers an additional 50,000

players refuse, regardless of the other options they take, Dr. Vance and
Xeno-Synth Corp will send Tracers (Bounty Hunters) after the crew.

- **Option A (Corporate Payout):** Deliver the containers and data to

- **Option B (Leaked Files):** Leak the files to the local system
  government. Vance blacklists the crew (**-1 Credit Score**), but the
  Local Government rewards them with 5,000 ISC per crew member,
  celebrates the crew as heroes, and offers them future work.
- **Option C (Black Market Broker):** Wipe their tracks, keep the data,
  and sell the pathogen on the black market. Earn 250,000 ISC on the
  black market, but corporate bounty hunters will hunt them in the
  future.
