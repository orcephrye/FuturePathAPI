The Helm Officer is a [Ship
Position](Space_Ships#Ship_Positions "Ship Position"){.wikilink}. Other
ship positions can be reviewed and may affect this position.

The Helm Officer is a primary position and is required on all ship
sizes. Tiny or smaller-sized ships can combine the Helmsman position
with the Weapon Officer and/or Communications Officer.

## Description

The Helm Officer pilots the ship. Their skills can help the ship avoid
danger and position it more effectively for weapon targeting.

: **Requirement**: Must have at least 1 rank in the Pilot Skill.
: **Associated Ship Attribute**: Engines
: **Associated Secondary Attribute**: Tactical Mobility - This is equal
  to the Ship\'s Engines Modifier + The Helm\'s Officer\'s Dexterity
  Modifier + The Helm\'s Officer\'s Misc Bonuses to Pilot check.
: **Associated Tertiary Attribute**: Tracking DC - This is based on Hull
  size as well as Tactical Mobility. It is 10 + (Tactical Mobility +
  Shield Mod) - Structure Mod +/- Size Mod
: **Officer Functions**:
  1.  [`<u>`{=html}Pilot Offensively or
      Defensively`</u>`{=html}](Fly_Offensively/Defensively "Pilot Offensively or Defensively"){.wikilink}:
      This is a Pilot check done against the Enemy vessel. The Pilot
      picks the target vessel and then rolls. If they win, then their
      chosen outcome happens. If the Pilot chooses Offensive flight,
      then the Weapon's office needs 1 less d10 for Battle Damage.
      (Minimum is 1). If the Pilot chooses Defensive flight, then the
      enemy ship has to have 1 more successful d10 in order to deliver
      Battle Damage. The enemy ship's pilot\'s choice only matters if
      their pilot check wins. If there are more enemy ships than their
      Pilot checks are unopposed, then they automatically win. Remember
      that in Swarm combats, the Pilot can target the Swarm as a whole,
      a single entity.
  2.  [`<u>`{=html}Close or Widen Distance to
      Target`</u>`{=html}](Close/Widen_Distance "Close or Widen Distance to Target"){.wikilink}:
      In Space Combat there are distances. Short, Medium, Long, Huge,
      Vast, Astronomical. Different distances can affect the ship\'s
      ability to target and fire. Some weapons cannot fire at some
      ranges. It may be useful to determine the enemy ship\'s abilities
      and then pick the best range to fire from. This Pilot check moves
      the ship to a different distance. If the opposing check is higher
      than the enemy ship, the enemy ship has the choice to either let
      the ship make the distance change or not.
  3.  [`<u>`{=html}Pursue or Intercept
      Target`</u>`{=html}](Pursue/Intercept "Pursue or Intercept Target"){.wikilink}:
      This is used for Chase mechanics. A Pilot who makes this check
      against an enemy ship allows the enemy ship to know that you are
      attempting to catch up to them and/or set up for a Ram. If the
      opposing check is higher, the enemy ship can choose to avoid the
      Interception.
  4.  `<u>`{=html}[Ram](Ram "Ram"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}: If in Short
      range and a successful
      [Pursue/Intercept](Pursue/Intercept "Pursue/Intercept"){.wikilink}
      check was done the last round, then this round the Pilot can
      choose to Ram the enemy vessel. Roll a percentile check. If above
      50%, both ships take Battle Damage. If below 50%, then only the
      enemy ship takes Battle Damage. If your ship takes Battle Damage,
      the crew is ready for impact and is not shaken. The DC is simply 7
      to repair the damage. While the enemy ship, you have to roll for
      Battle Damage and re-roll 7s.
: **Advanced Officer Functions**:
  1.  `<u>`{=html}[Emergency Evasive
      Thrusters](Emergency_Evasive_Thrusters "Emergency Evasive Thrusters"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}:
      The Helm Officer executes a sudden maneuver using emergency
      auxiliary thrusters to evade an immediate threat. Opposing Pilot
      check, but to succeed, the Pilot must beat the Opposing check by
      more than 4. Success forces the next enemy attack to require 3
      additional success dice to deal Battle Damage, and a Tracking DC
      of 1 +1 for every point beaten. A normal success just treats this
      like a Pilot Defensively Function. For that round, the Weapon\'s
      Officer must cancel 1 successful d10 from the Battle Pool before
      re-rolling. Afterward, the Engines must cool down, and this cannot
      be attempted again for at least 3 turns.
  2.  `<u>`{=html}[Feint](Feint "Feint"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}:
      (Advanced Flying Defensively): Allows the Pilot to make a highly
      complicated and special maneuver that completely throws off the
      enemy ship. This provides a +4 to Tracking DC and requires 2 more
      successful d10s to do Battle Damage. Each time a successful Feint
      is done against a ship, the Pilot gains disadvantage on the Pilot
      check to attempt another Feint, making it harder and harder until
      it is impossible to trick the enemy again.
