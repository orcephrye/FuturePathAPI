**Prerequisites**: Dexterity 13, Defensive Martial Arts.

**Benefit**: When fighting an opponent or multiple opponents in melee,
other opponents attempting to target the character with ranged attacks
take a disadvantage. This penalty is in addition to the normal
disadvantage for firing into melee, making the penalty to target the
character two disadvantages stacked. This also counts if the Character
is performing a Sprint or Dash
[move](Complete_Combat_Actions#Bonus_Actions "move"){.wikilink}.

**Normal**: -

**Special**: An opponent with the Precise Shot feat has the penalty
lessened by one disadvantage when targeting the character, as it removes
the disadvantage from firing into melee.
