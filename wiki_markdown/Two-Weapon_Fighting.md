**Prerequisite**: Dexterity 13.

**Benefit**: The character can now use a second weapon ranged or melee
if it is already in there and and is considered a Small weapon. This is
considered a bonus attack action and can only be done once in a round
and only if the Standard Action was too Attack. The Character is not
able to apply there Affinity bonus to the accuracy check even if they
are proficient with the off hand weapon.

If the offhand weapon is melee the offhand weapon\'s damage die is
reduced by one level. I.E 1d8 to 1d6.

**Normal**: Normally a Character can not use a second weapon even if
they have it in hand.
