**Benefit**: The character gains an advantage bonus on the following
checks and saves: hourly Swim checks to avoid becoming fatigued,
Constitution checks to continue running, Constitution checks to hold the
character's breath, Constitution checks to avoid damage from starvation
or thirst, Constitution saves to avoid damage from hot or cold
environments, and Constitution saves to resist suffocation or drowning.
