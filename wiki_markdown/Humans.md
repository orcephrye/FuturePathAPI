## Overview

Humans have an interesting habit of blowing things up, including
themselves. In this universe, the Cold War never ended. Sergei Korolev
didn\'t die from surgery during the space race. His expertise and
knowledge enabled the Soviets to reach the Moon. Behind the American\'s
but only by 3 months. Then, to one-up the Americans, they sent a
modified version of the N1 to Venus, making the first humans to orbit
another planet. The Soviet Union didn\'t collapse because Mikhail
Gorbachev never came to power. His pro-democratic and open policies were
applied with care and more slowly. This allowed the Soviet Union to
evolve carefully and, although not without its problems, stay united.
The United States and the allied powers\' attempt to crippling the
economy of the USSR was not nearly as successful. It nearly collapsed
until the late 90s, when the rise of China as an economic superpower
helped propel Russia. And together they reignite a Cold War 2 and the
new Space Race in the late 90s, with new missions involving the
colonization of Mars. The UN/USA became afraid of the idea of a whole
planet taken over by communism. So in turn, they rushed to lay claim.

Before China teamed with Russia to rapidly propel the nation into the
Space age, America and the European Union didn\'t collaborate as much.
The Freedom Station was built with the Space Shuttle instead of the
International Station. NASA\'s budget didn\'t decrease nearly as much
until the late 80s, and as such, the Saturn V wasn\'t canceled until the
mid-80s. The Shuttle was still constructed in part because of military
demand, and many of its flaws persisted. The Soviet Union also kept its
Buran project alive and upgraded it as a response to America replacing
its Shuttle Program with the X-33.

Russia and China together beat America to Mars. In order to one-up the
communists, America and the EU created a joint Mars Mission task force.
However, both space programs had a massive focus on low to geostationary
orbit, and the money made in selling satellite launches to these
positions. Team Blue was nearly a decade behind in achieving the first
step on Mars. However, in order to one-up the Russians and the Chinese,
America proposed an extremely expensive and almost inane project. One
that never truly got canceled and sat in the military\'s eye, and even
some from NASA. Project Orion. The project that used Nuclear Warheads to
propel a massive ship close to 1% the speed of light. This would require
10+ massive launches of Saturn V level of lift with X-33 Space Shuttles
acting as support. Sadly, the Saturn V had long been canceled, and due
to budget cuts and the insanely high cost of building the Freedom
station with the first version of the Space Shuttle, both the EU and
America lacked a launcher. So instead, they made a new one based on
Project Sea Dragon. The EU conducted research on habitats by building
Long-Term Habitats on the moon during the 2010s. The project took 11
years to complete, and in 2021, Orion exploded to life in high orbit
above Earth, sending nearly 30 people (only half the capacity) to spend
2 years on Mars testing survivability. This system used lasers to
achieve criticality rather than a classical explosion. This vastly
improved upon the old 1960s design and allowed for much more delta V.

The Russians and the Chinese were not idle during this time. They knew
something was up and that the Americans and the European Union were
building something on a massive scale. But there was an excessive amount
of secrecy. The Russians had reached Mars using Thermal Nuclear Rockets,
which were ideal for shuttling payloads between Earth and Mars due to
their greater efficiency and reusability. And it was just one more thing
that they used to rub it in the face of those Westerners. So the thought
in the USSR was that America was working on a newer generation of
high-trust gas-based thermal nuclear rockets. These massive engines
would require a massive amount of lift and multiple payloads to build in
orbit. Or so the thinking went. Although considered decades away, Russia
started to develop the concept in hopes of keeping up with anything
America produced. China, however, had a different approach, and this is
where the issues of their relationship became apparent on the global
stage. Before this, it appeared from the outside looking in that all
things were friendly between the nations. But China viewed the threat
with a long-term perspective. Instead, they continued with their already
existing and tested technologies for their long-term goal of a Red
Martian planet under a Communist banner.

When Orion was revealed, it was a shock to Russia. At first glance, this
massive beast looked superior to anything before it. Russia continued
its own project and, 4 years later, released the first generation
gas-based thermal nuclear rocket. In many respects, it actually
outperformed Orion. Orion was an overly expensive status symbol, way too
massive and powerful for the job it was given. It flew to Mars only 4
times. It had 6 missions in total, 2 of which involved exploring and
visiting Jupiter. The vessel was used more for science and exploration
than for guaranteeing dominance on Mars. It turns out that cheaper,
smaller, reusable rockets, in mass, were sending large numbers of
\'willing\' settlers to Mars. In the coming decades, one country will
lead the way in Mars colonization: China. America quickly realized it
was losing the battle Orion was supposed to fight and attempted to
correct the situation by catching up in Thermal Rockets and producing a
new line of cheaper, more reusable rockets. In reverse, Russia, not
wanting to be outdone by a massive nuclear exploding spacecraft of
capitalism, built its own. In 2032, it released its own Nuclear pulse
propulsion craft, despite the fact that it, along with China, had
originally protested the existence of Orion.

In 2025 AD the [Jove](Jove "Jove"){.wikilink} detected higher than
normal levels of neutrinos. The Jove noticed humans roughly a thousand
years previously and set up sensors in the nearby Alpha Centauri solar
system. The Jove decided it was time to pay closer attention to these
humans. They decided to wait a while to determine if the Species would
develop Fusion power and propulsion. Sure enough, America, hoping to
one-up the Russians again, built a second Orion-style craft, this time
with joint EU participation, designed to continue Orion\'s exploratory
mission. In the early 2030s, fusion power had finally been achieved, and
even before that, a Fusion-powered pulse propulsion system had been
designed. Instead of Fission bombs, they would use Fusion explosions to
propel the craft. Taking advantage of their expertise in Laser-induced
Fission, which turned out to be a great way to perform Ignition of
Fusion materials as well. This higher level of efficiency was the final
step needed to not only explore the further reaches of the solar system,
its main mission, but also to send probes and eventually people to the
nearest solar systems.

One fateful day, August 21, 2044 AD, the [Jove](Jove "Jove"){.wikilink}
made official contact with Humans. You would think seeing evidence of
intelligent life would help to unite a species. But all it really did
was cause more problems. After the [Jove](Jove "Jove"){.wikilink}
introduced Humans to the rest of their galactic neighbors, humans were
bombarded by all kinds of species. It is always a treat to see new
species and their novelty. It\'s fun to watch the early years of
spaceflight, or how they first start to interact with the universe in a
whole new, big way. And for some, it\'s a chance to exploit a new race.
The Jove did explain these types of nefarious characters. Most humans
ignore it.

Within a decade, it had become frighteningly obvious that there were
issues. Who got what trade? Who decided what for the whole of the human
race? If the Communists get that latest new tech from so and so, then
what is the Free World to do? After just 3 decades, the human species
threw itself into a war most didn\'t even care to fight. And the 3rd
great World War started\... not even on Earth. Although it was finished
on Earth. The Jove do not step in on these matters. They would much
rather let such uncivilized activities take their course. It\'s the best
way to ensure the species learns a lesson and matures. As the AI of the
Jove has wisely learned over and over.

Before humanity had even shed its novelty to its neighbors, it had
gained the reputation as being uncivilized killers of their own race.
Humanity\'s first impressions on the rest of the galaxy were not a
shining moment some would dream of. They came off as a very
short-sighted and backward species willing to destroy their own home
planet to get at each other\'s throats. Humans had nuked their own
planet almost to oblivion. Had done nearly the same damage to Mars. And
had gone from nearly 12 billion to 400 million in less than 2 years.

## Culture/Civilization/Society

After the Great 3rd World War was finished, it left humanity with mass
starvation and little land left in their own solar system. Most left the
now pitiful empires of Earth, the Reds and the Blues, as they were
called by many, now reduced to nothing more than stubborn and old men
holding on to memories more than hope. The Unions and Federations formed
in the ashes were largely structured by ideals presented by the
Corporatocracy. What was left of Humanity was ruled by aggressive
companies that cared more about the few remaining resources on the
planet. This was accepted by most people because it is the only way to
live. With such scant resources left, every person had to have their
place, perform their job, and produce something for themselves and the
whole, in other words, the Company. The more wealth you have, the more
you can produce, the more valuable you are to everyone else. The more
powerful you were.

However, others choose to leave this dark and depressing lifestyle. They
dared to have hope in a new life. They left Sol and went out into the
Great Ring. Having to find new homes and make friends with species that
neither wanted them nor liked them. Looked down upon for either their
reckless behavior or countless other reputational issues. Humans are now
seen as the homeless riffraff of the Milky Way.

Many roam the wastelands of Earth trying to scrape a living and avoid
the large population centers. Many of which are domed cities ruled by
some federation of companies, and most occupants border-line slaves
working daily for corporations, and their own survival. Other dangers
lurk in the apocalyptic wastelands of Earth. Mutated people and animals
and deadly radioactive weather all put fear into the bravest explorers.

Mars is slightly better off. Most of its land is not radioactive, but
Mars never started to be Terraformed, making it a harsh place to live,
let alone try to thrive on. People here are less likely to be mutated,
but they do have a whole host of other issues. Corporations view the
planet as just an industrial resource, and its inhabitants are of no
concern to them. Leaving people to fend for themselves. The planet
plunged into anarchy after the War and has been struggling to find peace
ever since.

The Venus exploration and habitat project began just before first
contact and experienced a massive boom in the years leading up to the
war. It was almost completely untouched by the War. Just before the war
broke out, the first phase of Project New Eden started. The goal of
terraforming Venus began and then abruptly halted with the start of the
War. However, the scientists and workers stationed around Venus survived
their families and, 70 years later, struggle to keep the project alive.
Feeling the pressure of this massive undertaking and swelling with
weight from the realization that Earth may be truly lost, this may be
humanity\'s only hope for a new start. These people brave opposition and
conflict from pirates to the Corporations trying to gain access to the
Solar System resources, not willing to give them up for some foolhardy
plan.

Many allies have taken up residence in all the chaos and hope to profit
from it. They plan to take the riches of the Sol Solar System for
themselves. They see these backward humans as barely a Tech 1
civilization and stealing from them like stealing from a baby. Some
humans have devoted their lives to making sure Sol stays rightfully in
human hands. They see their origin solar system as theirs by right,
whether it be divine or otherwise.

## Needs/Role Playing/Mysteries {#needsrole_playingmysteries}

Humanity has a lot to prove to itself and to the galaxy, and little
means to do it. Will you try to help rebuild the home world? Protect
Earth\'s last great cities from collapse. Or go out into the wastelands
and make a name for yourself. Perhaps the whole of Sol itself and its
many planets and moons are of interest? After all, they are rightfully
human. How about protecting the heritage of humanity, its solar system,
from being overrun by greedy aliens who are taking advantage of the
weekend race. Perhaps help keep the Terraform Venus project alive? A new
home with a fresh start is what humanity needs. If Humanities heritage
is not of interest, then the whole of The Great Ring is? Will you boldly
go into the bigger galaxy as a pirate or mercenary? Or perhaps as an
Ambassador to help fight for humanity?
