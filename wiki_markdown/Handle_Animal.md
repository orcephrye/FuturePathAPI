You are trained at working with animals and can teach them tricks, get
them to follow your simple commands, or even domesticate them.

NOTE
: This is a special skill that has to be earned/unlocked for a
  Character. Once earned, this skill instantly gets the Ranks of both
  Diplomacy and Survival combined. This skill is not limited by the Rank
  level rules. This skill is associated with either Wisdom **OR**
  Charisma. When the Skill is acquired, the Player must choose. The
  Player cannot spend skill points on this skill. To improve its Rank
  the Player must spend points on Diplomacy or Survival.

## Uses

### Handle an Animal {#handle_an_animal}

This task involves commanding an animal to perform a task or trick that
it knows. If the animal is wounded or has taken any nonlethal damage or
ability score damage, the DC increases by 2. If your check succeeds, the
animal performs the task or trick on its next action.

### Push an Animal {#push_an_animal}

To push an animal means to get it to perform a task or trick it doesn't
know but is physically capable of. This category also covers forcing an
animal to perform a forced march or to hustle for more than 1 hour
between sleep cycles. If the animal is wounded or has taken any
nonlethal damage or ability score damage, the DC increases by 2. If your
check succeeds, the animal performs the task or trick on its next
action.

### Teach an Animal a Trick {#teach_an_animal_a_trick}

You can teach an animal a specific trick with one week of work and a
successful Handle Animal check against the indicated DC. An animal with
an Intelligence score of 1 can learn a maximum of three tricks, while an
animal with an Intelligence score of 2 can learn a maximum of six
tricks.

### Rear a Wild Animal {#rear_a_wild_animal}

To rear an animal means to raise a wild creature from infancy so that it
becomes domesticated. A handler can rear as many as three creatures of
the same kind at once.

A successfully domesticated animal can be taught tricks at the same time
it's being raised, or it can be taught as a domesticated animal later.

## Action

Varies. Handling an animal is a move action, while "pushing" an animal
is a full-round action. (A druid or ranger can handle her animal
companion as a free action or push it as a move action.) For tasks with
specific time frames noted above, you must spend half this time (at the
rate of 3 hours per day per animal being handled) working toward
completion of the task before you attempt the Handle Animal check. If
the check fails, your attempt to teach, rear, or train the animal fails,
and you need not complete the teaching, rearing, or training time. If
the check succeeds, you must invest the remaining time to complete the
teaching, rearing, or training. If the attempt to teach, rear, or train
the animal is interrupted or not carried through to completion, the
attempt automatically fails.

## Try Again {#try_again}

Yes, except for rearing an animal.

## Special

- **IMPORTANT**: The Rank of this skill is the Rank of Diplomacy and
  Survival combined and doesn\'t follow the normal Skill Rank level
  restrictions.
- **IMPORTANT**: This skill can be either associated with Wisdom **OR**
  Charisma. When the Skill is acquired, the Player must choose.
- **IMPORTANT**: The Player cannot spend skill points on this skill. To
  improve its Rank the Player must spend points on Diplomacy and
  Survival.
- **Low Intelligence**: Non-Animals You can use this skill on a creature
  with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2 that is not an animal, but the DC
  of any such check increases by 5. Such creatures have the same limit
  on tricks known as animals do.
