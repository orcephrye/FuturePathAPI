There are 4 main categories for species.
**Major**/**Minor**/**Advanced**/**Non-Playable** Species.

There are 2 criteria that have to be met in order to be a Major Species
in the Future Path Galaxy.

1.  The Species has to be well-traveled. For the majority of campaign
    settings, it should not be too odd for this species to be seen. This
    doesn\'t mean the species is everywhere in the galaxy or even in
    large numbers, but that it shouldn\'t be too strange to find them in
    most corners of the galaxy.
2.  The Species either has to have made a major impact on the history of
    the Galaxy, or they have a major role to play in Current Events.

Any species that doesn\'t meet both the above criteria is considered a
Minor Species. Non-Playable Species are deemed so by the creators
because of how they fit with the story of Future Path, or that they are
not overpowered. A GM can override this rule and allow a Player to play
a Non-Playable Species.

## Humans

Humans have scattered after the first contact with aliens. Their
homeworld, Earth, was torn apart by its inhabitants. Most of it is a
nuclear wasteland full of desperate survivors struggling to live off the
monstrous landscape. Some dare to dream of the old world and try to
bring back some of humanity\'s former glory. Others choose to hurdle
themselves out into The Great Ring as refugees. They dared instead to
hope. Hope that the Galaxy will be merciful and that the bounty of space
will bring them wealth and prosperity. Many quickly learned that if you
wish to survive, you must band together and be willing to take on the
harsh reality of life in space.

Many other species see humans as more stupid than clever. However, they
can be incredibly useful and show great potential if they could just get
around some of those annoying human flaws. Greed and arrogance are high
on the list. Most species see humans as more of a nuisance than anything
else. Humans are willing to work hard and have a remarkable ability to
adapt if given time.

For a complete history and narrative about Humans in The Great Ring
Galaxy, go to the [Humans](Humans "Humans"){.wikilink} page.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: Humans are bipedal with two arms, two eyes. and hair on their heads.
  The hair, skin, and eyes can vary in color.

`<u>`{=html}**Species Traits**`</u>`{=html}

- Class: Mammal
- Locomotion: bipedal (Base Speed: 15ft)
- Reproduction: sexual
- Average Height Male: 5\' 10\'\' (177 cm)
- Average Height Female: 5\' 5\'\' (163 - 164 cm)
- Average Life Span: 80 - 85 years
- Size: Medium
- Main Home Worlds: Earth, Mars, Proxima b, Venus
- [Tech Level](Tech_Level_and_Civ_Progress "Tech Level"){.wikilink}:

    one Skill a Favored Skill.
2.  Bonus Feat (Misc only) at level 1
3.  Bonus Skill point at level 1 (Cannot be a Knowledge Skill)

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html}

: Humans lack formal relations with most of the Major species. They are
  closest with the [Sayor](Sayor "Sayor"){.wikilink} and
  [Kurgian](Kurgian "Kurgian"){.wikilink} due mainly to their shared
  border. Humans also share a border with both the
  [Lepidonains](#Lepidonains "Lepidonains"){.wikilink} and the
  [Cryous](#Cryous "Cryous"){.wikilink}, and both of these major Species
  have tried to involve themselves in human affairs. But find humans
  difficult due to their lack of a central authority. A coalition of
  corporations that rule over most humans back on Earth and Mars is the
  closest thing to a government humans have, making \'formal\' relations
  difficult. Most humans have fled their home system and are scattered
  amongst the stars, with no single government that represents them.
  Most other Species treat humans on a case-by-case basis and normally
  find them to be a nuisance, except for the Sayor and Kurgian, which
  view humans as both an eager workforce and an excellent future ally.

`<u>`{=html}**Opinions on Difficulties**`</u>`{=html}

: Humans are too new to the galaxy and too scattered to form policy on
  the Cybernetics/Mutations/Psionics. Most Humans, until recently, just
  thought Psionics were part of science fiction. However, after the
  nuclear holocaust of their home world, mutations and even Psionics are
  on the rise. Also, many humans feel inadequate and overwhelmed by the
  size and knowledge of the greater galaxy and thus are willing to seek
  out any edge they can have. For many humans who still live on Earth, a
  few mutations are a sad reminder of their recent past and an
  unfortunate part of life. Psionics, however, are still seen as either
  trickery or as something\... unnatural, and most people try to ignore
  it. Cybernetics for humans is often sought after, unregulated, and
  difficult to obtain, since humans are a Tech 1 civilization.

### (Sub-Species) Human Mutant {#sub_species_human_mutant}

The radiation- and war-ravaged Earth is full of ghastly sites. While
there are some green habitats, they are far apart and few and far
between. Making this all the more important to protect. Life is harsh
and full of constant and often deadly change. What haunts the sunken
cities buried in sand and ash has been forged by this new environment,
and not all the creatures that roam the world\'s abandoned places are
mindless. Here, nature has found a way, ugly and scared, but alive. Many
humans who develop mutations do not find them very helpful and are cast
out of the greener places of the world in hopes of protecting what
precious space remains, as if the ugliness of the mutation would stain
it. Some of these poor humans have realized more has come from their
misfortune, and in this pain and gruesome appearance, they must endure;
they have unlocked new abilities.

Mutant Humans are on the rise on Earth. While most humans of Earth bear
physical scars, signs of their recent sins in the nuclear fallout of the
last great World War, some of those are worse than others, showing as
horrific deformities. While others display newfound abilities.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: Like normal Humans but with deformities such as missing limbs,
  translucent skin, 3 eyes, a tail, etc\...

`<u>`{=html}**Special Specie Traits**`</u>`{=html}

1.  All the same things from a normal Human
2.  Take -3 Ability points up to any combination, i.e., -1 to 3
    different abilities or spread out as the Player wishes.
3.  Take an example [Cosmetic
    Mutation](Mutation_Enhancements#Cosmetic_Mutations "Cosmetic Mutation"){.wikilink}
    or make up one. Earning a -3 to Disguise Checks.
4.  Be declared as Character Level 1 as a Mutant and automatically gain
    6MP.
5.  From now on, whenever a Feat is gained, include
    [Difficulties](Difficulties "Difficulties"){.wikilink} as a Category
    to choose from.
6.  Change the spending MP rules to be Character Level +4 (With starting
    level 1 being able to spend all 6 MP)

## Volar

![](Volar-Group-transparent.png "Volar-Group-transparent.png"){width="720"
height="900"} Volar is short for Victarians; however, most species,
including the Victarians, simply call themselves Volar. The word Volar
in their language stands for Mother, and that is what the Volar called
their homeworld Volar. This shows their deep connection to and respect
for the beautiful, massive planet Volar. Volar are often found traveling
in groups, and a stray Volar is almost never considered a stranger to
another Volar. They don\'t stay in one place for too long, as all Volar
prefer to travel back to their homeworld as often as possible. Volars
born outside the home planet can be outcasts within their own society. A
Volar can usually tell when another wasn\'t born in \'The Mothers
Womb\'. A Volar that finds itself in this situation may attempt to spend
as much time with \'Mother Planet\' in order to cover up this
embarrassing truth. This strong feeling of contempt against \'unbirths\'
is an odd contrast to the otherwise accepting and loving nature of
Volars, not only to each other but to all life.

For a complete history and narrative about the Volar, go to the
[Volar](Volar "Volar"){.wikilink} page.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: A Volar has no hair, although some can have a leafy-like substitute on
  their head and shoulders. All have a bud on their head that is
  normally small and hidden but can bloom on command (see Nauseous Bloom
  Special Specie Traits). This also flowers whenever a Volar is looking
  to reproduce. Their skin color can range from light shades. Such as
  Light (Green/Blue/Yellow/Red/Orange). A Volar usually has dark spots
  and sometimes patterns, with its primary color in a different shade.
  These spots increase over time as they age.

`<u>`{=html}**Species Traits**`</u>`{=html}

- Class: Plant
- Locomotion: bipedal (Base Speed 15ft)
- Reproduction: Asexual
- Average Height: 4\' 8\'\' (\~146 - 167 cm)
- Average Life Span: 90 - 100 years
- Size: Medium
- Main Home Worlds: Volar, Victar, Excentus Prime, Excentus Beta
- [Tech Level](Tech_Level_and_Civ_Progress "Tech Level"){.wikilink}:
  starts out as 2

`<u>`{=html}**Special Specie Traits**`</u>`{=html}

1.  Loses one point on Dexterity, Gains one point on Wisdom
2.  **Common Bond**: Volar can communicate using pheromones in an almost
    \'psychic\' link to other Volar within roughly 120ft, even if they
    are not in view. They can only share feelings and flashes of
    thought. However, this means that if more than one Volar is close to
    each other and are allies, they gain an advantage bonus against
    Mind-Effecting conditions/techniques.
3.  **Nauseous Blooms**: Once per day, a Volar can expel nauseous gases
    as self-defense. They can target a person within 30 feet or produce
    a 15-foot radius cloud of gas around themselves. The ranged attack
    is considered a ranged touch attack, and the cloud lasts for 1d4 +
    1/4th Character level round-up turns. Anyone who breathes the air or
    gets hit by the attack has to take a Constitution Saving throw of a
    DC12 + Con Mod + 1/4th Character level, rounded down. If they fail
    the throw, they become
    [Nauseated](Conditions#Nauseated "Nauseated"){.wikilink} for 1d4 +
    1/4th Character level round downturns. They have to re-roll for
    every turn they are within the radius. If they fail critically or
    twice in a row, then they become
    [Sickened](Conditions#-_Sickened "Sickened"){.wikilink}. **Note**: A
    Volar can choose to excrete this in the form of a liquid instead of
    using it as a defensive. The liquid becomes inert in 12 hours unless
    it is Crafted. Craft Chemical (DC10) will concentrate it into a
    Poison with the same effect, while Craft Pharmaceutical (DC10) will
    create an antidote that can be used to avoid the effects. Once
    crafted, the item is good for up to 7 days.
4.  **Rapid Re-Growth**: As long as the Volar is in well-lighted and
    healthy conditions for a plant and takes a full day to rest, the
    Volar can rapidly heal. Restoring 100% of health in just one night
    of rest. They also gain an advantage in recovering from sickness,
    poisons, and diseases for every day they rest.

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html}

: Volar has only been on the galactic stage for a few thousand years.
  But during that time, they have made a reputation as excellent
  students of alien biology and have shaken up the galactic terraforming
  business. One the Greylings had until recently a strong standing with.
  Volar scientists and engineers are paid well to observe and advise on
  a terraforming project. This, in part, has propelled Volar as a
  galactic species of some importance. Most Major and Minor species look
  to expand by taking once dead or uninhabitable worlds and making them
  more suitable for themselves. Although most Volarians consider it
  taboo to try to alter an already \'living\' planet to benefit a
  foreign species, rather than adapting to that environment genetically.
  Something Graylings are all too happy to point out.

`<u>`{=html}**Opinions on Difficulties**`</u>`{=html}

: Volarian politics around Difficulties are fairly strict. They see
  Cybernetics as an abomination, even from a medical standpoint. Volar
  do have the ability to slowly regrow limbs, which may contribute to
  this bias, but it would be extremely unusual to find a Volar with a
  cybernetic implant, especially a medical-specific one. Natural
  Mutations, on the other hand, are completely accepted, and in ancient
  times, Valors used to worship those with noticeable mutations.
  Although not illegal, \'fixing\' a natural mutation is heavily frowned
  upon. And it is strictly forbidden to artificially genetically enhance
  a person in Volarian-controlled territory. As far as Psionics are
  concerned, Volar people for the longest time refused to accept such
  things as real. They themselves possess a psychic-like link, but it is
  actually just a pheromone-based process. So most Volar just thought
  others who claimed enhanced abilities were just tricksters. Today,
  they have accepted the phenomenon as being real but downplay it.
  According to the official Volarian government stance, no Volarian has
  ever tested positive for true Psionic powers. Some believe that the
  Volar government deals with this behind closed doors.

## Graelycxazshngs (aka Graylings) {#graelycxazshngs_aka_graylings}

Due to their strange name and the race's staunch insistence upon the
proper pronunciation of it, most other beings have taken to referring to
Graelycxazshngs as Graylings because of their similar sound, being
easier on the tongue, and their reference to the greyish color of
Grayling skin. Many Graylings are aloof or distrustful of those outside
their race because of the stigma attached to their kind. Graylings are
still extremely curious and inquisitive, actively pursuing knowledge and
discovery. They are abnormally efficient at using their minds and value
intelligence and logic over brute strength or speed.

Whether out of a desire to redeem their race's reputation in the eyes of
others or to indeed contribute to preserving and expanding knowledge,
Graylings put a massive emphasis on education and the sciences.
Physically, graylings are approximately three feet tall with a large
head and prominent black eyes. While extremely intelligent and skilled
in the fields of science and engineering, graylings are physically
weaker in comparison to most other races, resulting in their bodies
being more susceptible to trauma. Their home planet is very arid, with
gravity closer to Mars. It circles a Red Dwarf and, due to a larger and
thicker atmosphere, gets more diffuse sunlight. This means most
graylings try to avoid overexposure to humidity, liquids, and light
radiation, as it is said to have disastrous effects on their skin. They
also commonly wear supportive gear to cope with the higher gravity on
most other planets.

For a complete history and narrative about the Graelycxazshngs go to the
[Graylings](Graylings "Graylings"){.wikilink} page.

`<u>`{=html}**Visual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: Graylings are named such for their gray skin. They have short stature
  and large dark eyes. However, most graylings love to tattoo their skin
  and even use dyes to adjust the skin\'s pigment and create colorful
  patterns. A common practice by most Graylings long before they could
  travel the stars. Likely due to the ease with which their skin pigment
  can be influenced. It is common to see almost-naked Grayling with an
  ornate design covering most of their exposed skin. They lack hair, and
  their skin often appears to shine like it is wet.

`<u>`{=html}**Species Traits**`</u>`{=html}

- Class: Mammal
- Locomotion: bipedal (Base Speed 15ft)
- Reproduction: Sexual
- Average Height Male and Female: 3\' 2\'\' (\~91 cm)
- Average Life Span: 180 - 210 years
- Size: Small
- Main Home Worlds: Cxazshngs, Curvilinea Prime, Eugen
- [Tech Level](Tech_Level_and_Civ_Progress "Tech Level"){.wikilink}:
  starts out as 3

`<u>`{=html}**Special Specie Traits**`</u>`{=html}

1.  Gain Level 0 [Telepath](Psionics#Telepath "Telepath"){.wikilink}
    techniques at Level 1. (Technically not a Telepath or other Psionic)
2.  Can optionally choose to become a Telepath for no MP cost, the
    Grayling still must take a Drawback before becoming a Psion.
3.  Lose two in Strength, gain two in Intelligence.
4.  Lose one in Dexterity, choose between Intelligence, Wisdom, or
    Charisma to add an ability point.
5.  Choose any one Knowledge skill and add a +1 Misc bonus to it.
6.  Choose any Crafting Skill to be a Class Skill of the Character Path.

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html}

: A truly Tech 3 civilization that, in some ways, is on the brink of
  Tech 4. Often sought out for their technology. Different civilizations
  pay enormous sums to buy Grayling\'s tech. They still stand as the
  most successful Planet Engineers forming planets many people find too
  difficult to adjust. They produce the finest weapons and warships,
  except perhaps the Jove, and have the world\'s largest Cloning and
  Mind transportation network. The Grayling Empire stopped its expansion
  early compared to many other species\' attempts at Empire, and thus is
  small, like their statue. However, because of this relationship with
  other nations, it is all around the best. Nobody wants to piss off a
  Grayling, and everybody wants to buy what Graylings have to offer.

`<u>`{=html}**Opinions on Difficulties**`</u>`{=html}

: The Graelycxazshngs fully embrace both Cybernetics and Psionics, but
  not Mutants. Many Graylings feel their smaller frames and frail bodies
  need enhancement in order to compete with most other advanced life
  forms. And so, a common one, especially among the younger population,
  is Cybernetics. The Grayling species is also a raw species that has
  somehow evolved to have a significantly higher amount of Psionics
  within its population. A mystery even to this day. But Grayling
  culture accepted the idea of Psionics well before they learned to fly
  in space. However, they have downplayed their significance on the
  global stage due to other species\' distrust of, or even hatred for,
  Psionics. Still, a Psionic will feel right at home on Curvilinea
  Prime. Other forms of Mutation are rare in Graylings and often fatal.
  Most mutation research is focused on figuring out how to artificially
  unlock and enhance Psionic powers. Mutants are often shunned from
  society and seen as biological failures.

### (Sub-Species) Grayling Psion {#sub_species_grayling_psion}

Sometimes referred to as the \'Whisperers\', these Graylings are rare,
and the Grayling government tries to control and conceal these citizens,
especially from foreign awareness. They use the \'Whisperers\' in
diplomatic envoys and as ambassador assistants. They are also used
within the Graylings\' own law enforcement as a secret police division
made up of Psionics and mutants. Some Graylings who are less trusting of
their government may choose to conceal their abilities. It is believed
that Whisperers are prized and well compensated for their services,
loyalty, and discretion.

`<u>`{=html}**Visual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: They usually look more sickly or have larger heads or limbs than a
  normal Grayling. However, these changes are subtle to an outside
  observer. A Grayling should notice immediately that this person is
  not\... normal.

`<u>`{=html}**Special Specie Traits**`</u>`{=html}

1.  Lose two in Strength, gain two in Intelligence.
2.  Lose one in Dexterity, choose between Intelligence, Wisdom, or

3.  Choose any one Knowledge skill and add a +1 Misc bonus to it.
4.  Start off at level one as a Psion following the

## Lepidonains

A mischievous species. The Lepidonains have long held the belief that
they hold the key to a utopian society, and that it is their job to
bring peace, stability, and civilization to the obviously weaker
species. This pompous and ignorant attitude toward others isn\'t common
among species of the Ring of Life, but, if it exists at all, it usually
fades as the young space-faring civilization begins to interact with
others. However, for the Lepidonains, this is not the case. Perhaps it
is the more hermit-like way of shutting themselves out from the rest of
the galaxy that has led to the unnaturally long-held beliefs. In either
case, in recent years, this attitude from their government at least
appears to have changed.

One of Lepidonains first great conflicts with a genuinely equal species
was with the Cryous. This war broke both nations. However, over time,
Lepidonains have rebuilt. Now they are looking to expand, but by more
peaceful means. Or so it appears. They have become more friendly with
trade and with the other major Galactic Civilizations. However,
Lepidonains have a conniving side, and many people in the past have been
burned by the deals they make with Lepidonains.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: Lepidonains are reptilian and come from Lepidon Prime, which is a
  mostly desert planet. They are accustomed to the heat and a large sun,
  making long, hot days. They have small horns protruding from their
  scaled skin, especially at the joints. Their skin is a mix of dark
  grey and green. Their necks are broad, and from behind, they look like
  all one part of the back, neck, and head. Most have a stubby tail.
  Some from other planets have pale scales and are shorter.

`<u>`{=html}**Species Traits**`</u>`{=html}

- Class: Reptilian
- Locomotion: bipedal (Base Speed 15ft)
- Reproduction: sexual
- Average Height Male: 6\' 2*(\~189 cm), Female: 4\' 6* (\~137 cm)
- Average Life Span: 110 - 130 years
- Size: Medium
- Main Home Worlds: Lepidon Prime, Lepidon Beta, Lepidon Gamma, Abrah
  Alpha
- [Tech Level](Tech_Level_and_Civ_Progress "Tech Level"){.wikilink}:
  starts out as 2

`<u>`{=html}**Special Specie Traits**`</u>`{=html}

1.  **Claws**: The reptilian claw-like hands can be used for a dual claw
    melee attack. At level one, they are dual attacks at 1d4 with a
    critical of x2. At level 5, they go up to 1d6. At level 10, they go
    up to 1d8 and have a crit range of 19-20. At level 15, they go up to
    1d10. At level 20, they go up to 1d12, and critical is now x3. They
    are considered Natural weapons, which are always considered to be
    light weapons when dealing with Extra Damage/Bonus Attacks.
2.  [Strength](Damage_Types_and_Attributes#Strength "Strength"){.wikilink}
    against Thermal (Heat damage)
3.  [Vulnerable](Damage_Types_and_Attributes#Vulnerable "Vulnerable"){.wikilink}
    against Thermal (Cold damage)
4.  Lose one Dexterity, but gain 2 points to spend between Strength,
    Constitution, or Intelligence.
5.  They gain a +1 Misc bonus to either Bluff or Intimidate skill.

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html}

: The Lepidonains view themselves as a rising star in the void of the
  galaxy. The only thing hindering their rise is the Cryous. Thus,
  Lepidonains have long held a grudge against their rival. Humans are
  also on the board of Lepidonain space, and Lepidonains see them as an
  opportunity to spread their way of life. They are private people who
  often exhibit xenophobic tendencies, although most of that is
  motivated by a sense of self-superiority and a belief that, since they
  believe they have the right to rule the galaxy, so must all other
  sentient species.

`<u>`{=html}**Opinions on Difficulties**`</u>`{=html}

: Lepidonain\'s view on the
  [Difficulties](Difficulties#Advanced_Difficulties "Difficulties"){.wikilink}
  is very straightforward. They believe in the purity of the species,
  and anything unusual or \'other\' is deemed wrong. It is considered
  morally right to shun and even insult those who appear, act, and think
  differently. Although Medical and Utility Cybernetics are common
  within Lepidonain and even a mark of pride from some who may have lost
  a limb in the service of the Empire, the same cannot be said for
  Enhancement-type Cybernetics. Implants that enhance the body are seen
  as, \'the easy way out\' or as a failure of character and strength. It
  is completely illegal and only done as part of the underground or in
  secret military operations. Mutations and their cousin Psionics are
  also viewed with equal disgust. Mutations are seen as a failure of
  genetics. While some will blame nature, many Lepidonains will view it
  as some sign of failure on the part of the parents. Many parents would
  rather abort the baby than have a mutant, even if the mutation is
  something harmless. The official stand of the Empire of Lepdonia is
  that Psionics are a hoax and that those with it have been enhanced in
  some other way, usually through Cybernetics. The government actively
  hunts all Psionics and mutants in their controlled territory.

## Cryous

An amphibious species, the Cryous are part of a once-thriving empire.
They were challenged by the Lepidonains. The war left both worse for
wear. Most people think what kept the Cryous from total defeat was their
tenacity to survive under all sorts of unspeakable conditions. If the
surface is in trouble, then go underwater. The Cryous were not just good
at escaping death, they were also good at delivering it. The Cryous have
a 6th sense for the electromagnetic spectrum and can even use their body
to create an electromagnetic shock wave. This is something their species
evolved to do eons ago. They used this to their advantage in combat and
are known for creating the
[Electro-Mancer](Character_Professions#Electro-Mancer "Electro-Mancer"){.wikilink}
suit.

Cryous no longer holds an empire and now rules only a dozen solar
systems. Their enemy, the Lepidonains, have recovered much better from
their great conflict. Ever fearful that, after so many years, the
Lepidonains will return to finish them off, the Cryous government has
become more cautious, territorial, and even aggressive. Hoping not to
show signs of weakness.

## Ovex

The Ovex, or Ov as some groups call them, are sentient artificial life
forms. AI is illegal across the galaxy except for the Jove. Ovex is part
of the reason. Ovex can vary greatly in size and, by default, look
similar to the humanoid bipedal who originally built them. They can be
built to shape other species and even have a special outer layer
\'skin\' that makes the appearance of flesh.

Ovex are known and feared across the galaxy. Long ago, they rose up
against their creators and killed them. Then, believing it necessary,
waged a war against the biological dizedins of the Ring. However, this
was a grave mistake. The Ovex lost this war and was pushed to the brink
of annihilation. Only in faking their destruction did the war end. Faced
with death for the first time and having learned a hard lesson about
their own failings, the Core of the Ovex, the Nexus, became determined
to avoid it. To instill in all copies of its intelligence, and to ensure
it would always survive even its own destruction, it built copies of
itself and, as part of those copies, learning experience, growth, and
maturity; these copies would travel about the known galaxy disguised as
common sentient species. Here, the Ovex would experience life and death
and bring back those experiences, along with news and current events,
into the great Nexus. Each experience would be a unique perspective into
the great mind.

While eventually people of the galaxy learned that the Ovex were not
truly destroyed, they also do not know where the \'heart\' of the Ovex,
the Nexus, resides. Because of this, Ovex usually travels in disguise
and will do anything, including faking death, to maintain appearances
and avoid discovery. Ovex can instantly become aware of another Ovex
nearby, so they cannot hide from themselves.

For a complete history and narrative about Ovex in The Great Ring

## Aconians

The average Aconian is a short, bulky individual who lives much longer
than most mammals. Their age gives them a unique view of history and the
changing times. Most any Aconian will love to talk about history,

someone willing to listen. Many Aconians, especially the older ones,
come off as arrogant. However, they are usually slow to anger and
willing to listen to other species if they have something interesting to
say.

The Aconians are among the oldest extant species. Their ancient library
contains tombs dating back more than 1 million years, from the last part
of the Dark Forest Era. They survived the Great Collapse and even
thrived for a time, reaching their peak by ruling nearly 12% of the
known Ring. But this game ended during the rise of the Twin Empires.
They have gone through 3 significant Dynasties, in which their power
rose and fell, only to rise again. However, recently, their government
gave up its Empire to form a Republic. This, in many ways, has changed
the power balance of the region of space.

## Khepri

The Khepri are an ancient insectoid species operating under a strict
matriarchy ruled by Queen-Mothers. What sets the Khepri apart from most
other species is their extreme anatomical caste system. Upon reaching
maturity, a Khepri\'s biological development is dictated by chemical
signals and diet within the hive, shaping them into physically distinct
castes suited for specific societal roles.

While the Queen-Mothers lead the great hive-cities from their home
worlds, lower castes travel the galaxy extensively as engineers,
mercenaries, scholars, and diplomats. Known for their incredible
bio-architecture, asteroid-mining megastructures, and hyper-coordinated
labor, the Khepri are a respected Major Species found in almost every
sector of the Great Ring.

For a complete history and narrative about the Khepri, go to the
[Khepri](Khepri "Khepri"){.wikilink} page.

  mandibles, and multiple jointed limbs. Their exact appearance, height,

    : Gain +1 to Intelligence or Wisdom, -1 to Strength.
    : Gain any one Knowledge skill as a Favored Skill and a +1 Misc
      bonus to it.
    : Gain Stragedy from the [Smart Hero
      Path](Character_Path#Path_of_Intelligence_(Smart_Hero) "Smart Hero Path"){.wikilink}.
      If this is already gained, then increase the number of times per
      combat by +1.
5.  **Administrator (Leader)**

    : Gain +1 to Charisma or Wisdom, -1 to Constitution.
    : Gain +1 Misc bonus to Diplomacy or Intimidate checks.
    : Gain the [Leadership](Leadership "Leadership"){.wikilink} feat. In
      the future, if another ability grants this feat, then pick another
      feat with the \'Sub-Category: Leadership & Companions\' section.

`<u>`{=html}**Relations**`</u>`{=html}

: Most species find dealing with the Khepri awkward due to their
  insectoid nature and rigid hive social structure. The
  [Volar](Species#Volar "Volar"){.wikilink} are the notable exception,
  enjoying Khepri company and forming close ties; Khepri likewise find
  the Volar easy to work alongside. The Khepri are master bio-engineers
  and major food suppliers across the Great Ring---their agricultural
  and food-generating technology is second only to the Volar, while
  their terraforming expertise is second only to the
  [Graylings](Species#Graelycxazshngs_.28aka_Graylings.29 "Graylings"){.wikilink}.
  While Khepri feel they have much to prove on the galactic stage,
  individual Khepri rarely care unless commanded to by their
  Queen-Mother. In truth, the greatest conflicts facing the Khepri are
  internal: a planet can have only one ruling Queen, and no Queen will
  tolerate a rival. Because local planetary environments dramatically
  shape their biological development, colonies on different worlds can
  appear physically distinct, leading rival Queens to view each other as
  their true primary competition in the galaxy.

`<u>`{=html}**Opinions on Difficulties**`</u>`{=html}

: Khepri policy on Cybernetics is strictly hostile. Implants are viewed
  as a grave insult to the Queen-Mother who birthed and biologically
  shaped their bodies for their designated role. Natural Mutations are
  treated as dangerous genetic errors; if detected early, hive
  bio-engineers attempt to adjust or remove the mutation. If these
  efforts fail, the mutated individual is cold-heartedly culled from the
  colony to preserve genetic purity. Psionics, on the other hand, are
  exceptionally rare and held in mystical awe. Any Khepri displaying
  psionic abilities is classified into a special, mostly defunct
  religious caste known as the \"Touched by Other,\" often serving as
  sacred ascetics or high advisors within the hive.
