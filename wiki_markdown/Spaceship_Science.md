**Prerequisites**: Study 2 Ranks

**Benefits**: You can add your affinity die to rolls for multi science
related skills while on board your Spaceship. The list of skills are as
follows: Computer Use, Decipher Script, Disable Device, Knowledge
\[Behavioral Sciences, Life Sciences, Physical Sciences, Technology\],
Study, Tech.

**Normal**: Normally a Character cannot add there affinity die to any
skill check rolls regardless if they are on a ship.
