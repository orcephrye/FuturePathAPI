**Prerequisites**: Simple Proficiency, Advanced Proficiency

**Benefits**: The Character doesn\'t take disadvantage when using heavy
melee weapons. Heavy Melee weapons are any weapon that is between level
7 and 8.

**Normal**: Normally a character cannot use a melee weapon that he/her
is not proficient in without taking a disadvantage. The rules for weapon
levels is located
[here](Weapons#Future_Path_Custom_Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.
