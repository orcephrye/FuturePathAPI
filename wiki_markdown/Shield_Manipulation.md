Adjust the Shield Frequency to match their lasers!

## Description

This is an action any crew member with access to a console can
technically do. However, the [Science
Officer](Science_Officer "Science Officer"){.wikilink} may be best
equipped to do this, as it requires the Character to have [Knowledge
Science](Knowledge#Science "Knowledge Science"){.wikilink}.

This roll opposes an enemy\'s Scan action. If you meet or beat the
opposing roll, the following effects can happen.

- This can block **Gather Information**
- This can increase the DC for a Hacker to stay connected to the hack by
  1 +1 for every 4 the opposing roll is beaten, max +5.
- The Sensor roll for Scan Weakness still succeeds, but the bonus given
  to the Enemy\'s Battle Pool is reduced by 1 +1 for every 4 the
  opposing roll is beaten. This cannot go negative.

If the enemy doesn\'t attempt a Scan, then the DC is equal to 8 + Number
of Active Hard Points aimed at your Ship + Weapon\'s Officer\'s Dex or
Wis Modifier (whichever they are using). If successful, the Player who
did the Manipulation may choose to cancel one d10 from the Battle Pool
before re-rolls are done. For every 4 the DC is beaten, choose another
d10 to cancel.
