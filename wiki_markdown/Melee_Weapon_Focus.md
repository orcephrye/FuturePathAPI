**Prerequisite**: -

**Benefit**: Provides an advantage bonus for the use of a selected Melee
Weapon or Weapon Style. This stacks on top of any other weapon bonuses.
This only works for melee \*weapons\*, not hand-to-hand combat.

**Normal**: Normally, characters only get an advantage for weapon
proficiency. This provides a second advantage.

**Notes**: The weapon selection is based on the Weapon Style. Any other
weapon is the same Weapon Style the Feat applies to. If the weapon has
no Style, it only applies to that exact weapon. (The GM may give
exceptions if the Weapon is supposed to closely resemble other weapons
or a Style, but simply doesn\'t have one)
