This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons go
[here](Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.

This can provide a +1 Accuracy bonus only for weapons with large or
bigger ranges, same as a [Standard
Scope](Standard_Scope "Standard Scope"){.wikilink}. However, this can
overlook the disadvantages of poor lighting.

Benefit
: +1 Accuracy bonus to Ranged Weapons only. This only works on targets
  that exceed the initial range increment. Ignores penalties for poor
  lighting.
