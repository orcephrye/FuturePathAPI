Advancements in science coupled with your drive to excel enable you to
progress faster toward becoming a truly charismatic hero.

**Prerequisites**: Your Character must have an Affinity with the
Charismatic ability this Affinity can be gained through an Advance Class
meaning that the character doesn\'t have to have the Charismatic Hero
Base Class.

**Benefit**: You gain one talent from the Charismatic hero's talent
trees.

**Special**: You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you
select this feat, you must choose a different talent.
