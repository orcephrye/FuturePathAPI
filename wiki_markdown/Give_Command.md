## Description

The Captain gives a directive and attempts to assist a crew member in
following that directive. A Leadership/Charisma check DC10. This
provides a +1 to the target Officer with an additional +1 for every 4
the DC is beaten. For the Weapons officer, provide one of the following,
but not both.

- 1+1 (per DC beaten) Points to be distributed to the d10s to determine
  success.
- 1+1 (per every 3 DC beaten) re-rolls (acts like a boost to Weapons
  Modifier)
