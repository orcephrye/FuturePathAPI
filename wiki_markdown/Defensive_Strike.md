**Prerequisite**: Melee Weapon Focus

**Benefit**: Do not add your strength modifier to damage, and reduce the
damage die level by 2 (min 1, ie 1d2). However, the Character gains +4
AC for one round on any attack from the target, regardless of success.
The AC goes up to +5 at Character level 10 and +6 at Character level 20.
This does not count as a Standard Attack Action but a Standard
Technique/Talent/Feat Action. Thus, by default, it doesn\'t grant the
Character a Bonus Attack.

**Normal**: Normally, an attack has no effect on AC, only heals or
conditions.

**Special**: -
