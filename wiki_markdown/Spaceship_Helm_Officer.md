**Prerequisites**: [Pilot](Pilot "Pilot"){.wikilink} 2 Ranks

**Benefits**: You can add your advantage die to Pilot checks if you are
at the helm. You can also use [Advanced Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Advanced Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character cannot add their advantage die to
Pilot checks while at the helm. And you can only use [Standard Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Standard Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Special**: -
