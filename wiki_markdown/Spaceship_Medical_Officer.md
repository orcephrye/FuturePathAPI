**Prerequisites**: [Medical](Medical "Medical"){.wikilink} 2 ranks

**Benefits**: You can add your affinity die to rolls for Medical while
on board your Spaceship, regardless of being in combat or the condition
of the ship. They can also use [Advanced Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Advanced Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character cannot add their affinity die to rolls
for Medical unless an Advanced Class specifies otherwise. However, even
in those situations, combat conditions aboard the ship can create
disadvantages. They can only use [Standard Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Standard Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Special**: -
