You are a Mutant that is more powerful than normal. Learn more about
Mutations go [here](Mutations "here"){.wikilink}.

**Prerequisites**: Character must already be a
[Mutant](Mutations "Mutant"){.wikilink} or
[Psion](Psionics "Psion"){.wikilink}.

**Benefit**: This grants 1MP at level 1 and one more MP at every 5th
level, ie: 5,10,15,20 for a total of 5 more MP.

**Normal**: Without this feat, the only way to gain MP to spend is with
a [Drawback](Mutation_Drawbacks "Drawback"){.wikilink}.

**Special**: -
