**Prerequisite**: -

**Benefit**: Immediately gain a level in any Proficiency Tree currently
available. This includes levels that require spending two points.
(Cannot be used to unlock the Power Armor Tree.)

**Normal**: Normally, a Character only gains a Proficiency point to
spend on the Proficiency Tree by leveling up. Every level, the player
gains a point.

**Special**: This Feat can be taken more than once.
