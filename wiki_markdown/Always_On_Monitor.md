This is a Power Armor Mod. Please review the [Power
Armor](Armor#Power_Armor "Power Armor"){.wikilink} rules for more
information.

This provides a +4 to Passive Perception and normal Perception checks.
This also makes the Wearer immune to certain types of stealth as this
enhancement uses Rader/Lader/Sonar/Micro-wave detection systems. However
most ships or other Power Armor Stealth systems also jam those things.
