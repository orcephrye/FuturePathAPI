Contagious

- This means that whatever is effected by the
  [Charge](Weapons#The_Charge "Charge"){.wikilink} can carry the effect
  onto other objects. If a distance is not specified then it is by touch
  only. The Charge is Contagious for at least 1 turn or as many turns as
  it [Lingers](Lingers "Lingers"){.wikilink} if it
  [Lingers](Lingers "Lingers"){.wikilink}.
