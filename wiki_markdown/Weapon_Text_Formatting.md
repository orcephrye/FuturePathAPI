The Weapon\'s Text is formatted like:

: Accuracy Bonus + Weapon Damage Die \[Damage Type\] + Extra Damage Die
  \[Damage Type\] + Bonus Damage + (AP Bonus) Critical Range/Critical
  Multiplier \-- Ammo (X), Bonus Effects.

Examples:

: [Pistol](Pistol "Pistol"){.wikilink} 1d6 20/x2 \-- Ammo(7), Versatile

  : **Explanation**: Any Bonuses that do not exist are simply not
    specified. So this weapon has no accuracy bonus or Bonus Damage or
    AP, so it is not mentioned. Also, the DR isn\'t mentioned because
    the default is Kinetic.
: [M16](Assault_Weapon#M16_Assault_Weapon "M16"){.wikilink} +1 1d10
  19-20/x2 \-- Ammo(6), Switching Fire Mods

  : **Explanation**: Shows what the Accuracy Bonus and the change to the
    Critical Range are.
: [Tactical Plasma
  Rifle](Plasma_Rifle#Tactical_Plasma_Rifle "Tactical Plasma Rifle"){.wikilink}
  +1 2d8 \[T\] + 1 (AP 1) 20/x2 \-- Ammo(5), Switch Firing Modes, Fully
  Unload

  : **Explanation**: This shows the Damage Type change from the default
    Kinetic to Thermal. This also shows how multiple attributes are
    comma-delimited.
