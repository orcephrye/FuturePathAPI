The Science Officer is a [Ship
Position](Space_Ships#Ship_Positions "Ship Position"){.wikilink}. There
are other ship positions that can be reviewed and can affect this
position.

This is considered a secondary ship position.

## Description

The Science Officer normally assists the ship\'s crew in discovering new
life forms, studying astronomical phenomena, or charting new star
systems. They can perform a lot of white-collar and civilian-like duties
onboard a spaceship. However, having a highly trained expert can also be
useful in combat. Many scientists can use their expertise to do cyber
warfare. They can also help decipher crypto messages.

: **Associated Ship Attribute**: Primary Electronics and Secondary
  Sensors
: **Officer Functions**:
  1.  `<u>`{=html}[Boost](Boost "Boost"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}: This
      provides a modifier bonus to one of the ship\'s Attributes,
      excluding Structure. It uses ship Electronics. It converts the
      Ship\'s Electronic Modifier over to either
      Engines/Weapons/Shields/Sensors. If the Electronics Mod is 0, then
      it cannot provide any more boost. This also takes away the
      Electronics Mod for that round. The DC is 10. If successful,
      Electronics loses 1 modifier, and it is instead given to a
      different ship system. For every 3, the Science Office beats the
      10, another pint is taken from Electronics and given to the
      Attribute being boosted. Only one attribute can be boosted per
      turn. Afterward, a cool-down of 1 turn where that Attribute cannot
      be boosted.
  2.  `<u>`{=html}[Hack](Hack "Hack"){.wikilink}/[Counter
      Hack](Hack#Counter_Hacking "Counter Hack"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}:
      Hacking uses the ship\'s computers and Communications systems
      (Electronics and Sensors) to attempt to gain access to an enemy
      ship's systems. Once inside, the Hacker can gather information,
      disrupt systems, and even deal damage. To learn more, visit the
      [Hack](Hack "Hack"){.wikilink} page.
: **Advanced Officer Functions**:
  1.  `<u>`{=html}[Cloak](Cloak "Cloak"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}: This
      ability allows the ship to increase its Detection DC, hopefully
      making it not detectable by other enemy ships without a Scan. If
      this is the case, the ship can only be targeted by weapons on a
      successful scan check.
  2.  `<u>`{=html}[Crypto](Crypto "Crypto"){.wikilink}`</u>`{=html}:
      This ability allows the ship to encrypt information so that it is
      more difficult for a hacker to get information. So that
      communications between ships cannot be seen, read, or even
      detected. This can also be used to attempt to decrypt enemy
      ships\' information or communications. This can also be used to
      secure communication with Drones so that they are not jammed by
      the enemy.
