This page is a starting place for all the rules of the game. If you are
new to Pen and Paper and roleplaying styles of games, you may wish to
review the [New to P&P](New_too_P%26P "New to P&P"){.wikilink} page.

Are you an experienced player? The [Whats
Different](Whats_Different "Whats Different"){.wikilink} page is a good
place to learn about what makes this system **D20+** so different from
normal D20, as well as other things that stand out about this
ruleset/game.

If you are a **Player** (Meaning you do not intend to run the game or be
the storyteller), then it is recommended that you either [make a new
Character](Character_Creation_and_Advancement "make a new Character"){.wikilink}
or pick a [Starter
Character](Starter_Characters "Starter Character"){.wikilink}. We
provide a Character Sheet
[here](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1cZ3PPchkXAgTSWTHKsONEIWNrg8PhN2P)!

After making a Character, you may want to review concepts like
[Combat](Combat "Combat"){.wikilink} and
[Skills](SkillsBasics "Skills"){.wikilink}.

If you intend to run a game as the **Game Master** or **GM** (that means
that you are the storyteller and manage the game for the players), then
it is recommended that you familiarize yourself with all the pages under
the \'Rules\' category. A GM should also be familiar with all the
different [Skills](Skills "Skills"){.wikilink} and how they are used.
The [GMs Tools](GMs_Tools "GMs Tools"){.wikilink} page is full of
helpful resources and guides. You may then want to look at the pages
under the \'Campaign Setting\' section to learn about the lore and
cosmology of the FuturePath Universe.

As you read on, you may find sections that are in boxes like the one
below. These are usually used to explain something outside of the rules.
For example, we would record something on a physical Character Sheet. Or
perhaps a snippet of \'flavor text.\'

    Anything in this box-like format will be instructions for editing the Character sheet.
