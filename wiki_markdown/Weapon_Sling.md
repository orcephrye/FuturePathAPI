This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons, go
[here](Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.

A Weapon Sling is used to carry larger firearms (Medium or Large size)
on a person\'s shoulder or back. It acts as a single Quick Access Slot
that can hold a Medium or Large, Two-Handed weapon. This allows the
weapon to be \'Drawn\' as a Simple Move Action rather than a Standard
Move Action.

Benefit
: 1 Quick Access Slot that can hold a Medium or Large weapon. A
  Character can only have one sling active at a time.
