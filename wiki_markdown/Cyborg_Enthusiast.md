You are progressing and improving your Cyborg self. You can add/upgrade
Cybernetic implants. You are now a Level 2 Cyborg.

**Prerequisites**: The Character must be a Level 1
[Cyborg](Cybernetics#Cybernetic_Levels "Cyborg"){.wikilink}.

**Benefit**: Learn more about Cybernetics
[here](Cybernetics "here"){.wikilink}. This will allow the Character to
gain nonbiological enhancements in the form of Implanets. The total
number of Power and Slots available to the Character is now increased as
a Level 2 Cyborg.

**Normal**: Without this feat, a Character must either take a new
Detractor from the
[list](Difficulties#Detractors_List "list"){.wikilink} or enhance an
existing Detractor in order to level up their Cyborg frame.
