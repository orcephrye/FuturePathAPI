**Prerequisite**: Melee Weapon Focus, Advantage Die 1d8.

**Benefit**: If the Character gets off a successful attack, the target
has to perform a saving throw or be tripped. The DC is 12 + Masterworked
Mod (0 - 5). If the attack is critical, the DC is 20 + Masterworked Mod.
The Player must declare that the attack will be a Sweeping Blow, and it
can be attempted only once per turn. This doesn\'t work for Bonus
Attacks.

**Note**: This can only be applied to a Melee Weapon the Character has
\'Melee Weapon Focus\' for.

**Normal**: Normally, weapons do not automatically cause trips.
