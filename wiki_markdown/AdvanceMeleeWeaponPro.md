**Prerequisites**: Melee Weapon, Simple Proficiency

**Benefits**: The Character doesn\'t take disadvantage when using
advanced melee weapons. Advanced Melee weapons are any weapon that is
between level 4 and 6.

**Normal**: Normally a character cannot use a melee weapon that he/her
is not proficient in without taking a disadvantage. The rules for weapon
levels is located
[here](Weapons#Future_Path_Custom_Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.
