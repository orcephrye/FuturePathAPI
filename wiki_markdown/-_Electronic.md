This skill allows a character to build electronic equipment from
scratch, such as audio and video equipment, timers and listening
devices, or radios and communication devices.

The main categories from the [General
Equipment](General_Equipment "General Equipment"){.wikilink} page that
require Electronics are:

- [Computers and Consumer
  Electronics](General_Equipment#Computers_and_Consumer_Electronics "Computers and Consumer Electronics"){.wikilink}
- [Surveillance
  Gear](General_Equipment#Surveillance_Gear "Surveillance Gear"){.wikilink}
- [Sensor
  Equipment](General_Equipment#Sensor_Equipment "Sensor Equipment"){.wikilink}
- [Miscellaneous
  Equipment](General_Equipment#Miscellaneous_Equipment "Miscellaneous Equipment"){.wikilink}
  (Some things)
- [Detonator\'s for
  explosives](Weapons#The_Detonator "Detonator's for explosives"){.wikilink}
- Space ship\'s and some Space Ship upgrades

Identify: If a Character wants to attempt to identify the Electronic
item, they can follow the rules for identifying technology using their
Tech rating.

An Electrical Tool Kit from the [Profession
Kits](General_Equipment#Profession_Kits "Profession Kits"){.wikilink}
page is necessary to craft Electronic items. An advanced kit provides an
Advantage when crafting or repairing.

Electronics follows the core rules for Crafting with no modifications
except for Identifying as noted above. More rules on crafting, costs,
and time can be found [here](Craft "here"){.wikilink}.
