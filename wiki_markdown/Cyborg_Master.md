You are progressing and improving your Cyborg self to its ultimate form.
You are at a delicate balance between biological beings and machines,
leaning more towards the mechanical side, and you would lose yourself.
You can add/upgrade Cybernetic implants.

**Prerequisites**: [Cyborg
Expert](Cyborg_Expert "Cyborg Expert"){.wikilink}

**Benefit**: Learn more about Cybernetics
[here](Cybernetics "here"){.wikilink}. This will allow the Character to
gain nonbiological enhancements. The total number of Power and Slots
available to the Character is now increased as a Level 5 Cyborg.

**Normal**: Without this feat, a Character must either take a new
Detractor from the
[list](Difficulties#Detractors_List "list"){.wikilink} or enhance an
existing Detractor in order to level up their Cyborg frame.
