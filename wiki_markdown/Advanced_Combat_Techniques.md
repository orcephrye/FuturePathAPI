Gain access to a [Advanced Combat
Technique](Techniques#Advanced_Combat_Techniques "Advanced Combat Technique"){.wikilink}.

**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefits**: Allows a player to pick a single Advanced Combat Technique
that they can now use at will.

**Normal**: Characters cannot normally use Advanced Combat Techniques
unless specified otherwise.

**Special**: This can be picked multiple times to learn multiple
Advanced Combat Techniques.
