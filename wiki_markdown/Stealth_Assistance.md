Stealth Assistance jams or makes reduces the effect of
Rader/Lader/Sonar/Micro-wave detection systems. It provides static DC
check of 15 when not trying to hide and when trying to be stealthy it
provides a static bonus to the Stealth check that is usually provided
next to its name.
