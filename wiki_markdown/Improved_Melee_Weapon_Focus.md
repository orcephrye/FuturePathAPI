**Prerequisite**: Melee Weapon Focus

**Benefit**: A character with this feat can more effectively use their
melee weapon of choice (The weapon chosen from the Melee Weapon Focus
Feat). The weapon damage die is now increased by one and gains +1 to its
bonus damage.

**Normal**: Weapon damage die is normally the \'source of truth\' when
it comes to how much damage a weapon can do. However, this feat
increases that amount.

**Special**: -
