## Campaign Background {#campaign_background}

Located in the Orion-Cygnus arm, Earth sat inside the **A.P.E.Z.**
(Jovian Advancing People\'s Exclusion Zone)---a protected buffer zone
established by the robotic **Jove** between the rival Cryous and
Lepidonan empires. Blocked by the zone, the two empires clashed
directly, sparking a rebellion that saw two subjugated species (the
[Peoples of Sayor](Sayor "Peoples of Sayor"){.wikilink} and the [Kurgian
Republic](Kurgian "Kurgian Republic"){.wikilink}) break away to form an
independent Federation, later legitimized by a peace treaty arbitrated
by the neutral
[Graylings](Species#Graelycxazshngs_.28aka_Graylings.29 "Graylings"){.wikilink}.

When humanity reached sub-light spaceflight (0.05--0.10c), the Jove
granted humanity ownership of the resource-rich, 250-light-year
territory, allowing alien interaction but strictly forbidding forced
conquest. Before any faction could convince humanity to ally or cede the
land, a devastating world war erupted on Earth---triggered in part by
the shock of alien contact and advanced technology. With no unified
human government left to sign treaties, the Jove refuse to recognize any
foreign claims, freezing out the desperate empires and leaving open the
question of whether humanity destroyed itself or was pushed.

## Modern Events {#modern_events}

Seventy years after Humanity\'s catastrophic System War 1 (WW3)---and a
century after the Federation\'s hard-won peace with the
Lepidonians---the border has plunged back into chaos. Accusations fly as
a mysterious mercenary outfit known as **The Claw** (or **Dark Claw**)
is implicated in recent crises, leaving galactic powers to question who
is pulling the strings.

Desperate to prevent total destabilization, the Federation has rushed
its cutting-edge Tech 3 flagship, the *FSS Vanguard* (or player-chosen
name), to the front lines before completing its scheduled shakedown
cruise. As the Federation\'s elite crew, your mission is to uncover the
truth behind The Claw and restore order to a volatile border on the
brink of war.

## From the Eyes of the Federation {#from_the_eyes_of_the_federation}

From the Federation\'s perspective, the Lepidonians are laying the
groundwork for war by manufacturing treaty violations. They have
formally accused the Federation of breaching their century-old peace
pact on three specific counts:

1.  Abandoning commitments to terraform **Tarienth**.
2.  Covertly manipulating Humanity toward Federation membership.
3.  Sabotaging the neutral trade hub of **Acbar**.

To validate these claims, the Lepidonians have summoned a Grayling Envoy
to inspect the border. The stakes are existential: if the Graylings rule
the treaty breached, they will revoke diplomatic protection, leaving the
Federation exposed to unprovoked Lepidonian aggression. With the border
currently in chaos, the Federation must restore order immediately before
the arriving Envoy mistakes unrest for guilt.

## Campaign Rules & Player Options {#campaign_rules_player_options}

- **Races:** All playable [Species](Species "Species"){.wikilink} except
  the Lepidonians (the primary antagonists). If you play as an
  [Ovex](Ovex "Ovex"){.wikilink}, you must disguise yourself as either a
  Human, Sayor, or Kurgian.
- **Classes:** All classes are available to play.
- **Alignment:** Characters serve in the Federation naval hierarchy, so
  **Chaotic alignments are prohibited**. If you play an evil character,
  you *must* be **Lawful Evil**. All other alignments are welcome.
- **Starting Character Level:** All Characters start at **Level 5**!

:\* **Special Note:** Players are encouraged to try being a
[Cybernetics](Cybernetic_Enhancements "Cybernetics"){.wikilink},
[Mutations](Mutations "Mutations"){.wikilink}, or
[Psionics](Psionics "Psionics"){.wikilink} (social penalties are waived
for this elite task force).

:\* **Bonus Feat:** All Characters receive one free Feat from the
**[Space Ship
Operations](Spaceship_Operations "Space Ship Operations"){.wikilink}**
subcategory!

:\* **Secondary Characters:** Players may create a second character to
explore different roles or tactical duties.

- **Available Bridge & Ship Positions:**

:# **Ship's Captain** *(Must have a Lawful alignment)*

:# **Second in Command (Commander / Captain\'s #1)**

:# **Chief Science Officer** *(Any scientific field of study)*

:# **Chief Medical Officer**

:# **Chief Engineer**

:# **Chief Tactical / Weapons Officer**

:# **Ranking Helmsman**

:# **Sensors and Comms Operator**

:# **Lt. Commander of Space Marine Squad Alpha** *(Default: \"Hell\'s
Hammer\")*

:# **Lt. Commander of Space Marine Squad Beta** *(Default:
\"Void-Walkers\")*

:\*(Unassigned positions are handled by competent NPCs whom players may
temporarily direct or control in encounters).\*

- **Optional Character Traits:**

:# *Old Comrade:* Longtime close friend and confidant of the Captain.

:# *Black Mark:* Troubled or controversial service record with the
Federation; this mission is redemption or ruin.

:# *Pulled from Retirement:* Veteran recalled to active service due to
specialized border experience.

:# *Lepidonian Scarred:* Survivor scarred by past trauma or atrocities
involving the Lepidonian Empire.

:# *Diplomatic Conscience:* Sworn pacifist assigned to the crew for
sensitive political or diplomatic oversight.

:# *Ties in the DMZ:* Romantically involved with or closely related to
someone recently reported missing in the Demilitarized Zone (near
Tarienth or Acbar).
