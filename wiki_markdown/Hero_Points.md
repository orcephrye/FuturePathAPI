Below are the rules for Hero Points. Hero Points are earned every time a
Character levels up and is also a handy alternative for the GM to
provide rewards for Player involvement instead of using extra experience
points. Hero Points can be spent by Players to help there Characters
excel at a particular action or objective. Some Species and Classes
require them for the use in special features or talents.

## Getting Hero Points {#getting_hero_points}

Normally a Character gets Hero points every time they level up. The
amount of Hero Points acquired is based on the [Base
Class](Future_Hero_Base_Class "Base Class"){.wikilink} that gains a
level. Characters keep previously unused Hero points. So they do not go
to waste unless the Character never uses them.

    Editing Character Sheet: There is currently not a location on the Character sheet to record how many Hero Points a Character has earned. This will hopefully change shortly. 

The GM can also give out Hero points to Player\'s who have done well or
was really impressive in some way. (Perhaps bought the GM pizza)

### Alternative Rules {#alternative_rules}

The game has Alternate rules for \"Low Fantasy\" and \"High Fantasy\"
settings.

Low Fantasy

:   

- Hero points earned are half what they are normally.
- Hero\'s are limited to only one point per day.

Extremely Low Fantasy

:   

- There are no Hero points. Talents that require hero points are either
  twice as hard to preform (DC checks are higher) or have 1/2 the
  effect.

High Fantasy

:   

- Hero\'s do not gain points every level. Instead they have a pool of
  points equal to how much they would have gotten at each level. This
  pool regenerates 1 hero point per 8 hours of rest the character has.

## Spending Hero Points {#spending_hero_points}

A Character can spend a Hero point to preform the following things:

- Improve the chances on a d20 roll used to make attack, a skill check,
  an ability check, a level check, or a saving throw.
- Use a class talent or class feature during your turn.
- Used in conjunction with certain [skill](Skills "skill"){.wikilink}
  features.

A Character cannot spend more the one Hero point per type of action. A
character cannot spend more action points then 1/2 their character level
rounded up between resting periods. The Character cannot spend more then
one point per round of combat either.

### Improve d20 roll {#improve_d20_roll}

To improve a roll the Player rolls a d20 twice and takes the higher one.
The Player cannot spend more points on the same d20. The Player must
declare they are going to use this action point before rolling.

### Class Talent {#class_talent}

When a Player declares that their Character will attempt to use a talent
that requires a Hero point they have to mark the point off the Character
sheet.

### Hero Points and Skills {#hero_points_and_skills}

Hero points can be used to roll a skill check twice. They can also be
used to provide a +4 bonus to take 10 and +8 bonus to take 20 on skill
checks that allow taking time instead of rolling.

Some skills have special features for use with Hero Points and are meant
to be used as a game mechanic for the Players to help them along when
they are seriously stuck. This should help alleviate some of the feeling
of a GM \'coddling\' or helping along the Players as the Players still
had to \'pay\' with something that they could of used elsewhere. For
example it could be used to help find the clue or secret passage when
they shouldn\'t of because the roll was too low. (But they need to in
order to continue the plot).
