The Galactic Guide is a collection of knowledge from different people
who explored The Great Ring, navigating around the dangers of the
galaxy. This can be considered a **Besteriary** or **Monster Manual** of
sorts. However, it also has knowledge about all sorts of people, cults,
and organizations that are capable of doing violence.

## The Galactic Guide to Monster, Mods and Masterminds {#the_galactic_guide_to_monster_mods_and_masterminds}

A guide to all the wondrous, but often dangerous, life a Traveler may
encounter while exploring the Great Ring of Life! Our galaxy is full of
life, and often it is dangerous. A prudent traveler should want to
prepare and learn from those who have gone before them.

### Monster

There are beautiful, awe-inspiring, and dangerous creatures that a
traveler may run into while exploring the Ring. These creatures are
often not truly sentient beings. Instead, they are more like animals or
other creatures. Some would even call them monsters.

### Mods

Many people, cultures, and cult groups themselves almost conventionally
as if they knew there were people who wanted to categorize them. Being
aware of who these people are, what they believe, and what they are
capable of is important if one wants to prepare when exploring the more
civilized parts of the Ring.

### Masterminds

These singular great beings of power or excessive resources make them
stand out. One should try to avoid such people unless that is the very
point of the traveler.
