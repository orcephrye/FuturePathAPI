These are descriptions of the explosives from the
[Weapons](Weapons "Weapons"){.wikilink} page. You can view the table
directly [here](Weapons#Grenades.2FExplosives_Table "here"){.wikilink}.

## Tech Level 0 Explosives {#tech_level_0_explosives}

### Greek Fire Grenade {#greek_fire_grenade}

A great military secret kept by the Byzantine Empire, Greek fire was not
only used by flame-throwers to protect their harbor from enemy ships,
but was also thrown as one of Earth\'s first effective uses of a
grenade-like object. Normally contained in jars of ceramic, stone, or
glass. This deadly weapon could ignite anything and keep burning even
underwater. If the dexterity save is successful, the target does not get
caught on fire. However, if the character fails, they take 1d4 burn
damage for 1d6 turns.

### Gun Powder Explosive {#gun_powder_explosive}

First used by the Chinese army likely between 960 and 1044 AD. Gunpowder
was packed into ceramic or sometimes even metal containers. Grenades
with metal shrapnel proved deadlier and thus dealt more damage.

## Tech Level 1 Explosives {#tech_level_1_explosives}

### Fragmentation Grenade {#fragmentation_grenade}

The most common military grenade, this is a small explosive device that
sprays shrapnel in all directions when it explodes.

### C4/Semtex

So-called "plastic" explosives resemble slabs of wax. Hard and
translucent when cold, these explosives warm up when kneaded and can
then be coaxed into various shapes. The information in the table
represents a 1-pound block. Additional blocks can be wired together,
increasing the damage and range increment; each additional block
increases the damage by +2d6, and for every two blocks, the range
increment by 1, and requires a Demolitions check (DC 15) to link them.
Although the damage statistics in the table are for a 1-pound block, C4
is sold in 4-block packages. The cost given represents a package of 4
blocks. C4/Semtex requires a detonator to set off. It is considered to
be a moderate explosive for the purpose of using a Craft (chemical)
check to manufacture it.

### Dynamite

Perhaps one of the most common and straightforward explosives, dynamite
is very stable under normal conditions. A stick of dynamite requires a
fuse or detonator to set it off. Additional sticks can be set off at the
same time if they are within the burst radius of the first stick,
increasing the damage and burst radius of the explosion. Each additional
stick increases the damage by +1d6 (maximum 10d6) and ever 4 sticks a
range increment. It's possible to wire together several sticks of
dynamite for even greater explosive effect. Doing so requires a
Demolitions check (DC 10 + 1 per stick). If the character succeeds on
the check, the damage or the burst radius of the explosion increases by
50% (the character's choice). Dynamite is sold in boxes of 12 sticks. It
is considered a simple explosive for the purpose of making a Craft
(chemical) check to manufacture it. To set off dynamite with a fuse, the
fuse must first be lit, which requires a move action (and a lighter or
other source of flame). The amount of time until the dynamite explodes
depends on the length of the fuse---a fuse can be cut short enough for
the dynamite to detonate in the same round (allowing it to be used much
like a grenade), or long enough to take several minutes to detonate.
Cutting the fuse to the appropriate length requires a move action.

### Molotov Cocktail {#molotov_cocktail}

A Molotov cocktail is a flask containing a flammable liquid, plugged
with a rag. A Molotov cocktail is easily made by hand (Craft
\[chemical\] check DC 10 or Intelligence Ability check DC 15). The price
given is for the components. To use it, the rag must first be lit,
requiring a move action (and a lighter or other source of flame). The
cocktail detonates in 2 rounds or on impact with a solid object,
whichever comes first. A target that takes a direct hit is dealt an
additional 1d6 points of fire damage in the following round and risks
catching on fire.

### Thermite Grenade {#thermite_grenade}

Thermite does not technically explode. Instead, it creates intense heat
intended to burn or melt through the object on which the grenade is set.
Military forces use thermite grenades to quickly destroy key pieces of
equipment.

### White Phosphorus Grenade {#white_phosphorus_grenade}

White phosphorus grenades use an explosive charge to distribute burning
phosphorus across the burst radius. Any target that takes damage from a
White Phosphorus grenade is dealt an additional 1d6 points of fire
damage in the following round and risks catching on fire.

In addition, a WP grenade creates a cloud of smoke. Treat a white
phosphorus grenade as a smoke grenade (see above), except that it only
fills squares within 5 feet of the explosion point.

### Smoke Grenade {#smoke_grenade}

Military and police forces use these weapons to create temporary
concealment. On the round when it is thrown, a smoke grenade fills 1
range increment with smoke. On the following round, it fills 2 range
increments 10ft radius, and on the third round it fills all squares
within 3(15ft) range increment. The smoke obscures all sight, including
the darkvision granted by night-vision goggles. Any creature within the
area has total concealment (attacks suffer a 50% miss chance, and the
attacker can't use sight to locate the target). It disperses after 10
rounds, though a moderate wind (11+ mph) disperses the smoke in 4 rounds
and a strong wind (21+ mph) disperses it in 1 round. Smoke grenades are
available in several colors, including white, red, yellow, green, and
purple. As such, they can serve as signaling devices.

### Tear Gas Grenade {#tear_gas_grenade}

Military and police forces use these weapons to disperse crowds and
smoke out hostage takers. On the round that it is thrown, a tear gas
grenade fills a 1(5ft) range increment with a cloud of irritant that
causes eyes to fill with tears. On the following round, it fills 2(10ft)
range increments, and on the third round, it fills a 3(15ft) range
increment. It disperses after 10 rounds, though a moderate wind (11+
mph) disperses the smoke in 4 rounds and a strong wind (21+ mph)
disperses it in 1 round.

A character caught in a cloud of tear gas must make a Constitution save
(DC 15) or be [Nauseated](Conditions#Nauseated "Nauseated"){.wikilink}.
This effect lasts as long as the character is in the cloud and for 1d4
rounds after he or she leaves the cloud. Those who succeed at their
saves but remain in the cloud must continue to save each round. A gas
mask renders the target immune to the effects. A wet cloth held over the
eyes, nose, and mouth provides a moderate (+2) advantage for the
Constitution save.

### Concussion Grenade {#concussion_grenade}

Military and police use these types of grenades similarly to tear gas in
their role. The goal is to clear a room of combatants before entering
it. Instead of disabling one or more of the combatants, the Concussion
Grenade attempts to render the target/s unconscious. Instead of a
Dexterity save, the target must roll as a Constitution save of DC15 + 1
for every Tech level of the grenade. If the target fails the check, he
is knocked out for (+2)1d4 turns.

## Tech Level 2 Explosives {#tech_level_2_explosives}

### EMP Explosive {#emp_explosive}

An EMP grenade releases an electromagnetic pulse that instantly shorts
out all electronic devices (including computers) within a 3(15ft) range
increment. Affected devices remain nonfunctional until repaired (see the
Repair skill description for details).

The EMP grenade deals no damage to living creatures. However, a creature
with cybernetic attachments takes 1d6 points of electricity damage per
cybernetic attachment (maximum 5d6) and must succeed on a Constitution
save (DC 15) or be stunned for 1d4 rounds. If the cybernetically
enhanced creature rolls a natural 1 on its Constitution save, each of
its cybernetic attachments takes 1d6 points of electricity damage as
well.

An EMP grenade deals 5d6 points of electricity damage to a mecha or
robot.

### Rad-Cloud Grenade {#rad_cloud_grenade}

This grenade releases a burst of ionizing radiation and chemical toxins.
Upon detonation, it fills a 2(10ft) radius with a sickly green cloud
that lingers for 1d4 rounds. Any living creature caught in the cloud
takes 3d6 points of chemical damage and must succeed on a Constitution
save (DC 15) or be [Sickened](Conditions#Sickened "Sickened"){.wikilink}
for 1d4 rounds.

### Static Charge Mine {#static_charge_mine}

The static charge mine is designed for perimeter defense against
electronic targets. It remains invisible to standard sensors (Electronic
Sense) and triggers upon detecting the electromagnetic signature of a
mecha, robot, or cybernetically enhanced character. It deals 4d6
electrical damage and has a 50% chance to temporarily disable one random
electronic system or cybernetic implant for 1d3 rounds.

### Fireflash Explosive {#fireflash_explosive}

A fireflush grenade contains a chemical compound that combusts
immediately upon contact with oxygen. When a fireflush grenade
detonates, it produces geysers of flame that fill a 2(10ft) burst
radius. All creatures and objects within the burst radius take 3d6
points of fire damage. The blast also ignites clothing and other
flammable items. A Dexterity save of D15+(1 for every extra Tech level)
will receive only 1/2 damage.

### Tangler Explosive {#tangler_explosive}

The tangler grenade operates on the same principle as the tangler gun,
but targets an area rather than one specific character. Any time a
character is struck by a tangler grenade, that character suffers a
staking moderate disadvantage (--2) penalty to Dexterity. This penalty
remains in place until the compound is dissolved.

If the character is struck with enough tangler grenades that the penalty
is equal to or greater than his Dexterity, he is immobilized and may
take no actions until the compound is dissolved. Dexterity penalties
from tangler grenades stack with those from tangler guns.

The hardened compound dissolves naturally after 8 hours or can be
removed using Solvaway or another spray solvent.

## Tech Level 3 Explosives {#tech_level_3_explosives}

### Dis-solver Explosive {#dis_solver_explosive}

This grenade releases a cloud of gas that dissolves organic matter,
dealing 2d6 points of acid damage per round to all living creatures and
organic materials in a 1(5ft) radius burst. Suspended in an artificial
gravity well, the cloud persists until the gravity well collapses after
30 seconds (10 rounds). The gravity well prevents even strong winds from
dispersing the acid cloud.

### Heavy Frag Grenade {#heavy_frag_grenade}

A high-yield version of the standard fragmentation grenade. It uses a
high-density HMXXX charge to produce a devastating burst of shrapnel. It
deals 6d10 kinetic damage in a 3(15ft) radius. Due to the weight of the
reinforced casing, it has a shorter throwing range than lighter
grenades.

### Napalm Walker {#napalm_walker}

This terrifying device is a mobile incendiary mine. Once deployed, its
\"Smart Sense\" detonator allows it to identify targets. It then uses
its internal \"Walker\" mechanism to move toward the nearest enemy
before detonating in a geyser of sticky, burning napalm. It deals 5d8
thermal damage, and the target takes an additional 1d6 damage for the
next 1d3 rounds as the napalm continues to burn.

### Gravimetric Explosive {#gravimetric_explosive}

When this grenade detonates, it releases incredible gravitational
forces. All creatures within a 2(10ft) radius burst are crushed for 6d6
points of bludgeoning damage. A successful Dexterity save (DC 15+(1 for
ever additional Tech Level) halves the damage. This weapon cannot be
converted into a cone-shaped blast unless constructed by a Character
with Tech Level 4 skills.

### Stunner

A stun grenade deals no damage, but any creature within its 3(15ft)
burst radius that fails a Dexterity save (DC 18) must immediately make a
Constitution save (DC 15) or be stunned for 1d4 rounds. A creature that
succeeds on the Dexterity save is not required to make a Constitution
save and is unaffected by the grenade. A stun grenade has no effect on
robots and other creatures immune to stunning effects.

## Tech Level 4 Explosives {#tech_level_4_explosives}

### Cryonic Explosive {#cryonic_explosive}

When a cryonic grenade explodes, it deals 8d6 points of cold damage to
all creatures in a 2(10ft) burst radius. A successful Dexterity save (DC
15) halves the damage. The freezing cold automatically stabilizes any
creature reduced to negative hit points by the blast. If the cryonic
blast deals sufficient damage to reduce a target to --10 or fewer hit
points, the target instead drops to --9 hit points and stabilizes
automatically (in other words, the target does not die).

### Psionic Wave Explosive {#psionic_wave_explosive}

A psionic grenade releases a 2(10ft) radius psionic blast that affects
only creatures with psionic abilities or powers. Any such creature that
fails a Dexterity save (DC 18) cannot use its psionic abilities or
psionic powers for (+1)1d4 rounds. In addition, it must succeed on a
Wisdom save (DC 15) or suffer 1d6 points of temporary Intelligence,
Wisdom, and Charisma damage.

### Quantum Nade {#quantum_nade}

Utilizing stabilized psionic resonators, the Quantum Nade creates a
localized ripple in the fabric of reality. Unlike the Psionic Wave
Explosive, which targets psionic users, the Quantum Nade\'s energy is so
intense it affects all sentient life. It deals 6d8 damage that ignores
all physical DR. Targets must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 18) or be
[Confused](Conditions#Confused "Confused"){.wikilink} for 1d4 rounds.

### Singularity Implosion Device {#singularity_implosion_device}

A singularity grenade generates a gravity field so powerful that it
bends space into a miniature black hole. The black hole instantly
devours all light and matter before collapsing and vanishing. Any
character or object in the grenade's 1(5ft) burst radius takes 15d6
points of damage. A Character cannot perform a Dexterity Save against
this explosion. If the damage is enough to reduce a creature to --10 hit
points, the grenade leaves no trace of the creature behind. The
explosive cannot be used or thrown as a grenade. This explosive\'s area
type cannot be a cone, only a circle.

### Ice-Nine Seeker {#ice_nine_seeker}

The Ice-Nine Seeker is a self-propelled cryonic grenade. It utilizes a
\"Contact 3\" chasing detonator to hunt down its target. Upon impact, it
releases a stabilized Ice 9 charge that instantly freezes everything in
a 1(5ft) radius. It deals 6d12 cold damage and is
[Contagious](Contagious "Contagious"){.wikilink}, meaning any creature
that touches a frozen target within 1d6 turns must also save or take
half damage from the residual cold.
