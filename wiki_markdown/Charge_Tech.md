Tech

- This means that the item can be incrementally improved upon in the
  next Tech level. Either by improving its damage to the next die level.
  Or by improving its range by doubling it. While others may improve
  another attribute such as increasing the Lingers. Or adding a new
  effect like Contagious. Some items have specific rules for how they
  can be improved upon.
