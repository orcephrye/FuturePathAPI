**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefits**: The Character doesn\'t take disadvantage for using
advanced firearms. Advanced firearms are weapons of level 4 to 6.

**Normal**: The Character has to take disadvantage for using any kind of
firearm or weapon that they are not proficient at. The rules for weapon
levels is located
[here](Weapons#Future_Path_Custom_Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.

**Special**: Almost all classes allow for advanced firearm proficiency.
This should be a default for all character\'s starting out at level 1.
