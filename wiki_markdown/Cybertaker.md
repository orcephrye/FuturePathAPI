The Character gain install a single Utility Implant without becoming a
full cyborg. If the Character is already a Cyborg and thus has a
Framework, they can either gain 1 Cybernetic Point or 1 Cybernetic Slot.
This doesn\'t have any prerequisites.

**Prerequisite**: -

**Benefit**: Gain a Power or Slot. Or be able to install a Utility
Implant without becoming a full Cyborg.

**NOTE**: The maximum amount of Points any Cyborg can have is 10, and
the Maximum number of Slots is 6. This cannot change that.

**Special**: You **CANNOT** take this Feat more then once.
