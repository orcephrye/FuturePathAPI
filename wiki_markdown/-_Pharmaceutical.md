This skill allows a character to create medicinal drugs to aid in
recovery from treatable illnesses, poisons, as well as other
Pharmaceuticals.

## Crafting Antidotes {#crafting_antidotes}

  Disease Fortitude Save DC   Crafting Cost   Craft DC   Time
  --------------------------- --------------- ---------- --------
  14 or lower                 \$25            15         1 hr.
  15--18                      \$100           20         3 hr.
  19--22                      \$400           25         6 hr.
  23 or higher                \$1200          30         12 hr.

  : Crafting Antidote

The Craft (Pharmaceutical) check is based on the severity of the disease
and/or Poison to be countered as measured by the DC of the Constitution
save needed to resist it.

The Character must first identify the illness or Poison inflicting the
person. They can use the rules from
[Medical](Medical "Medical"){.wikilink} to attempt to identify the
patient\'s problem. However, if the infection is chemical-based, then a
character can choose to follow the rules noted on the
[Chemical](-_Chemical "Chemical"){.wikilink}. Lastly, a Character can
also choose to use their Knowledge of the
[Sciences](Knowledge#Science "Sciences"){.wikilink} to attempt to
identify the inflection. The DC is 15 + Procure Diff. It takes 5 Minutes
to identify; however, only a Take 20 will auto succeed. Also, a
Pharmacist Kit found in [General
Equipment](General_Equipment "General Equipment"){.wikilink} can provide
an advantage bonus to Identify.

The Character can also craft these Pharmaceutical items, which can be
injected in advance to provide an ongoing defense against potential
exposure. A Cost and Craft DC increase is added for the extra chemicals
used to slowly release the compound over time so that its effect can
last. How long does a single dose last, based on the extra cost and DC?
Every 6 hours of bonus defense is a +\$25 and 1 Craft DC. However, this
item can only help defend a Character from one specific type of
disease/infection/poison.

## Crafting Pharmaceuticals (For HP) {#crafting_pharmaceuticals_for_hp}

A Player can find a list of possible items to construct in the [General
Equipment](General_Equipment "General Equipment"){.wikilink} page on the
[Medical
Items](General_Equipment#Medical_Items "Medical Items"){.wikilink}
section.

The Player needs a Pharmacist Kit located in the [Profession
Kits](General_Equipment#Profession_Kits "Profession Kits"){.wikilink}
section in order to craft any Antidote or Pharmaceutical item. An
advanced kit provides an advantage bonus.

More rules on crafting, costs, and time can be found
[here](Craft "here"){.wikilink}.

## Special

- A character with the [Medical
  Expert](Medical_Expert "Medical Expert"){.wikilink} feat gets to Misc
  +1 bonus to this Skill.
