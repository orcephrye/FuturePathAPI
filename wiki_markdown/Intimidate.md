You are a frightening presence, and you use it to get people to act in
ways that benefit you. Being Intimidating is more than just verbal
threats; it also can involve physical displays of prowess.

NOTE
: **Intimidate** can use both Charisma and Strength. When attempting to
  be intimidating, the Player can choose which of these 2 abilities to
  add the Modifier for. By default, a Character cannot add both Charisma
  and Strength modifiers to an Intimidate check.

## Checks

### Demoralize

Demoralizing an opponent is a standard action. You can use this skill to
cause an opponent to become shaken for a number of rounds. This shaken
condition doesn't stack with other shaken conditions to make an affected
creature frightened. The DC of this check is equal to 10 + the target's
Hit Dice + the target's Wisdom modifier.

`<u>`{=html}Success`</u>`{=html}: If you are successful, the target is
shaken for one round. This duration increases by 1 round for every 5 by
which you beat the DC. You can only threaten an opponent this way if it
is within 30 feet and can clearly see and hear you. Using demoralize on
the same creature only extends the duration; it does not create a
stronger fear condition.

`<u>`{=html}Fail`</u>`{=html}: The opponent is not shaken.

### Influence Attitude {#influence_attitude}

Using Intimidate to change an opponent's attitude requires 30 seconds of
conversation or, for a higher DC during combat, one Standard Use Item
Action. The DC of this check is equal to 10 + the target's Hit Dice +
the target's Wisdom modifier.

`<u>`{=html}Success`</u>`{=html}: If successful, the opponent will:

- give you the information you desire
- take actions that do not endanger it
- offer other limited assistance

After the intimidation expires, the target treats you as unfriendly and
may report you to local authorities.

`<u>`{=html}Fail`</u>`{=html}: If you fail this check by 5 or more, the
target attempts to deceive you or otherwise hinder your activities.

## Try Again {#try_again}

You can attempt to intimidate an opponent again, but each additional
check increases the DC by +5. This increase resets after one hour.

## Special

- **Feats**

1.  [Athletic Prowess](Athletic_Prowess "Athletic Prowess"){.wikilink}
    Feat allows your Athletic Skill to help boost your Intimidate skill.
    Take your Athletic Skill rank, divide it in half, round up, and
    apply that value as a Static Misc Bonus to your Intimidate skill.
2.  [Persuasive](Persuasive "Persuasive"){.wikilink} Feat provides a
    Misc +1 to both Diplomacy and Intimidate skills.
3.  [Intimidating
    Prowess](Intimidating_Prowess "Intimidating Prowess"){.wikilink}
    Feat allows you to add both your Strength and Charisma modifiers to
    Intimidate skill checks.
