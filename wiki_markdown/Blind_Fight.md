**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefit**: In combat, every time the character misses because of
concealment, the character can reroll the miss chance roll one time to
see if the character actually hits. This only counts for targets within
60 ft. The character takes only half the usual penalty to speed for
being unable to see.

**Normal**: Darkness and poor visibility in general reduce the
character's speed to three-quarters of normal, instead of one-half.
