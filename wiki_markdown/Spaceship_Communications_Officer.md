**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefits**: You can add your affinity die to Charisma-based rolls
while at the Communication/Sensor position. You can also use [Advanced
Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Advanced Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character cannot add their affinity die to
Charisma-based rolls while at the Communication/Sensor position. You can
only use [Standard Officer
Functions](Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions "Standard Officer Functions"){.wikilink}
for that position.
