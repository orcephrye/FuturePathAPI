**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefits**: The Character doesn\'t take disadvantage when using simple
melee weapons. Simple Melee weapons are any weapon that is between level
0 and 3.

**Normal**: Normally a character cannot use a melee weapon that he/her
is not proficient in without taking a disadvantage. The rules for weapon
levels is located
[here](Weapons#Future_Path_Custom_Weapons "here"){.wikilink}.

**Special**: This is granted for free to all Characters at level 1
unless specified otherwise.
