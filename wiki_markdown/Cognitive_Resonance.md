Your body has adapted more effectively to the heightened mental activity
that drives your Psion powers. Thus, you are able to enhance your
Psionic abilities more quickly.

**Prerequisites**: Character must already be a
[Psion](Psionics "Psion"){.wikilink}.

**Benefit**: The Character can advance to Level 2 Psion at Character
level 5+, Level 3 Psion at level 10+, Level 4 Psion at level 15+

**Normal**: Without this feat, a Character can only reach Level 2 Psion
at Character level 6+, Level 4 Psion at level 12+, and Level 4 Psion at
level 18+.

**Special**: -
