The Kurgian are one of the founding and primary members of the young
Federation. The Federation is a newly formed coalition of species that
border the Lipdonians and Cryous. It was formed as Sayor and Kurgian
rebels took advantage of a devastating war between Lipdonians and
Cryous. The Kurgian were subjects of Cryous before gaining their
freedom. Although not as mistreated as the Sayor, who were under their
Lipdonian suppressors. The Kurgian still didn\'t have equal rights and
were viewed by many within Cryous society as a lesser species.
Surprisingly, though, the relationship between Cryous and the Federation
isn\'t one filled with animosity like it is with Lipdonians. Instead,
the Cryous leadership chooses to recognize the rise of the Kurgian
civilization and sees the Kurgian success at gaining their freedom as a
sign that they deserve it. Or at least some see it that way. The subject
creates a great divide in Cryous politics. Many see the only reason the
Cryous Empire didn\'t punish the rebels was that the Kurgian, and thus
the Federation, formed a buffer zone between it and the Lepidonains.

The Kurgian see themselves as more than just a buffer, but as an old and
rich civilization thriving in its newfound freedom. Because the Cryous
didn\'t suppress their culture as much as the Lepidonains suppressed
Sayor\'s culture, the Kurgian have a lot of knowledge about their
history as a species and a people, and have found strength in
celebrating who they are. However, the Sayor often sees them as cocky
and finds flaunting history equally hurtful.

`<u>`{=html}**Visiual Description**`</u>`{=html}

: Kurgians have light green skin with multicolored spots. Most have
  brown and white spots, but different Kurgian may have red or even
  black spots as well. Kurgian eyes are always bright. The most common
  are Teal, Pink, Violet, and Golden. Kurgian do not have hair, but they
  do have scales that start at the top of their heads and go down the
  middle of the back and stop at the butt. Kurgian are not born with
  these scales, but they grow and darken in color as they mature into
  adults. By adulthood, these scales can be all sorts of different
  vibrant shades of green. As a Kurgian ages, its scales lose their
  pigment until they become almost transparent white.
