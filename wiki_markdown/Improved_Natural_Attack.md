Attacks made by one of this creature\'s natural attacks leave vicious
wounds.

**Prerequisite**: Natural Attack

**Benefit**: Choose one of the creature\'s natural attack forms. The
damage for this natural attack is increased by one Hit Die Level and
gains a +1 to Accuracy and +1 to damage.

**Special**: This Feat can be taken up to 5 times. However, the Hit Die
level of the Natural attack can only be boosted once. So the feat then
only boosts the Accuracy and damage. The max base accuracy and damage of
a natural attack is +5.
