**Prerequisite**: No Other Proficiency Training. IE, If you pick this,
you cannot also pick Heavy Melee or Heavy Ranged Weapon Training.

**Benefit**: Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 7 and higher for only
1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Armor Branch.

**Normal**: Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 7 or
higher.
