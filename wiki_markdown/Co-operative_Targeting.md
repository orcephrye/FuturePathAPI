Armor](Armor#Power_Armor "Power Armor"){.wikilink} rules for more
information.

This allows other people with this same Ability to know about your
available Targets and \'lock\' onto them even if they are not in visual
range. For example a Sniper can shoot someone through a Wall because
there was someone else on the inside informing the Sniper of the target.
This also provides an advantage bonus too accuracy for every member who
can also see the target and has Co-operative Targeting.
