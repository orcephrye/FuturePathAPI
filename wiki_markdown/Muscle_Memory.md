**Prerequisites**: Defensive Martial Arts, Affinity Die 1d10.

**Benefit**: In melee combat, if an opponent attacks and misses the
character, the character gains a Reaction Action of
[Trip](Techniques#Combat_Techniques "Trip"){.wikilink}. This is a choice
on behalf of the target character. If they choose to make the Trip
action, that must take up any Reaction Action they may have.
