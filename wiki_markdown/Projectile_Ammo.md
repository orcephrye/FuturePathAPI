This is a page dedicated to different custom Projectile Ammo for
Spaceships. To learn more about Space Ship Weapons and Equipment go
[here](Ship_Equipment "here"){.wikilink} and to learn more about Space
Ships themselves go [here](Space_Ships "here"){.wikilink}.

### Explosive Rounds {#explosive_rounds}

This grants the **Explode** attribute to the Projectile weapon 10s.

Cost
: This doubles the cost.

### Armor Piercing {#armor_piercing}

This allows the re-rolls of 1s to happen automatically and immediately,
and to count before re-rolls on the Weapon\'s mod.

Cost
: This doubles the cost.
