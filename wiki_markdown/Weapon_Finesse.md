**Prerequisites**: -

**Benefit**: With the selected melee weapon
[Style](Weapons#Weapon_Styles "Style"){.wikilink}, the Character may use
their Dexterity modifier instead of their Strength modifier on attack
rolls. The Character must be proficient with the weapon.

**Normal**: -

**Special**: A Character can gain this feat multiple times. Each time
the character takes the feat, they select a ­different weapon style.

**Notes**: The weapon selection is based on the Weapon Style. Any other
weapon is the same Weapon Style the Feat applies. If the weapon has no
Style then it only counts for that exact weapon. (The GM may give
exceptions if the Weapon is supposed to closely resemble other weapons
or a Style, but simply doesn\'t have one)
