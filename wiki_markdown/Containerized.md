Containerized

- This means that the casing can contain multiple different types of
  [Charges](Weapons#The_Charge "Charges"){.wikilink}. This means one of
  two things. If the weapon only has one
  [Detonator](Weapons#The_Detonator "Detonator"){.wikilink} then the
  effect of the weapon can be from multiple types of
  [Charges](Weapons#The_Charge "Charges"){.wikilink}. Or if the
  [Casing](Weapons#The_Casing "Casing"){.wikilink} has multiple
  [Detonators](Weapons#The_Detonator "Detonators"){.wikilink} then it
  can go off multiple types before being spent.
