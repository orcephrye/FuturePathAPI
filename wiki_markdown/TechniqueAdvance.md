**Prerequisites**: Technique, Novice

**Benefits**: A Character can sacrifice a higher-level technique to be
able to use another lower-level technique. This means that if the
Character can use a level 2 technique but wishes to use a level 1 even
though they have already used all their level 1 techniques, they can
sacrifice the use of the level 2 technique to gain the ability to re-use
a level 1. If the Character has a level 3 technique and wishes to use a
level 1 technique, they can sacrifice the level 3 for 2 level 1
techniques. They cannot be used at the same time. This continues for
every level, with a lower gain and an additional use.

**Normal**: Normally, you cannot trade off techniques. What you have
learned and the number of uses per level are static.

**Special**: Some
[Professions](Character_Professions "Professions"){.wikilink} allow
similar abilities. For example, look at the talents for the
[Eletro-Mancer](Character_Professions#Electro-Mancer "Eletro-Mancer"){.wikilink}.
