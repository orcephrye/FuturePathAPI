You can graft cybernetic attachments onto living tissue and safely
remove them.

**Prerequisites**: Medical 3 ranks, Surgery.

**Benefit**: You can make a Medical check (DC 20) to install or remove a
cybernetic attachment. If you do not have a surgery kit or access to a
medical facility, you take disadvantage on the check. Cybernetic surgery
takes 1d4 hours.

The consequences of failure are severe: If your check result fails by 5
or more, the installation or removal of the cybernetic attachment causes
undue physical trauma to the patient, who suffers 1d4 points of
Constitution damage. If the check result fails by 10 or more, the
Constitution damage is treated as Constitution drain instead.

A character who undergoes cybernetic surgery (successful or not) is
fatigued for 24 hours. Reduce this time by 2 hours for every point above
the DC the surgeon achieves. The period of fatigue can never be reduced
below 6 hours in this fashion.

**Normal**: Characters without this feat take double disadvantage on
Medical checks made to perform cybernetic surgery (Single disadvantage
if they have the Surgery feat).
