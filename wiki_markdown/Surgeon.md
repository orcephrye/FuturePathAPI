**Prerequisite**: Medical 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: The character can use the
[Medical](Medical "Medical"){.wikilink} skill to perform surgery without
penalty. They cannot fail a critical hit at Surgery. See the Medical
skill description.

**Normal**: Characters without this feat take a disadvantage penalty on
Medical checks made to perform surgery.
