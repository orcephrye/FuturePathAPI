**Prerequisites**: Proficiency level 5+

**Benefit**: When using an automatic firearm, the character may fire a
short burst as a single attack against a single target. This requires
the Character to brace themselves as a Simple Action before performing
the Standard Ranged Attack action. The Character gains an Extra Damage
status, which also moves the amount of the Accuracy check for the first
Extra Damage by one. Ie: Target\'s AC is 15, Accuracy check role is 18,
then if the Character has two Extra Damage, then they both hit, the
first for hitting 16+ and the second for 18+.

This requires at least two rounds of ammunition to remain in the gun and
spends two rounds. This doesn\'t allow for Bonus Attacks.
