You are a Mutant that is more powerful than normal. Learn more about
Mutations go [here](Mutations "here"){.wikilink}.

**Prerequisites**: Character must already be a
[Mutant](Mutations "Mutant"){.wikilink}.

**Benefit**: The Character can now have more than 1 secondary Mutation
at a time. This still means they can have only 1 primary, but now they
can have more than 2 of each Mutation type. Also this ignores upgrade
costs for secondary mutations. This also allows the Character to ignore
costs for new mutations after the 3rd mutation.

**Normal**: Without this feat, a Character can only have 2 mutations of
each type. 1 Primary and 1 Secondary. Also, the Secondary costs 1MP more
to acquire and 1 more to upgrade; this feat ignores the additional
upgrade cost. It also ignores the acquisition cost for mutations beyond
the 3rd mutation.

**Special**: -
