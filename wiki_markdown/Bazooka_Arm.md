**Prerequisite**: N/A

**Benefit**: Gain a +15ft range to throwing objects, including grenades.
Also, a successful grenade-throwing accuracy check now falls within the
range of 2 to 9. Meaning that 1 is 5ft to the left or low, while 10 is
5ft to the right or high, and so on for 1 and 10.

**Normal**: Normally, a successful hit is just between 4 and 7. To learn
more about grenade-throwing rules, visit the [Other Actions and
Situations](Combat#Other_Actions_and_Situations "Other Actions and Situations"){.wikilink}
page in the [Combat](Combat "Combat"){.wikilink} rules.
