Wither in a position of 1st mate or a Captain that may not yet have all
the experience behind him this represents a person ability to lead a
bridge crew in some capacity.

**Prerequisites**: Spaceship Operations, Leadership

**Benefits**: You gain an advantage bonus when attempting to aid another
Officer. The Captain position can also take advantage of the
[Manager](Manager "Manager"){.wikilink} feat and allows for this feat to
effect multiple Officers at a time based on the character\'s wisdom
modifier but only if they also have the Manager feat and this feat.

**Normal**: Normally aiding another player while on the bridge in combat
acts like aiding another in combat except any other
bonuses/talents/feats that effect normal combat do not effect Spaceship
Bridge Operations. This Feat is required if you want the person in the
Captain position to be more effective at aiding his/her crew and leading
them into combat.

**Special**: This is a requirement for Spaceship Captain Feat.
