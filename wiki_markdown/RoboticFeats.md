This page is dedicated to Robotic [Hero
Companions](Hero_Companions "Hero Companions"){.wikilink} Feats. In
order to get a complete list of all Feats available for a normal Hero
Character please go [here](Feats "here"){.wikilink}.

On this page, these Feats are like special abilities or traits a Robotic
Companion can learn. Feats are themed and organized into **categories**
like Combat Martial Arts or Skill Enhancements.
