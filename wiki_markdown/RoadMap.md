Summary: **Alpha** denotes that the rules are not yet complete and may
need heavy tweaking. **Beta** is reserved for when 90%+ of the rules are
complete and only tweaking of the rules is required. Beta should focus
on adding more content, such as item examples, different classes, feats,
and so on. While also taking the time to test the software. **Release
Candidates** will try to freeze all rules. Playtesting is not required.
Here, any artwork and last-minute theme/stylization work to the website
and apps is meant to be done. Also, all my horrible grammar and spelling
will be checked.

The first **Release** should be scheduled tentatively for Q1 of 2021.
