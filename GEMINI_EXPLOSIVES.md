# Explosives (Grenades & Traps) Summary

## Core Rules & Overview
* **Area of Effect (AoE):** Explosives deal damage or apply conditions to all targets within an area of effect. The area is either **Circular** (radius) or **Cone-shaped**.
* **Deployment Methods:**
  * **Throwable (Grenades):** Thrown using standard ranged attack/throwing action rules.
  * **Placeable (Mines & Traps):** Planted in a fixed position until moved or triggered. These function as traps using trap rules (Perception DC to detect, Disable Device skill check to disarm).
* **Proficiency:** Explosives require **no proficiency** to use normally.
* **Component Architecture:** Every custom explosive is assembled from three distinct modular parts: **Charge**, **Casing**, and **Detonator**.

## Custom Explosives & Crafting Rules

### The 3 Modular Components
1. **The Charge:** Determines the base damage die (e.g., Xd6, Xd10), the damage type (Kinetic, Thermal, Chemical, Electrical, Gravity, Psionic, etc.), explosion range/radius, and any special charge attributes (e.g., Lingers, Fogs, Stun, EMP).
   * **Tech Level Limits:** TL 1 (max d6), TL 2 (max d8), TL 3 (max d10), TL 4 (max d12).
2. **The Casing:** Determines the maximum charge multiplier (number of damage dice, e.g., x3 = 3d6), delivery mode (Throw-able, Place-able, or Both), area shape (Circular or Cone), spotting Perception DC modifier for planted traps, and casing attributes (e.g., +Accuracy, +Damage, +Range, Chasing, Containerized).
3. **The Detonator:** Controls how and when the explosive triggers (Contact, Time Delay, Remote, Trip Wire, Laser Wire, Motion Sensor, Electronic Sense, Smart Sense, Gravity Sense, Chasing). Can also modify Disable Device DCs.

### Combining Explosive Components
* **Demolitions Skill:** When components already exist, combining them requires a **Demolitions** skill check rather than a crafting skill.
* **Assembly Rules & Restrictions:**
  1. Multiple types of Charges **cannot** be mixed in the same Casing unless the Casing specifically allows it (e.g., Containerized Casings).
  2. A Casing can have more than one type of Detonator, but the resulting explosive **cannot be throw-able** and can still only detonate once unless specified by the Casing.
* **Assembly Time:** It takes **10 minutes** to safely combine components.
* **Rushed Assembly:** If rushed, make a Demolitions check of **DC 10 + 1 for each minute reduced** (e.g., assembling in 3 minutes is DC 17). Failure results in negative GM consequences (e.g., accidental detonation or creating a dud).

## Special Explosive Attributes & Keyword Reference

* **Casing not Required:** The explosive compound can be shaped and detonated directly without requiring a standard hard casing.
* **Chasing:** The explosive possesses automated movement capabilities, advancing toward designated targets within the listed movement range.
* **Containerized:** Specialized casing allowing multiple charge types or delayed/sub-munition payload deployments within a single device.
* **Contagious:** The effect spreads to any creature touching an affected target during the lingering duration.
* **EMP Effect:** Disables electronic systems, computers, and sensors. Deals electric damage to cybernetics and mechanical targets (mecha/robots).
* **Fogs:** Obscures vision and imposes disadvantage on visual perception and attacks within the designated area for the lingering duration.
* **Lingers:** The explosion leaves behind a persistent hazard (gas, smoke, or fire) lasting for the specified number of rounds/turns.
* **Stuns:** Subjects failing their save condition are stunned (lose DEX to AC, drop items, cannot take actions) for the duration.
