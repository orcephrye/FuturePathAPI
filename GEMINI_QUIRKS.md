# Quirks & Flaws Summary

## Core Rules
1. **Quirk vs. Flaw:**
   - **Quirk:** Used strictly for roleplaying; penalties are optional.
   - **Flaw:** Penalties strictly apply; overcoming it yields a minor positive award (usually the antithesis of the penalty or a minor attribute/skill bonus).
2. **Structure:** Each Quirk/Flaw contains:
   - **Description:** Story and roleplay context.
   - **Penalty:** Mechanical or roleplay constraint (static penalties, scaling penalties like -1 per 5 levels, or daily DCs). Non-combat unless explicitly stated.
   - **Overcome:** Soft guidelines for resolving the difficulty (must be agreed upon by Player & GM before play).

---

## Quirks & Flaws List

### Absent-Minded
* **Description:** The character often forgets important information, especially if recently learned.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on INT checks to recall past info/knowledge. Must make INT check to recall info learned during the current game session.
* **Overcome:** Roleplayed mental exercises and/or gaining +3 INT Theme Score (or +2 INT, +1 WIS).

### Allergy
* **Description:** Allergic to a substance where modern cures are permanent or ineffective due to rarity or rare bodily reaction.
* **Penalty:** Treated as poison. Poison Save: CON DC 10 + 1 per 6 min of continuous exposure (onset 1 min, freq 6 min). Deals 1d2 CON damage; 2 consecutive saves to cure.
* **Overcome:** Crafting/discovering specialized medical treatment via Knowledge/Profession and Craft checks.

### Amnesia
* **Description:** The character cannot remember their personal history (partially or entirely).
* **Penalty:** Major -4 penalty on all social skills; cannot use prior contacts.
* **Overcome:** Storyline triggers, therapy, memory recovery over time, or cybernetic memory unlock.

### Amorous
* **Description:** Difficulty suppressing sexual desires; easily distracted and inclined to flirt or pursue sexual targets.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on social skills and mind-affecting abilities to/from targets of character's sexual preference. Enemies of that preference gain Advantage against the character.
* **Overcome:** Self-control, mindfulness exercises, or leveling milestones.

### Anger Management
* **Description:** Aggressive and quick-tempered, losing control easily.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on Diplomacy. Failed social check triggers WIS save (DC 10 + fail margin); failure causes enraged/confused state for 1 min per point failed by.
* **Overcome:** Therapy between sessions or preset Wisdom score increases.

### Bad Luck
* **Description:** Exceptionally unlucky; forced to work harder to succeed.
* **Penalty:** On skill checks and saving throws (excluding CON death saves), succeeding by the exact minimum required requires a re-roll, taking the lower result.
* **Overcome:** Player/GM agreed explanation and resolution criteria.

### Broke
* **Description:** Terrible at money management and chronically poor.
* **Penalty:** Starting Wealth reduced by 3/4 (affects starting items and Starter Packs).
* **Overcome:** Financial/story recovery plan agreed upon by GM and Player.

### Combat Paralysis
* **Description:** PTSD related to conflict or specific types of violence (melee, ranged, etc.).
* **Penalty:** WIS save (DC 15 + # of opponents) required to enter combat involving the specific violence type. Can flee. DC decreases by 1 per round.
* **Overcome:** DC drops by 1 per 2 levels (cleared at level 20), or via roleplay milestones.

### Coward
* **Description:** Terrified of risk and danger to personal well-being.
* **Penalty:** Cannot put ranks in Intimidate. Faced with danger -> WIS save (fear effect) DC 15 + opponent hit dice (or action DC). Fail by 1 = Frightened; fail 2–5 = Cowering; fail >5 = Panicked. Other players can Aid.
* **Overcome:** Base DC decreases by 1 per 2 levels (cleared at level 20), or step-by-step roleplay reducing DC below 5.

### Crude
* **Description:** Harsh, brash, and mean-mannered in conversation without realizing it.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on Diplomacy and generic Charisma checks.
* **Overcome:** Gradual self-awareness and corrective behavior over time.

### Curious
* **Description:** Overly curious to a fault, often leading into dangerous or unfortunate circumstances.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on Perception checks unless target is fascinating to character.
* **Overcome:** Agreed roleplaying solutions over time.

### Cybernetic Prejudice
* **Requirement:** Mandatory if character has non-medical cybernetic implants.
* **Description:** Society distrusts, shuns, or hates individuals with non-medical cybernetic enhancements.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on Charisma checks/skills with NPCs who notice implants (increases up to 3 Disadvantages for visible implants). Scanner/Perception check vs DC 15 + Tech Level to hide.
* **Overcome:** Cannot be overcome unless all implants are removed.

### Dark Secret
* **Description:** Haunted by a detrimental event in their past, causing irrational or evasive behavior when confronted with it.
* **Penalty:** Every level after lvl 2 -> CHA check (DC 7 + level) or secret is revealed to party members.
* **Overcome:** Resolving and coming to terms with the past before revelation.

### Dull Senses
* **Description:** Senses are physically or mentally reduced compared to average members of the species.
* **Penalty:** Passive Perception receives no WIS bonus. Initiative reduced by -1 (min 0).
* **Overcome:** Roleplayed recovery based on source (medical/mental treatment).

### Enigmatic Humor
* **Description:** Tells inappropriate or unfunny jokes and struggles with serious conversations.
* **Penalty:** -1 to Diplomacy, Intimidate, and Bluff, plus an additional -1 per 5 levels.
* **Overcome:** Behavior modification, gaining Wisdom, or communication exercises.

### Family Troubles
* **Description:** Dark or annoying family complications (e.g., crime ties or leeching relatives).
* **Penalty:** GM-determined penalty based on the nature of the family complication.
* **Overcome:** Side quest(s) to resolve family problems.

### Groupie
* **Description:** Obsessed with a specific band/group (e.g. The Stage Droids) and shapes life around them.
* **Penalty:** Being >4 hours away from group causes agitation: WIS save DC 10 + 1 every 4 hours; failure forces character to rush back to group.
* **Overcome:** Gradual exposure therapy (succeeding saves away from group until DC reaches 30).

### Hunted
* **Description:** Pursued by an entity or individual seeking to cause them harm.
* **Penalty:** Every level after lvl 2 -> Survival check (DC 7 + 1/level + 1/consecutive level uncaught) or hunter finds them before next level. Resolving hunter gives 1 level of peace before checks resume (if backed by organization).
* **Overcome:** Confronting and resolving conflict with hunter on player's own terms.

### Illiterate
* **Description:** Inability to read or write.
* **Penalty:** Cannot use skills/abilities requiring reading or writing; all such checks automatically fail.
* **Overcome:** Downtime study or advanced technological knowledge injection.

### Infamy
* **Description:** Notorious or widely disliked in specific galactic regions due to past events or misunderstandings.
* **Penalty:** Major -2 per 5 levels to Charisma skills (except Intimidate) in those areas.
* **Overcome:** Backstory integration and reputation-clearing story arcs.

### Jocund
* **Description:** Overly positive and jolly, unable to convey or comprehend negative emotions.
* **Penalty:** -1 (plus -1 per 5 levels) to Bluff and Intimidate. Enemies roll at Disadvantage when sensing motives.
* **Overcome:** Psychological, physiological, or cybernetic treatment.

### Kleptomaniac
* **Description:** Compulsive urge to steal items belonging to others.
* **Penalty:** Daily WIS save (DC 10 + 1 per day without stealing). Fail = must attempt to steal something of value that day.
* **Overcome:** Downtime therapy and medical/skill care.

### Klutz
* **Description:** Clumsy, prone to bumping into objects or dropping breakable items.
* **Penalty:** -2 per 5 levels penalty on non-combat Dexterity ability and skill checks.
* **Overcome:** Increasing Dexterity score (e.g., +3 or +4 DEX).

### Lazy
* **Description:** Unproductive and constantly full of excuses to avoid contributing.
* **Penalty:** -2 penalty on Craft, Study, and Profession checks.
* **Overcome:** Group intervention or motivational story arcs.

### Liar
* **Description:** Compulsive liar who struggles to tell the truth, especially to save face.
* **Penalty:** -2 per 5 levels penalty on Diplomacy checks.
* **Overcome:** Downtime therapy or party assistance to cure mental habit.

### Lightweight
* **Description:** Unusually low body mass or physical weight.
* **Penalty:** Carrying capacity halved. Shoved or thrown twice normal distance in combat.
* **Overcome:** Increasing Strength and Constitution scores.

### Nocturnal
* **Description:** Sleeps during the day and active at night (by habit, psychology, or physiology).
* **Penalty:** -1 per 5 levels (starting at lvl 1) to Craft, Study, and Profession checks conducted during daylight.
* **Overcome:** Forcing daytime activity to adjust circadian rhythm.

### Obsessed
* **Description:** Fixated on an object or person to an extreme degree.
* **Penalty:** Daily WIS save (DC 10 + 1 per day away from object). Fail = focus on reaching object; suffers Shaken condition until found or save passed.
* **Overcome:** Increasing Wisdom score to reflect mental growth.

### Paranoid
* **Description:** Irrational belief that someone or something is out to get them.
* **Penalty:** Daily WIS save (DC 10 + 1 per day without fit). Fail = Frightened. Successive daily failures worsen condition to Cowering, then Panicked.
* **Overcome:** Resolving psychological or external source with GM/party aid.

### Phobia
* **Description:** Severe, debilitating fear of a specific object, person, idea, or situation.
* **Penalty:** Encountering fear object -> WIS save DC 10 + 1/level (in combat: DC 5 + 1/2 level) or Panicked until out of sight for 1 min. Fail by >3 in combat = Cowering, else Frightened.
* **Overcome:** Psychological exposure therapy and fear management.

### Physically Scared
* **Description:** Hideous or severely disfigured in appearance compared to average citizens.
* **Penalty:** -3 penalty to Charisma score.
* **Overcome:** Point-for-point reduction as Charisma score increases.

### Socially Awkward
* **Description:** Struggles with conversation and social gatherings.
* **Penalty:** Disadvantage on non-combat Charisma saving throws; -1 (plus -1 per 5 levels) to Diplomacy checks.
* **Overcome:** Social skill lessons during downtime.

### Substance Addiction
* **Description:** Chemical dependency on a drug; starting wealth reduced by 1/4. Requires substance every 24 hours.
* **Penalty:** 25th hour: hourly WIS save (DC 5 + 1/hr without) or progress through 8 withdrawal stages: Frightened -> Cowering -> Panicked -> Fatigued -> Exhausted -> Nauseated -> Sickened/Helpless -> Death (Stage 8 requires hourly CON check DC 7 + 1/hr to survive 24 hrs).
* **Overcome:** Surviving physical withdrawal + 24-hr mental panic phase without relapsing, plus gaining 1 level and therapy/crafting treatments.

### Repugnant
* **Description:** Repulsive in appearance, smell, or mannerisms.
* **Penalty:** -1 per 5 levels (starting at lvl 0) on Diplomacy and non-combat Charisma checks.
* **Overcome:** Behavior modification, hygiene/grooming, or physiological fixes.

### Weak Stomach
* **Description:** Prone to frequent bellyaches and gastrointestinal sensitivity.
* **Penalty:** -1 (plus -1 per 5 levels) to CON saves involving ingested substances.
* **Overcome:** Gene modification, diet medication, or medical treatment.
