# Spaceship Combat Summary

## 1. Crew Phases
Combat is played in rounds with 3 phases. Actions within a phase occur simultaneously.
1.  **Support Phase:** Captain (Give Command/Encourage) and Science Officer (Boost/Hack).
2.  **Action Phase:** Helm (Maneuvers), Comms (Scanning/Jamming), and Weapons (Firing).
3.  **Response Phase:** Medical Officer (Healing) and Chief Engineer (Repairs).

## 2. Damage Mechanics
Ships take **Battle Damage (BD)** instead of Hit Points.
*   **Accuracy:** Compare d10 results (equal to Hard Points) vs target **BD-DC** (Default 4,4).
*   **Defense Counters:** Structure increases required successes; Shields increase minimum roll.
*   **Offense Counters:** Sensors add bonus to d10 results (after Scan); Weapons grant rerolls.
    *   *Critical Scan:* Successful d10 counts twice. Weapons Officer can reroll 1s and 2s. If successes equal twice the BD-DC +1, an extra BD is dealt (treat as a roll of 7 on the BD chart).
*   **BD Limits by Size:** Colossal (5), Gargantuan/Huge (4), Large/Medium (3), Small/Tiny (2), Diminutive (1), Fine (0 - Destroyed on hit). Only 1 BD can be taken per round unless a critical hit occurs.

### Battle Damage Chart (2d6 Roll)
*   **2 (DC 27):** Plasma failure (Engineer damage). | **3 (DC 22):** Electronics short. 
*   **4 (DC 18):** Zero-G environment. | **5 (DC 14):** Rocked ship (Dex save vs Shaken).
*   **6 (DC 11):** Punctured hull. | **7 (DC 7):** Ruptured Valve (No effect).
*   **8 (DC 10):** Console explosion (Shaken). | **9 (DC 13):** Pilot disadvantage. 
*   **10 (DC 17):** Engines disabled. | **11 (DC 21):** Sensor array damaged. 
*   **12 (DC -):** Engine meltdown (Critical fix).

## 3. Ship Functions
*   **Standard:** Fire, Scan, Pilot (Offensive/Defensive), Boost, Repair, Ram.
*   **Advanced:** Cloak, Jam, Crypto, Feint (+4 AC), Grapple (Disables enemy AC), Concentrated Fire, Beam.
*   **Hacking:** Takes 3 rounds to deal damage. Can only cause **1 Battle Damage per combat** via hacking.

## 4. Tactical Maneuvers & Stealth
*   **Swarm Combat:** Multiple ships pool d10 successes; size-based pool penalties apply.
*   **Specified Targeting:** Targets attributes (disabled at 5) or Bays (8 HP). Every success = 1 damage. Max 5 damage per system per turn (9 if critical).
*   **Point Defense:** Counters missiles/drones. Roll d10 vs d10 (highest vs highest).
*   **Silent Operations:** Detection DC + Electronic Mods. Disables shields/engines/outgoing scans.
*   **Distance Penalties:** Huge (+2), Vast (+5), Astronomical (+9) to Detection DC and AC.

## 5. Small Crewed Ships (Fighters)
*   Ships with 1-2 crew (no Captain).
*   The Pilot chooses which phase to act in. They can roll for Piloting, Sensors, and Weapons simultaneously in the Action Phase.
*   Alternatively, they can perform a Science action in the Support Phase or Engineer repairs in the Response Phase, passing buffs to the next round if applicable.

## 6. Repairing Damage
*   **Repairing Effects:** Fixed DC 15. Takes 1 round.
*   **Repairing Battle Damage:** Base DC (from Chart) + Enemy's Ship Weapons Mod + Enemy Weapon Officer’s WIS or DEX score. Takes 2 non-consecutive rounds of focused work.
