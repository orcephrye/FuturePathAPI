'''Prerequisites''': [[Study]] 2 Ranks

'''Benefits''': You can add your affinity die to rolls for multiple science-related skills while on board your Spaceship. The list of skills is as follows: Computer Use, Decipher Script, Disable Device, Knowledge Science, and Study. They can also use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Advanced Officer Functions]] for that position. 

'''Normal''': Normally, a Character cannot add their affinity die to any skill check rolls, regardless of whether they are on a ship. You can only use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Standard Officer Functions]] for that position.
