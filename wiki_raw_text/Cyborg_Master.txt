You are progressing and improving your Cyborg self to its ultimate form. You are at a delicate balance between biological beings and machines, leaning more towards the mechanical side, and you would lose yourself. You can add/upgrade Cybernetic implants. 

'''Prerequisites''': [[Cyborg Expert]]

'''Benefit''':  Learn more about Cybernetics [[Cybernetics|here]]. This will allow the Character to gain nonbiological enhancements. The total number of Power and Slots available to the Character is now increased as a Level 5 Cyborg. 

'''Normal''': Without this feat, a Character must either take a new Detractor from the [[Difficulties#Detractors List|list]] or enhance an existing Detractor in order to level up their Cyborg frame.

'''Special''': -
