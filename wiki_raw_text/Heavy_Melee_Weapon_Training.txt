'''Prerequisite''': No Other Proficiency Training. IE, If you pick this, you cannot also pick Heavy Ranged Weapon Training or Heavy Armor Training.

'''Benefit''': Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 6 and higher for only 1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Melee Weapon Branch.

'''Normal''': Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 6 or higher.
