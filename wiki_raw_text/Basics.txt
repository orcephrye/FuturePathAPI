This page is a starting place for all the rules of the game. If you are new to Pen and Paper and roleplaying styles of games, you may wish to review the [[New_too_P%26P|New to P&P]] page.

Are you an experienced player? The [[Whats Different]] page is a good place to learn about what makes this system '''D20+''' so different from normal D20, as well as other things that stand out about this ruleset/game.

If you are a '''Player''' (Meaning you do not intend to run the game or be the storyteller), then it is recommended that you either [[Character_Creation_and_Advancement|make a new Character]] or pick a [[Starter_Characters|Starter Character]]. We provide a Character Sheet [https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1cZ3PPchkXAgTSWTHKsONEIWNrg8PhN2P here]! 

After making a Character, you may want to review concepts like [[Combat]] and [[SkillsBasics|Skills]].

If you intend to run a game as the '''Game Master''' or '''GM''' (that means that you are the storyteller and manage the game for the players), then it is recommended that you familiarize yourself with all the pages under the 'Rules' category. A GM should also be familiar with all the different [[Skills]] and how they are used. The [[GMs_Tools|GMs Tools]] page is full of helpful resources and guides. You may then want to look at the pages under the 'Campaign Setting' section to learn about the lore and cosmology of the FuturePath Universe.
