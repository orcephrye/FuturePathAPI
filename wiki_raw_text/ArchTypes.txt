The ArchType concept is meant to be a sort of 'Patch' to an existing Class or group of Classes. 

This differs from Prestige Classes in that it doesn't extend the levels of the Class. Merrily adjusts the abilities of the existing class.

It is like a new Theme for an existing class instead of an extension.
