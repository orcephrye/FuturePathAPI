'''Prerequisite''': No Heavy Weapon Proficiency Training. IE, if you pick this, you cannot also pick Heavy Contact Weapon Training or Heavy Firearms Training.

'''Benefit''': Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 7 and higher for only 1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Armor Branch.

'''Normal''': Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 7 or higher.
