'''Description''':
A sinister, matte-black warship operated by '''The Iron Mandible''' syndicate under classified Lepidonian contract. Engineered as a high-speed infiltration Battle Cruiser, the ''Obsidian Fang'' marries heavy capital skirmish firepower with specialized electronic warfare, an active stealth cloaking field, an ECM jammer, and a dedicated Class 1 Transporter Bay. Armed with specialized '''[[Missile_Ammo#shield_piercers|Shield Piercers]]''' and magnetic '''Heavy Breaching Leeches''', its primary operational doctrine is stripping enemy capital shields to execute surgical transporter snatch-and-grab extractions or shock boarding operations.
