The Psion can supercharge their Psionic tech as if it were done with a Psion 1 Level higher.

'''Prerequisites''': Character must already be a [[Psionics|Psion]].

'''Benefit''': Psion techniques often have something like damage, duration, or other attribute that is directly linked to the Psion's Level. This is treated as if it were one level higher, possibly dealing more damage, lasting longer, or providing more protection, etc. The Psion can empower a Technique a number of times per day equal to their Psion Level.
