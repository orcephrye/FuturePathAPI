'''Benefits''':  Allows a Character to roll with advantage when assisting others in actions. It allows them to assist even if they themselves do not know how to perform the action.

'''Normal''': Normally, a Character can only assist with whatever advantages or disadvantages that are by default provided, and can only assist if they themselves know how.
