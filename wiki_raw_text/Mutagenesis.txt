You are a Mutant that is more powerful than normal. Learn more about Mutations go [[Mutations|here]].

'''Prerequisites''': Character must already be a [[Mutations|Mutant]] or [[Psionics|Psion]].

'''Benefit''': This grants 1MP at level 1 and one more MP at every 5th level, ie: 5,10,15,20 for a total of 5 more MP.

'''Normal''': Without this feat, the only way to gain MP to spend is with a [[Mutation_Drawbacks|Drawback]].
