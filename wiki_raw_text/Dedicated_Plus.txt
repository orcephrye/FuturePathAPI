Advancements in science coupled with your drive to excel enable you to progress faster toward becoming a truly dedicated hero.

'''Prerequisites''': Your Character must have an Affinity with the Wisdom ability this Affinity can be gained through an Advance Class meaning that the character doesn't have to have the Wisdom Hero Base Class.

'''Benefit''': You gain one talent from the Dedicated hero’s talent trees.

'''Special''': You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you select this feat, you must choose a different talent.
