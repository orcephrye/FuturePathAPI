'''Prerequisite''': -

'''Benefit''': This feat is very useful when needing to repair items on the go. This allows the Item or object to remove one of its [[Item_Damage#Battle_Scaring|Battle Scars]] instantly and without crafting materials. This can only be done once per item. Although this resets for each item after it has been fully repaired. The DC for the repair check is +4, though. This represents a 'quick fix' approach to keep something working.

'''Normal''': In order to repair and item it is a Craft check that requires a 'Bill of Materials' and a professional kit in order to perform.
