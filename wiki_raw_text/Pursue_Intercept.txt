Pursue/Intercept is a Function of crew members. To learn about other functions go the [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Space Ship Functions]] section of the [[Space Ships]] page.

== Description ==
This is used for Chase mechanics. A Pilot who makes this check against an enemy ship allows the enemy ship to know that you are attempting to catch up to them and/or setup for a Ram. If the opposing check is higher than the enemy ship can choose to avoid the Interception.
