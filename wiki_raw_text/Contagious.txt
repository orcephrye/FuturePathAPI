;Contagious
* This means that whatever is effected by the [[Weapons#The_Charge|Charge]] can carry the effect onto other objects. If a distance is not specified then it is by touch only. The Charge is Contagious for at least 1 turn or as many turns as it [[Lingers|Lingers]] if it [[Lingers|Lingers]].
