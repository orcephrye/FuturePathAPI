'''Prerequisites''': [[Medical]] 2 ranks

'''Benefits''':  You can add your affinity die to rolls for Medical while on board your Spaceship, regardless of being in combat or the condition of the ship. They can also use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Advanced Officer Functions]] for that position.

'''Normal''':  Normally, a Character cannot add their affinity die to rolls for Medical unless an Advanced Class specifies otherwise. However, even in those situations, combat conditions aboard the ship can create disadvantages. They can only use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Standard Officer Functions]] for that position.
