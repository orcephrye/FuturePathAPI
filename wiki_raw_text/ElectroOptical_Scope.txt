This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons go [[Weapons|here]].

This can provide a +1 Accuracy bonus only for weapons with large or bigger ranges, same as a [[Standard Scope]]. However, this can overlook the disadvantages of poor lighting.

;Benefit: +1 Accuracy bonus to Ranged Weapons only. This only works on targets that exceed the initial range increment. Ignores penalties for poor lighting.
