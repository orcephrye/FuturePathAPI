This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons, go [[Weapons|here]].

This is a small flashlight connected to the barrel of the gun. It can shine light on anything in front of the gun.

;Benefit: Lights up a 45ft Cone from the direction of the gun. It can be easily turned on and off as a Free Action and can be used in and out of combat to see one's surroundings in the dark.
