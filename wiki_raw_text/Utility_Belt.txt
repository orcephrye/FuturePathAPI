A Utility Belt is a versatile piece of equipment that provides Quick Access Slots for small items, such as medical kits, stims, or tools. This allows the Character to retrieve these items with a Simple Move Action rather than a Standard Move Action.

;Benefit: 3 Quick Access Slots for small [[General_Equipment|General Equipment]] items (e.g., healing items).
