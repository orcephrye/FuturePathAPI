'''Prerequisite''': [[Medium Firearms Training]]. If you pick this, you '''cannot''' also have [[Heavy Contact Weapons Training]]

'''Benefit''': Allows one to gain Proficiency Level 6 and higher for only 1 Training Point instead of 2 in the Ranged Weapon Branch.

'''Normal''': Normally, a Character has to spend two points for level 6 or higher in the Ranged Weapons Proficiency Tree.
