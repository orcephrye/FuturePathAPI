;Alter Emotion
* This is a Mind Altering [[Weapons#The_Charge|Charge]]. The emotion is irrational, excessive, aggressive and uncompromising. The subject cannot help but either Love, Hate or Fear the nearest person or thing to them they can focus on until something or someone else because the closest thing that they can focus on. Then that becomes the focus of there new emotion. The target must do a Wisdom saving throw against a DC 15 (unless specified otherwise) once hit by the [[Weapons#The_Charge|Charge]]. If they fail then the emotion takes effect. If successful the effect seams to just be a distant thought in the back of there mind before disappearing completely.
