Long/Short Automated Care is an Advanced Function for crew members. To learn about other functions, go to the [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Space Ship Functions]] section of the [[Space Ships]] page.

== Description ==
If a Medical Bay or Facility is provided, then the Medic will move the Character into the Bay or Facility, where they receive automatic, constant attention from the Medic. This qualifies as receiving constant Long/Short Term Care automatically successfully. For more rules regarding Care, review the [[Medical]] skill.
