This is a Weapon Accessory. To learn more about Weapons, go [[Weapons|here]].

A Concealed Holster is used to carry small firearms on a person discreetly. It acts as a single Quick Access Slot that can hold a Small, Single-Handed weapon. This allows the weapon to be stored on the person in an easily accessible way, enabling them to 'Draw' the weapon as a Simple Move Action rather than a Standard Move Action, which would require pulling it from a bag or case.

;Benefit: 1 Quick Access Slot that can hold a Small, Single-Handed weapon. Can only have 1 of these on a Character, and this counts towards the total Holsters a Character can have on their person.
