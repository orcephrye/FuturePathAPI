Gain access to a [[Techniques#Advanced_Combat_Techniques|Advanced Combat Technique]].

'''Prerequisites''': -

'''Benefits''':  Allows a player to pick a single Advanced Combat Technique that they can now use at will. 

'''Normal''': Characters cannot normally use Advanced Combat Techniques unless specified otherwise. 

'''Special''': This can be picked multiple times to learn multiple Advanced Combat Techniques.
