The Science Officer is a [[Space_Ships#Ship_Positions|Ship Position]]. There are other ship positions that can be reviewed and can affect this position.

This is considered a secondary ship position.

== Description ==

The Science Officer normally assists the ship's crew in discovering new life forms, studying astronomical phenomena, or charting new star systems. They can perform a lot of white-collar and civilian-like duties onboard a spaceship. However, having a highly trained expert can also be useful in combat. Many scientists can use their expertise to do cyber warfare. They can also help decipher crypto messages. 

: '''Associated Ship Attribute''': Primary Electronics and Secondary Sensors 
: '''Officer Functions''': 
:# <u>[[Boost]]</u>: This provides a modifier bonus to one of the ship's Attributes, excluding Structure. It uses ship Electronics. It converts the Ship's Electronic Modifier over to either Engines/Weapons/Shields/Sensors.  If the Electronics Mod is 0, then it cannot provide any more boost. This also takes away the Electronics Mod for that round. The DC is 10. If successful, Electronics loses 1 modifier, and it is instead given to a different ship system. For every 3, the Science Office beats the 10, another pint is taken from Electronics and given to the Attribute being boosted. Only one attribute can be boosted per turn. Afterward, a cool-down of 1 turn where that Attribute cannot be boosted.
:# <u>[[Hack]]/[[Hack#Counter_Hacking|Counter Hack]]</u>: Hacking uses the ship's computers and Communications systems (Electronics and Sensors) to attempt to gain access to an enemy ship’s systems. Once inside, the Hacker can gather information, disrupt systems, and even deal damage. To learn more, visit the [[Hack]] page.
: '''Advanced Officer Functions''':
:# <u>[[Cloak]]</u>: This ability allows the ship to increase its Detection DC, hopefully making it not detectable by other enemy ships without a Scan. If this is the case, the ship can only be targeted by weapons on a successful scan check. 
:# <u>[[Crypto]]</u>: This ability allows the ship to encrypt information so that it is more difficult for a hacker to get information. So that communications between ships cannot be seen, read, or even detected. This can also be used to attempt to decrypt enemy ships' information or communications. This can also be used to secure communication with Drones so that they are not jammed by the enemy.
