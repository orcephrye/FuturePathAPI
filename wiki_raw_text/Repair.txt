Repair is a Function of crew members. To learn about other functions, go to the [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Space Ship Functions]] section of the [[Space Ships]] page.

== Description ==
The DC is based on the Damage that was given via a 2d6 roll noted in [[Battle Damage Chart]]. This is a Craft check for a Craft Structure, with bonuses from the Ship's Repair check.

Review the [[Giving_and_Receiving_Damage_in_Space_Ships#Repairing_Damage|Repairing Damage]] rules on the [[Giving_and_Receiving_Damage_in_Space_Ships|Giving and Receiving Damage in Space Ships]] page.
