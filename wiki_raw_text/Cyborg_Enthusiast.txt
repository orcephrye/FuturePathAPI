You are progressing and improving your Cyborg self. You can add/upgrade Cybernetic implants. You are now a Level 2 Cyborg. 

'''Prerequisites''': The Character must be a Level 1 [[Cybernetics#Cybernetic_Levels|Cyborg]].

'''Benefit''':  Learn more about Cybernetics [[Cybernetics|here]]. This will allow the Character to gain nonbiological enhancements in the form of Implanets. The total number of Power and Slots available to the Character is now increased as a Level 2 Cyborg. 

'''Normal''': Without this feat, a Character must either take a new Detractor from the [[Difficulties#Detractors List|list]] or enhance an existing Detractor in order to level up their Cyborg frame.
