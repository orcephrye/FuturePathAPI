
The Weapon's Text is formatted like:
: Accuracy Bonus + Weapon Damage Die [Damage Type] + Extra Damage Die [Damage Type] + Bonus Damage + (AP Bonus) Critical Range/Critical Multiplier -- Ammo (X), Bonus Effects. 
Examples:
: [[Pistol]] 1d6 20/x2 -- Ammo(7), Versatile
:: '''Explanation''': Any Bonuses that do not exist are simply not specified. So this weapon has no accuracy bonus or Bonus Damage or AP, so it is not mentioned. Also, the DR isn't mentioned because the default is Kinetic.
: [[Assault_Weapon#M16_Assault_Weapon|M16]] +1 1d10 19-20/x2 -- Ammo(6), Switching Fire Mods
:: '''Explanation''': Shows what the Accuracy Bonus and the change to the Critical Range are.
: [[Plasma_Rifle#Tactical_Plasma_Rifle|Tactical Plasma Rifle]] +1 2d8 [T] + 1 (AP 1) 20/x2 -- Ammo(5), Switch Firing Modes, Fully Unload 
:: '''Explanation''': This shows the Damage Type change from the default Kinetic to Thermal. This also shows how multiple attributes are comma-delimited.
