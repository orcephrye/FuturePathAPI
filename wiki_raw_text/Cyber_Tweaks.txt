You can now swap a Point for a Slot or a Slot for a Point. This can help gain access to an Implant that otherwise could not be installed on a given Framework.

'''Prerequisites''': [[Cyborg Enthusiast]]

'''Benefit''': Swap a Point for a Slot or a Slot for a Point in your Framework. 

'''NOTE''': The maximum amount of Points any Cyborg can have is 10, and the Maximum number of Slots is 6. This cannot change that. 

'''Normal''': Without this feat, a Character's slots or points are not swapable and are provided by their Framework, which is determined [[Cybernetics#Framework|here]].

'''Special''': -
