Your physical might is intimidating to others.

'''Prerequisite''': -

'''Benefit''': Add both your Strength and Chrisma modifiers to Intimidate skill checks.

'''Normal''': By default, when doing an Intimidate skill check, the Player must choose between one of these Abilities to utilize as part of the Skill.

'''Special''': Cannot also take [[Athletic Prowess]].
