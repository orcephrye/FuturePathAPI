'''Prerequisites''': [[Pilot]] 2 Ranks

'''Benefits''':  You can add your advantage die to Pilot checks if you are at the helm. You can also use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Advanced Officer Functions]] for that position.

'''Normal''':  Normally, a Character cannot add their advantage die to Pilot checks while at the helm. And you can only use [[Space_Ships#Officer.2FShip_Functions|Standard Officer Functions]] for that position.
