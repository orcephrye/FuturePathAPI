# Weapon Customization & Crafting Summary

## Weapon Acquisition
*   **Stock vs. Custom:** Players can buy pre-designed weapons or craft custom ones.
*   **Cost Calculation:** `Base Price (WL + TL) + (25% per Customization Point)`. 
*   **Procure Difficulty:** Higher Tech Levels and Masterwork status increase the difficulty to acquire.

## Tech Levels (TL)
Determines the ceiling for weapon power and initial customization capacity.
*   **TL 0:** Max 1d10 (WL 4), 0 Pts, +1 Proc Diff.
*   **TL 1:** Max 1d12 (WL 5), 1 Pt.
*   **TL 2:** Max 2d8 (WL 6), 2 Pts.
*   **TL 3:** Max 2d10 (WL 7), 3 Pts.
*   **TL 4:** Max 2d12 (WL 8), 4 Pts, +1 Proc Diff.

## Weapon Level (WL) Calculation
**WL = Power Level + Speed Level**
*   **Power (0-3):** Small, Medium, Large, Gigantic. Affects Range and Weight.
*   **Speed (0-4):** Single Fire, Manual, Semi-Auto, Full Auto, Super Fast. Affects Ammunition and Rate of Fire.
*   **Emergent Hit Die:** WL 0 (1d2) up to WL 8 (2d12).

## Crafting Steps
1.  **Select TL:** Sets limits and starting points.
2.  **Select Style:** Defines the role (e.g., Shotgun, Sniper) and modifies base stats.
3.  **Select Arch-Type:** Sets damage type (e.g., Laser, Plasma, Kinetic) and provides specific bonuses/penalties.
4.  **Set Power/Speed:** Determines WL and base Hit Die.
5.  **Apply Default Attributes:** Check for "Exotic," "Clunky," "Two-Handed," etc.
6.  **Advanced Customization:** Spend points to fine-tune stats.

## Weapon Styles
Styles define the intended use of a weapon and provide unique modifiers:
*   **Hunting Rifle:** High range, cheaper, extra negative point; Two-Handed, no special ammo/bonus abilities.
*   **Personal Firearm:** Civilian pistol, versatile, cheaper, extra negative point; Low range, no special ammo.
*   **Side Arm:** Military pistol, medium range, +1 damage, versatile; -1 customization point, more expensive.
*   **Shotgun:** +1 Hit Die level; Pellets limit range to 30ft and use cone area rules.
*   **Sniper Rifle:** Tripled range, halved range penalty; Max semi-auto, expensive.
*   **CQB Specialized:** Indoors focus, +1 Crit Multiplier, Fully Unload; 75ft max range, expensive.
*   **Chemical Thrower:** Persistent chemical damage (1d3 rounds), area spray; Low range, clunky/exotic.
*   **DMR Specialized:** Switch Firing Modes, halved range penalty; No Fully Unload, expensive.
*   **Grenade Launcher:** Uses grenade damage, Exploding Die; No bonus damage/AP/crit mods, misses land short.
*   **Assault Specialized:** Military standard, choice of Enhanced Combat Options or Switch Firing Modes.
*   **Light Machine Weapon:** Support, Suppression Fire, tripled ammo, special ammo; Clunky, expensive.
*   **Melee Weapon:** Manual speed; Special negative point interaction for Versatile attribute.
*   **Throwing Weapon:** Throwable, range based on STR+DEX; Strength requirements based on Power Level.
*   **Archaic Ranged:** +1 restricted customization point; Single-Fire only.
*   **Specialized Attack Weapon:** TL 2+; Casts Electro-Mancer/Shield Splicer Techniques based on MW+TL.

## Weapon Arch-Types
Arch-Types determine the base damage type and elemental properties:
*   **Kinetic:** Default (TL 0-4); No special modifiers.
*   **Custom:** +1 customization point for Damage Type; Cannot be Intuitive.
*   **Energy:** Electric damage, +1 Damage, -1 Ammo.
*   **Laser:** Thermal damage, +1 Accuracy, -1 Ammo.
*   **Plasma:** Thermal, +1 Die level, -1 range increment; Expensive ammo, +1 Proficiency requirement.
*   **Gravity:** Area of effect (radius based on Power), +1 AP, expensive; +1 Proficiency requirement.
*   **Particle:** Chemical/Kinetic damage, +1 Accuracy, +1 Damage; Expensive ammo.
*   **Anti-Matter:** Thermal/Kinetic, +1 Die level, +3 AP, expensive; +1 Proficiency requirement.

## Weapon Costs & Pricing
*   **Cost Formula:** `(Final Base Price * Arch-Type Multiplier) + Style Modifier + (Customization Cost * Points)`.
*   **Base Prices (WL 0–8 at TL 0):**
    *   **Melee:** $15, $60, $240, $480, $960, $1440, $2160, $2700, $3375
    *   **Ranged:** $45, $180, $720, $1440, $2880, $4320, $6480, $8100, $10125
*   **Base Prices Tech Level One (TL1):**
    *   **Melee:** $20, $80, $320, $629, $1380, $2020
    *   **Ranged:** $50, $200, $800, $1600, $3200, $4800
*   **Base Prices Tech Level Two (TL2):**
    *   **Melee:** $25, $100, $400, $880, $1600, $2400, $3700
    *   **Ranged:** $55, $220, $880, $1760, $3520, $5280, $8280
*   **Base Prices Tech Level Three (TL3):**
    *   **Melee:** $30, $120, $480, $950, $1920, $2880, $4320, $5400
    *   **Ranged:** $60, $240, $960, $1920, $3840, $5760, $8640, $10,800
*   **Base Prices Tech Level Four (TL4):**
    *   **Melee:** $35, $140, $560, $1120, $2240, $3360, $5040, $6300, $7875
    *   **Ranged:** $65, $260, $1040, $2080, $4160, $6240, $9360, $11700, $14625
*   **Customization Point Cost:** Each point costs **25% of the Final Base Price** (WL Base + TL Increase), rounded to the nearest $5.
*   **Masterwork:** Doubles the base price and increases Procure Difficulty by +1.
*   **Common Modifiers:**
    *   **Personal Firearm / Hunting Rifle:** -$150 Price, -1 Procure Diff.
    *   **Side Arm:** +$100 Price, +1 Procure Diff.
    *   **Sniper Rifle:** +50% Price, +1 Procure Diff.
    *   **CQB / DMR:** +$1,000 Price, +1 Procure Diff.
    *   **Light Machine Weapon:** +$1,250 Price, +1 Procure Diff.
    *   **Arch-Types (Gravity, Anti-Matter, etc.):** Often add 2x to 4x the base price to the total.

## Advanced Customization Points
*   **Earning:** Gained via TL, Masterwork levels (+1 to +5), or taking Negative Attributes (2 Negative Pts = 1 Customization Pt).
*   **Accuracy:** Max +5 bonus to accuracy checks.
*   **Criticals:** Increase Range (max 17-20 based on speed) or Multiplier (max x4 based on Power).
*   **Special Effects:** Bane, Bleeding, Burning, Disarming, Shocking, etc.
*   **Special Mechanics:** 
    *   **Exploding Die:** Re-roll max values.
    *   **Split Die:** Split 1d8 into 2d4 (increases average and pool size).
    *   **Hit Die Increase:** Spend 2 pts to bump die level (subject to TL limits).

## Bonus Abilities
Special maneuvers and configurations (1 Customization Pt each):
*   **Burst Fire:** Accuracy and Crit bonuses at the cost of ammo.
*   **Fully Unload:** Automatic critical or AoE attack (halves range, grants enemies advantage).
*   **Suppression Fire:** Cone-based area denial; enemies moving through cone trigger attacks.
*   **Switch Firing Modes:** Toggle between Auto and Single Fire for accuracy bonuses.
*   **Versatile:** Allows WL 3-5 weapons to be one-handed.

## Default Attributes
*   **Exotic/Archaic:** Requires +1 Proficiency level to use.
*   **Clunky:** Requires two hands and has slower reload.
*   **Intuitive:** Reduces Proficiency requirement by 1 (limited to lower-level weapons).
*   **Masterwork:** Increases accuracy/customization but doubles price and +1 Proc Diff.

## Ammunition & Special Ammo
Ammunition cost is determined by the weapon's **Power Level**. Special Ammo doubles the base cost. A weapon must have the "Special Ammunition" Bonus Ability to use these variants (unless natively required by its Style/Arch-Type).

*   **Small:** $5 Base / $10 Special
*   **Medium:** $15 Base / $30 Special
*   **Large:** $45 Base / $90 Special
*   **Gigantic:** $135 Base / $270 Special

### Special Ammunition Types
*   **Damage Type Change:** Changes the base Kinetic damage to Electrical, Thermal (Radiation), or Chemical. 
*   **Bonus Damage:** Requires Masterworking. Adds flat damage (+1 for MW1, +2 for MW3, +3 for MW5).
*   **Bonus Damage Die:** Adds a bonus die (1d4 to 1d12 based on MW level) of Kinetic, Electric, Thermal, or Chemical damage. Cost formula: `Special Ammo Cost * (1 + MW Level)`.
*   **AP Bonus:** Grants +1 Armor Piercing. Requires MW 3 or higher. Cannot exceed +1.
*   **Explosive Rounds:** Grants the 'Exploding Die' attribute.
*   **AoE Rounds:** Reduces weapon range by 1 increment but adds a 5ft radius Area of Effect. Masterworking can remove the range penalty, increase the radius (max 15ft), or add status effects (Shaken or Prone based on saves).

## Ammo Crafting Rules
*   **Standard Ammo:** Requires Rank 2 in the relevant crafting skill.
*   **Special Ammo:** Requires Rank 3 in the relevant crafting skill.
*   **Relevant Skills by Damage Type:**
    *   **Kinetic:** Mechanical
    *   **Electric:** Electronics
    *   **Thermal (Radiation):** Electronics AND Chemical
    *   **Chemical:** Chemical OR Pharmaceutical
*   **Masterwork Limits:** Crafted ammo takes on the Masterwork level of the crafter (signal +1, etc.), except for "Bonus Damage Die" which requires the crafter to match the specific Masterwork level they wish to apply (up to +4).
