# Character Path Reference Summary

Character Paths form the foundational framework for heroes in d20 FuturePath. Progression occurs up to level 20 across 7 main core Paths (plus the Ovex species custom path). Characters further specialize through Professions, Archetypes, and Prestige Classes.

---

## 1. Core Path Features & Rules

- **Ability Affinity:** Each Path is tied to a specific Ability Score (e.g. STR for Strong, DEX for Fast). Saving throws associated with that Ability add the Path's Advantage Die ($\text{Ad}$) as an extra modifier to the roll.
- **Hit Die (HD):** Determines Hit Points gained per level. Level 1 grants max HD + CON Mod. Subsequent levels grant HD roll or average HP + CON Mod.
- **Favored Skills:** Path-specific skills that cost 1 Skill Point per rank instead of 2.
- **Skill Points per Level:** Granted at each level up (`1 + INT Mod` or `2 + INT Mod`, minimum 1).
- **Starting Feat / Skill Point Option:** Level 1 characters pick 1 of 2 starting feats or opt for 1 extra Skill Point instead.
- **Talents & Talent Trees:** Talents are unlocked at levels **1st, 6th, 12th, and 18th**. Characters follow a specific Talent Tree.
- **Feats Progression:** Feats are gained at every odd level (1st, 3rd, 5th, etc.), alternating between **Tactical** and **Utility** categories after 1st level.
- **Custom Character Bonus:** At levels **4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 20th**, characters choose between $+1$ to an Ability Score OR a Bonus Feat from Path-specific sub-categories.
- **Extra Damage:** Granted at specific levels (5th, 11th, 17th or 7th, 13th, 19th depending on path) when an attack action exceeds target AC by 2 or more.

## 2. Character Paths Overview

### 1. Path of Strength (Strong Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Strength (STR)
- **Hit Die:** `1d8` (Level 1: 8 + CON, Avg: 5)
- **Skill Points:** `1 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Athletics + Choose 2 (*Intimidate, Sense Motive, Survival*).
- **Recommended Professions:** Dimension Knight, Helix Warrior.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Athletic or Confident (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Extreme Effort:* Extreme Effort (Advantage on check 1x/day) $\rightarrow$ Athleticism (swim/climb at walk speed) $\rightarrow$ Improved Extreme Effort (2x/day or $2\times$ Ad) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Extreme Effort (3x/day or $3\times$ Ad).
  - *Melee Smash:* Melee Smash (+1 melee dmg) $\rightarrow$ Combat Expert (free Advanced Combat Technique) $\rightarrow$ Improved Melee Smash (+2 total dmg) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Melee Smash (+3 total dmg).

### 2. Path of Dexterity (Fast Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Dexterity (DEX)
- **Hit Die:** `1d8` (Level 1: 8 + CON, Avg: 5)
- **Skill Points:** `1 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Acrobatics + Choose 2 (*Pilot, Sleight of Hand, Stealth*).
- **Recommended Professions:** Starfighter, Space Marine.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Athletic or Acrobatic (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Defensive:* Evasion (+1 DEX saves) $\rightarrow$ Uncanny Dodge (contest hit with DEX save 1x/day) $\rightarrow$ Improved Evasion (+2 DEX saves total) $\rightarrow$ Improved Uncanny Dodge (Uncanny dodge 1x/combat).
  - *Aggressive:* Increased Speed (+5 ft base speed) $\rightarrow$ Applied Acrobatics (ignore disadvantage in difficult terrain/swimming) $\rightarrow$ Improved Increased Speed (+10 ft speed total, Ad on Charge/Tackle) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Increased Speed (+15 ft speed total, $+2\times$ Ad on Charge/Tackle).

### 3. Path of Constitution (Tough Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Constitution (CON)
- **Hit Die:** `1d10` (Level 1: 10 + CON, Avg: 6)
- **Skill Points:** `1 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Athletics + Choose 2 (*Acrobatics, Intimidate, Survival, Perception*).
- **Recommended Professions:** Dreadnought, Shield Splicer.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Endurance or Confident (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Healthy:* Healthy Body (+1 HP/lvl) $\rightarrow$ Stamina (4-hour full rest, $2\times$ natural healing) $\rightarrow$ Improved Healthy Body (+2 HP/lvl total) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Healthy Body (+3 HP/lvl total).
  - *Damage Resistance:* Chemical, Kinetic, Electricity, or Thermal Resistance (DR against energy types) & Damage Reduction 1/2/3 (ignores physical weapon damage).
  - *Unbreakable:* Undying (+10 CON when unstable) $\rightarrow$ Remain Conscious (move action while dying) $\rightarrow$ Second Wind (recover 1d10 HP 1x/day; 2d10 at lvl 10) $\rightarrow$ Iron Stomach (shrug off Nauseated/Sickened 1x/day).

### 4. Path of Intelligence (Smart Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Intelligence (INT)
- **Hit Die:** `1d6` (Level 1: 6 + CON, Avg: 4)
- **Skill Points:** `2 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Computer Use, Study + Choose 2 (*Any Knowledge, Any Craft*).
- **Recommended Professions:** Engineer, Electro-Mancer, Technosavant.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Educated or Studious (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Research:* Research (+1 misc to INT skills) $\rightarrow$ Book Worm (+2 misc to Study) $\rightarrow$ Savant (1 Knowledge or Craft skill becomes Favored) $\rightarrow$ Improved Research (+2 misc to INT skills total).
  - *Strategy:* Strategy (use INT mod for accuracy check 1x/combat) $\rightarrow$ Exploit Weakness (INT check DC 15 + CR vs player level to add INT mod as bonus damage for 1d6 rounds) $\rightarrow$ Improved Strategy (Strategy 1x/opponent, +1 accuracy) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Strategy (Strategy 2x/opponent, +2 accuracy).

### 5. Path of Wisdom (Dedicated Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Wisdom (WIS)
- **Hit Die:** `1d6` (Level 1: 6 + CON, Avg: 4)
- **Skill Points:** `2 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Perception, Sense Motive + Choose 2 (*Profession, Diplomacy, Survival, Study*) + 1 Knowledge.
- **Recommended Professions:** Xenophile, Tracer, Combat Medic.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Persuasive or Attentive (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Empathetic:* Empathy (+1 misc to Sense Motive) $\rightarrow$ Improved Aid Another (+1/+2/+3 to aid another) $\rightarrow$ Improved Empathy (+2 Sense Motive, +1 Diplomacy) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Empathy (+3 Sense Motive, +2 Diplomacy).
  - *Healing:* Healing Touch (+1 misc to Medical) $\rightarrow$ Healing Expert (out of combat kit healing adds Advantage bonus HP) $\rightarrow$ Improved Healing Touch (+2 Medical) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Healing Touch (+3 Medical).
  - *Aware:* Aware (+1 misc to Survival or Perception) $\rightarrow$ Attentive (+1/+2/+3 Passive Perception) $\rightarrow$ Improved Awareness (+1 Survival/Perception) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Awareness (+1 Survival/Perception).

### 6. Path of Charisma (Charismatic Hero)
- **Ability Affinity:** Charisma (CHA)
- **Hit Die:** `1d6` (Level 1: 6 + CON, Avg: 4)
- **Skill Points:** `2 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** Diplomacy + Choose 2 (*Profession, Bluff, Disguise, Sense Motive*) + 1 Knowledge.
- **Recommended Professions:** Envoy, Field Officer, Swindler.
- **Starting Feat Options:** Deceptive or Trustworthy (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Talent Trees:**
  - *Persuasive:* Persuasive (+1 misc to Diplomacy or Intimidate) $\rightarrow$ Inspirational (Advantage on Aid Another + extra +2 bonus) $\rightarrow$ Improved Persuasion (+1 Diplomacy/Intimidate) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Persuasion (+1 Diplomacy/Intimidate).
  - *Beguile:* Deceiver (+1 misc to Bluff or Disguise) $\rightarrow$ Charm (Advantage on CHA checks vs chosen gender) $\rightarrow$ Improved Deceiver (+1 Bluff/Disguise) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Deceiver (+1 Bluff/Disguise).
  - *Leadership:* Coordinate (standard action CHA check DC 10 + members grants allies within 30 ft +1 accuracy/skills for CHA mod rounds) $\rightarrow$ Confidence (add Advantage die to failed CHA check 1x/day) $\rightarrow$ Improved Coordinate (+2 bonus) $\rightarrow$ Advanced Coordinate (+3 bonus).

### 7. Path of No Path (The Freelancer)
- **Ability Affinity:** None
- **Hit Die:** `1d8` (Level 1: 8 + CON, Avg: 5)
- **Skill Points:** `2 + INT Mod`
- **Favored Skills:** 1 Knowledge, 1 Craft, and 2 of any other type.
- **Recommended Professions:** Any (blank slate archetype).
- **Starting Feat Options:** Any Skill Enhancement feat (or +1 Skill Point).
- **Generalist Training:** Bonus feats at 6th, 12th, and 18th level are not restricted by Tactical/Utility alternation and can be chosen from any feat category.
