# Animal Companions Reference (Gemini Context File)

[Official Animal Companion Character Sheet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UQeoUj-ZprwqFcrbEJgK2re1jY1izn8f/view?usp=sharing)

## 1. Character Creation Workflow & General Rules
* **Workflow:** Pick Mature Size & Type $\rightarrow$ Adjust stats via modified Point Buy $\rightarrow$ Pick Lifecycle & Locomotion $\rightarrow$ Choose 1 Natural Characteristic & 1 starting Natural Feature $\rightarrow$ Choose 1 starting Feat $\rightarrow$ Choose 3 Favored Skills & spend 1 starting skill point $\rightarrow$ Level up one level at a time.
* **Level Restriction:** A companion's level cannot exceed its owner's. If roleplay allows a higher-level companion, it cannot participate in combat until the owner reaches its level.
* **Maintenance Cost:** If a companion is found/trained or crafted instead of purchased, the player must still pay resources equal to the purchase price (by level) for maintenance.
* **Untrained Check:** In combat, commanding a companion without the Xenophile class or Companionship feat requires a CHA or Handle Animal check. Untrained companions level at **1/6th rate**.

## 2. Creature Mature Size & Point Buy
Size determines **AC Bonus**, **Hit Die**, and **Ability Points**.
No companion can be larger than **Large** without special rules (e.g., Xenophile class).

| Size | AC Bonus | Hit Die | Ability Points | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Tiny** | +2 | d4 | 9 | Cannot be the adult mammal size. |
| **Small** | +1 | d6 | 10 | |
| **Medium** | +0 | d8 | 10 | |
| **Large** | -1 | d10 | 10 | Max standard size. |
| **Gigantic** | -2 | d12 | 11 | Requires Xenophile; +1 Procure Diff. |

### Modified Point Buy System
All animals start with: **Str 10, Dex 10, Con 10, Wis 5, Int 2, Cha 8**.
Buying points scales relative to the starting base of the attribute:
* Raising from `[Base]` to `[Base + 1]` costs **1 point** (e.g., Wis 5 $\rightarrow$ 6 costs 1; Str 10 $\rightarrow$ 11 costs 1).
* Raising from `[Base + 1]` to `[Base + 2]` costs **2 points** (e.g., Wis 6 $\rightarrow$ 7 costs 2; Str 11 $\rightarrow$ 12 costs 2).
* Raising from `[Base + 2]` to `[Base + 3]` costs **3 points**, and so on.

## 3. Creature Types
* **Mammal:** +2 to either Str/Dex AND -2 to either Wis/Int/Cha, **OR** +1 to either Wis/Int/Cha. Gains +1 skill point. Flight cannot be primary (must learn it).
* **Reptile:** AC bonus: +1 (Tiny/Small), +2 (Med/Large), +3 (Gigantic). +1 to Str/Con. If the companion has an attack that inflicts Bleed or Poison, increase the damage or DC of that effect by +1. Flight cannot be primary (must learn it).
* **Amphibian:** -1 AC. +1 Con. +2 to either Dex/Con/Wis. Swim locomotion is free and does not count against starting locomotion limit.
* **Avian:** +1 AC when flying. +1 Str/Dex/Con **OR** +1 Wis/Int/Cha. Either Active Flight primary (60ft, 1/2 speed 2nd locomotion) or Gliding primary (40ft, full speed 2nd locomotion).
* **Arthropod:** +1 AC. +2 Dex/Con, -1 Str, -1 Wis/Int/Cha, -1 Cha. Free Lvl 1 Feature: Hard Shell, Hyper Awareness, or Natural Reflexes.
* **Aquatic:** +1 AC in liquid. Either (+1 Con, -1 Str) **OR** (+2 Wis/Int/Cha, -1 Str/Dex/Con). Swim locomotion only by default. Land locomotion (1/2 speed) costs either -1 Skill Point OR -2 Attribute points at creation.
* **Plant:** +1 AC. +2 Con, -2 Dex. Thermal Weakness 2. Does not require food (needs light/water/organic waste). Can learn `Spit` attacks for spores/acid.
* **Ooze:** -2 AC. +2 Con, -2 Dex, -2 Wis/Int/Cha. Amorphous Frame (Immune to critical hits, precision damage, and flanking). Corrosive attacks (inflict Chemical damage). Cannot take flight or jumping locomotion.
* **Annelid:** +0 AC. +1 Str, +1 Con, -2 Cha. Vibration Sense (Blindsight vibration up to 30ft, blind to light). Burrowing locomotion is 10ft (does not count against starting limit).
* **Silicon-Based:** +2 AC. +2 Con, -2 Dex, -1 Cha. Environmental Immunity (Immune to vacuum, toxic air, standard poisons/diseases). Kinetic DR +1. Sonic Weakness 2.
* **Symbiote:** +0 AC. +2 Wis/Con, -2 Str, -1 Cha. Host Attachment (as standard action, attaches to willing/helpless host in neck/shoulder/armor slot; shares 25% damage taken; grants host specific sensory or biochemical bonus like Night Eyes or Advantage on Initiative). Draws 1 HP from host per day.

## 4. Lifecycles
* **Maturing:** 
  * **Childhood (Lvl 1–7):** One size smaller and -1 Hit Die tier (min d4). Uses childhood size for size-based AC and Attack modifiers, but uses normal point-buy attributes.
  * **Adulthood (Lvl 8+):** Grows to mature size. Hit Dice are retroactively re-rolled using adult Hit Die size. Gains +1 Con, +2 attribute points (to Str/Dex/Wis/Int/Cha), and unlocks a new Natural Attack or Natural Feature.
* **Amphibian Metamorphosis:** Occurs at Lvl 4, 10, or 16. Gains a new locomotion (player chooses to lose old locomotion or keep it at 1/2 speed), gains environmental adaptation (breathe underwater, breathing at high altitude, extreme temperatures/pressures) including DR 1 to one damage type, and +1 attribute point.
* **Metamorphosis:** Metamorphose up to 2 sizes at Lvl 4 or 10. Helpless (cocoon/hibernation) for 1 level per size changed (levels up but cannot interact). 
  * **Helpless State:** Considered helpless in combat (AC = 5 + Dex modifier; adjacent coup de grace attacks succeed automatically) but gains **Hardness 5** and can be carried by a Medium or larger ally.
  * **Emergence:** Rebuilt as a new higher-level creature. Hit Die retroactively updates to larger size, and attribute points are completely re-spent based on larger size. Retains the Metamorphosis lifecycle, and gains a bonus Natural Attack and Natural Feature.
* **Fission:** Split cellular mass temporarily into coordinated entities once per encounter as a full-round action.
  * **Clones:** Body divides into 2 identical clones. Each is one size smaller (min Tiny) and has exactly half of current HP and Advantage Die tier.
  * **Mechanics:** Share same command checks and initiative. Must remain within 60ft. If a clone reaches 0 HP, it dissolves, and maximum HP is lost permanently until rest. Can merge back adjacent as a full-round action.

## 5. Locomotion & Natural Attacks
### Locomotion (Primary speed)
* **Bipedal / Tripedalism:** 30ft. Forelimbs can act as basic manipulators (hold items, open doors, carry gear) but cannot use weapons/complex tasks unless trained.
* **Quadrupedalism:** 40ft. Doubles carrying capacity for its Strength and size.
* **Undulation:** 30ft. Sidewinding, concertina, or rectilinear forms of movement.
* **Active Flight:** 60ft. Active Flight and Gliding require DC 15 Handle Animal/Athletics check in high winds, storms, or turbulent aerial terrain.
* **Gliding:** 40ft.
* **Jumping:** 20ft. Instantly grants the `Jumper` feat.
* **Swimming:** 30ft. Grants 5ft land speed by default.
* **Burrowing:** 5ft. Limited to loose soil, dirt, sand, mud, or clay. Solid rock/steel is impossible by default.

### Natural Attacks
Damage die is determined by creature size. Specialized Natural Attack Effects can be added or improved upon.
* **Claw / Claws:** Simple claw attacks. Claws is a dual-accuracy full-round action. Adds Bleed or Poison.
* **Bite:** Highly customizable. Combines any two effects: Bleed, Poison, Trip, Grapple, or Burn.
* **Grip:** Standard grapple-focused strike. Starts with Grapple, can add Trip.
* **Slam:** Target must be same size or smaller. Uses Con for bonus damage instead of Strength.
* **Trample:** Target must be same size or smaller. Cannot be used by Tiny. Relies on Charge rules.
* **Whip:** Melee reach attack. Default reach is 5ft (can pull grappled targets 5ft/round). Uses Dex for attack and damage.
* **Sting:** Tail-based sting. Default Poison/Bleed/Burn. Can convert to 25ft Ranged attack (lowers damage die tier).
* **Spit:** Ranged attack (30ft + 10ft/improvement). No base attribute bonus to damage (must buy Dex/Con/Wis).
* **Gore:** Horns/tusks ramming strike. Adds Advantage Die to damage at the end of a successful Charge combat technique.
* **Tail Sweep:** Sweeping bludgeoning tail club strike. 10ft reach. Can be improved to target two adjacent enemies.
* *Refer to [Natural Attacks Reference](file:///home/rye/PycharmProjects/d20FuturePathWiki/wiki_raw_text/Natural_Attacks.txt) for damage charts, improvements, and full descriptions of effects (Bleed, Poison, Trip, Grapple, Burn, Shoved, Blinded, Dazed).*

## 6. Natural Characteristics (Pick 1 at Lvl 1)
* **Carrier (Beast of Burden):** Ridden by any creature its size or smaller. x2 carrying capacity (by Strength and size), x3 pull capacity. Gains a free Slam or Trample attack (or improves it if already owned).
* **Tracker:** +1 Perception in the wild. +1 Survival tracking (+2 at Lvl 10). Free `Hunt/Track` trick.
* **Ambusher:** Gains Sneak Attack (adds Advantage Die as bonus damage when attacking unaware/flanked enemy).
* **Aggressor:** Companion price is doubled; +1 Procure Difficulty. Free `Improved Natural Attack` feat at Lvl 1 and Lvl 10.
* **Defender:** Owner always has Luck on Perception checks against combatants. Choose 1 free trick: Guard, Watch, Detect, or Menace.
* **Supporter:** Owner gains Advantage on saves against Feints, Distractions, Psionics, and Wisdom saves against Frightened, Cowering, or Panicked (which they receive even if the rules say the Character cannot normally save). Free `Aid` trick.
* **Scouter:** +1 Perception/Survival not to get lost in wild (+2 at Lvl 10). +1 Passive Perception. Free `Get Help` trick.
* **Enforcer:** Free `Guard` and 1 other trick. +1 Favored Skill, +1 starting Skill Point. Free `Advanced Combat Techniques` feat.
* **Pack Hunter:** Gains Pack Tactics (+1 attack per adjacent ally). When flanking an enemy, grants the flanking bonus (+2 accuracy) to all allies attacking that target. Free `Aid` trick.
* **Survivor:** Immune to natural diseases, biological poisons, and radiation. Advantage on saving throws against extreme environmental hazards (high heat, extreme cold, toxic gases). Can digest inorganic or decayed waste without harm. Free `Fetch` trick.

## 7. Natural Features
Features grant passive bonuses and improve at specific levels. Simple improvements occur at Lvl **1, 5, 10, 15, 20**. Advanced improvements occur at Lvl **7, 13, 19**.

### Simple Features
* **Hyper Awareness:** +1 Perception and +1 Passive Perception per improvement.
* **Graceful Motion:** +1 to Dex score per improvement.
* **Raw Speed:** +10ft speed per improvement. Also gains +1 Dex at Lvl 10.
* **Night Eyes:** Night vision. Gains Sneak Attack advantage in darkness (remains vulnerable to standard sight-based blindness).
* **Echo Location:** +1 Perception. Can see in total darkness using sonar (ignores sight-based blindness and gaze attacks; nullified if Deafened or in magical silence).
* **Local Adaptation:** DR +1 to one damage type per improvement (player describes physical adaptation).
* **Climber:** Climb speed = base speed. +1 AC climbing (this feature does not scale at higher improvement levels). Ignores climb attack disadvantage.
* **Teachable:** +1 Trick at each improvement level.
* **Camouflage:** Stealth becomes a favored skill. +1 Stealth per improvement.
* **Bioluminescence:** Emits light (bright light 15ft, dim light 30ft) at will as a Simple action. Flash overload (once per encounter): adjacent enemies must make a Dex save (DC 10 + Con Mod) or be Dazed for 1 round. Radius increases by +10ft and flash DC by +1 per improvement.

### Advanced Features (Lvl 7+)
* **Hard Shell:** +1 Natural AC per improvement; speed reduced by 5ft for every 2 AC gained.
* **Tough Skin:** DR 1 Kinetic/Electrical (no Thermal/Chemical protection) per improvement.
* **Natural Reflexes:** Advantage on Dex saves. +1 Dex every other improvement (starting with the first).
* **Large Girth:** Advantage on Con saves. +1 Con every other improvement (starting with the first).
* **Wild Rage:** Gains Rage (1d4 + 1/improvement rounds). During rage, the creature gains Strength, Dexterity, and DR boosts based on current improvement level (Lvl 7: +4 Str, +2 Dex, DR 1; Lvl 13: +5 Str, +3 Dex, DR 1; Lvl 19: +6 Str, +4 Dex, DR 2). While raging, attacks use 1 higher die tier, and the creature gains Advantage on Str/Dex/Con saves.
* **Brute Strength:** Gains +1 Str per improvement.
* **Influencer:** Free Tricks: Get Help, Menace, Distract, Disway (with Advantage). +1 Morale bonus to owner's saves.
* **Rapid Healing:** Heals at twice normal rate out of combat. Automatically recovers HP in combat at the start of its turn as long as it has at least 1 HP remaining (Lvl 7: +1 HP/round; Lvl 13: +2 HP/round; Lvl 19: +3 HP/round).

## 8. Skills, Feats & Leveling
* **Skills:** Pick 3 Favored at Lvl 1 from: `Acrobatics`, `Athletics`, `Bluff`, `Diplomacy`, `Intimidate`, `Perception`, `Sense Motive`, `Stealth`, `Survival`.
* **Skill Points:** Gains **1 + Int Mod (min 1)** skill points per level (consistent across all text and guide files).
* **Feats:** Gains 1 feat at Lvl 1 from the [Animal Feats List](file:///home/rye/PycharmProjects/d20FuturePathWiki/wiki_raw_text/AnimalFeats.txt).
* **Level 1 Table Bonuses (Crucial):** At Level 1, the table bonuses (Feat, Characteristic, Feature) are **already included** in companion creation. Do not add them as extra bonuses!
* **Advantage Die:** The Advantage Die on the table directly replaces the companion's current Advantage Die.

## 9. Animal Tricks
Tricks expand what commands a companion can execute. Without class/feats, commands in combat require Handle Animal/CHA checks.

* **Aid:** Companion can use 'Aid Another' action on skills it possesses. Owner can also aid companion.
* **Attack On Sight:** Companion instantly attacks unknowns on sight. Owner can roll initiative and attempt a DC 15 Handle Animal check as a reaction to stop the animal if the owner acts before it. Otherwise, grants surprise round potential.
* **Entertain:** Gains Perform skill as Favored with a +1 misc bonus. Can aid owner in perform, profession (entertainment), or distraction checks.
* **Guard:** Menacingly blocks a passage or targets. Attacks only if attacked.
* **Combat Technique:** Companion can use one Combat Technique.
* **Watch:** Companion remains stealthy (Stealth check) and uses Perception to raise an alarm (barking, screeching, etc.) upon detecting unknowns.
* **Bombard:** (Flying/Glide only) Advanced charge; allows companion to keep moving after charging, or drop an object on an enemy using a standard ranged attack (Dexterity).
* **Bury/Hide Object:** (Requires Int or Wis $\ge$ 8) Hides an object (Dexterity/Survival check sets the DC). Daily DC 10 Survival or Wisdom check to locate again.
* **Deliver:** Delivers an object over long distance/time (DC 10 Survival check per day of travel).
* **Detect:** Advantage on Perception/Study to pinpoint explosives, traps, passages, etc. Cannot retry the check.
* **Distract:** Uses Feint combat technique to grab target's attention.
* **Dissuade:** Intimidation check vs target's Wisdom save. If successful, target is discouraged from attacking the animal or owner for 1 round (choosing other targets). Has no effect if they are the only possible targets.
* **Fetch:** Retrieves a pointed or random object.
* **Feign Death:** Companion falls limp, forcing enemies to pass a Sense Motive check (vs companion's Bluff +4) or ignore it. Can spring up as a free action, gaining Advantage on its first attack.
* **Flee:** Runs and hides, tracks owner from safe range, and returns on command. Useful for evading capture.
* **Get Help:** Trainer designates up to [Int score] creatures as "help." Companion journeys to find and retrieve them.
* **Hunt/Track:** Uses Survival to forage food/water or aid owner's Survival checks.
* **Menace:** Keeps target from moving using Intimidate vs Wisdom save. Target compliance ceases attack; movement or hostile actions trigger an immediate opportunity attack.
* **Rescue:** Moves to an adjacent incapacitated/dying ally, grabs them as a free action, and drags them up to half speed away from hostiles.
* **Stay:** Companion waits in place, defending itself only if attacked.
* **Throw Rider:** Trip combat technique to fling a rider to the ground. If combined with Guard trick, automatically attempts to throw anyone other than its trainer trying to ride it.
