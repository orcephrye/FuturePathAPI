# Spaceship Equipment & Accessories

Space Ship Equipment is divided into three primary categories: **Hard Points** (external, mostly weapons), **Bays** (internal compartments), and **Upgrades** (fitted internally or externally via Customization Slots).

---

## 1. Ship Weapons (Hard Points)
Weapons fit onto a Ship's Hard Points. Weapon size is dictated by Hull Size.

### Weapon Types
*   **Projectile:** Cheap, reliable kinetic damage.
    *   **Range:** Infinite
    *   **Ammo:** 6 Rounds
    *   **Class Bonus:** +1 round per Class. At Class 3, gains **Burst Fire** (reroll failures for every 2 rounds left).
*   **Laser:** Powered by the ship's core. Thermal damage. Generates waste heat.
    *   **Range:** Long
    *   **Ammo:** Infinite (Stops if Engines/Electronics drop below 7).
    *   **Bonus:** +1 to d10. Each shot lowers ship Detection DC by 1 for the next round.
    *   **Class Bonus:** Class 2 = +2 d10 (Detection -2). Class 4 = +3 d10 (Detection -3).
*   **Plasma:** Most powerful and expensive. Thermal damage.
    *   **Range:** Small, Medium
    *   **Ammo:** 4
    *   **Bonus:** Reroll 1s at Small range (loses bonus at Medium).
    *   **Class Bonus (Costs 3x normal):** Choose between raw accuracy (up to automatic successes on 9s/10s) or range/reroll extensions (up to exploding 10s).
*   **Missile:** Flexible kinetic damage depending on loaded ammo.
    *   **Range & Bonus:** Ammo Dependent
    *   **Ammo:** 3
    *   **Class Bonus:** Class 3 = +1 Ammo capacity. Class 5 = +1 Ammo. Max reload speed is 3 per turn.

## 2. Ammo Types & Costs
Ammo provides extra d10s, Burst Fire/Explode functions, or changes damage types. 1 "Magazine" = 1 Unit of space (Small/smaller), 2 Units (Large/Huge), or 3 Units (Gargantuan/Colossal).

*   **Projectiles:** Shells/bullets customizable with different materials.
*   **Laser Crystals:** Focusing crystals that alter laser properties. Degrade after 10 shots. Cannot be replaced in combat, only ejected to fire normally.
*   **Plasma:** Upgrades affect all plasma weapons on a ship and take up bays/customization slots rather than standard "ammo" space.
*   **Missiles:** Highly flexible; payloads swapped on the fly.

## 3. Ship Bays & Facilities
Internal compartments. "Facilities" are upgraded bays that take up 2 standard bays (or 1 bay on Gargantuan/Colossal ships) and allow multiple workers.

*   '''Cargo Bay:''' Increases Cargo Capacity by 1/2 the base units of the ship's size.
*   '''Science Bay:''' Chemical/Electronic Kit. Provides +X to crafting based on Class level.
*   '''Medical Bay:''' Medical/Surgical Kit (+2 Treat Injury). Facility allows multi-patient care, acts as Pharmacy Kit, and provides +4 Treat Injury.
*   '''Industrial Bay:''' Chemical/Mechanical Kit. Facility provides +1/2 level bonus to repairing Battle Damage.
*   '''Engineering Bay:''' Electronics/Mechanical Kit. Facility provides +1 per level to ship repairs. Allows combat modifications.
*   '''Astrometrics Bay:''' Doubles sensor range. +1 per Class to Sensor scans; +1/2 per Class for scanning stealth ships.
*   '''Refinery Bay:''' Breaks down raw materials/salvage (50% weight/size reduction) to create crafting mats. 100 units/day per Class.
*   '''Docking Bay:''' Docks ships 2 sizes smaller. Multiple bays can be combined for larger capacities.
*   '''Passenger Bay:''' Increases living capacity by the ship's base size capacity. Luxury versions cost double and provide half space.
*   '''Detention Bay:''' Holds prisoners safely. +5 DC on escape/hacking attempts. Requires Tiny+ size.
*   '''Hydroponics Bay:''' Extends life support by 50%. +2 to organic chemical/pharma crafting. Requires Small+ size.
*   '''Armory:''' Secure storage for personal gear. Boarding prep/defense takes half the time (Move action). +2 to repair ground combat gear. Requires Tiny+ size.
*   '''Electronic Intelligence (ELINT) Center:''' Dedicated cyberwarfare room. +2 to Hacking, Jamming, and Counter-Hacking Electronics checks. Requires Medium+ size.
*   '''Recreation Bay:''' Crew rest area. Recover from Shaken/Fatigued in half the time. +1 morale bonus to Will saves and Initiative. Requires Small+ size.

## 4. Ship Function Upgrades
Specific modules required to perform Advanced Ship Functions.

*   **Transporter Bay (Beam):** Requires a Bay. Class 0: Bay-to-Bay only. Class 1: Anywhere to/from Bay. Class 2: Site-to-site. Max distance = Long. Shield penetration DC = 12 + Target Shield Mod.
*   **Cloaking Device (Cloak):** Requires a Tiny+ ship, 1 Hard Point, and 1 Bay (one can be a Cust. point). Disables shields. Class 0 gives +3 Detection DC. Firing weapons reduces stealth temporarily (Kinetic -2, Missiles -3, Plasma -4, Laser -5).
*   **Crypto Computer (Crypto):** Requires Tiny+ ship, 1 Bay or Cust. point. Secures comms and drone links.
*   **Jammer (Jam):** Requires Tiny+ ship, 1 Hard Point. Interrupts messages, hacks, drones, and guided missiles.
*   **Grappler (Grapple):** Requires a Hard Point. Used to grab/hold enemy ships.
*   **CSA Upgrades:** Core System Attributes can be upgraded from Class 0 to 5. Each Class adds the base price again. Masterworking doubles the final cost. Requires a Customization Point, Hard Point, or Bay (becomes an 'Upgrade Slot' holding 2 upgrades).

## 5. Other Accessories
Crafting these requires Rank 3 Mechanics and Rank 3 Electronics.
*   '''Warp Field Expander ($450k):''' Expand warp field around another ship. Slot: 1 HP & 1 Bay.
*   '''Jump Assistance Drive ($30k):''' Act as a Jump Gate for other ships. Slot: 1 HP & 1 Bay.
*   '''Shield Extender ($1.5M):''' Manipulate shields to protect other ships. Slot: 1 Bay.
*   '''Shield Enhancer ($600k):''' Boosts Reflector (+1) and Shield-based DR (+1). Slot: 1 HP.
*   '''Phase Discriminator ($500k):''' Bonus to scanning "Out of Phase" ships. Slot: 1 HP.
*   '''Plasma Flow Enhancer ($200k):''' +1 to Warp Speed (Max 10). Slot: 1 Bay.
*   '''Engine Boosters ($250k):''' Passive sub-light thrusters. Provides +2 on active Pilot checks using Engines. Slot: 1 HP or 1 Cust. Slot.
*   '''Drone Controller ($500k):''' Enables drone use. Slot: 1 HP & 1 Bay.
*   '''Reactor Auxiliary Capacitor ($150k):''' Stores reserve energy. +2 on Craft checks to Overclock Core, and absorbs failed overclock backcharges. Slot: 1 Cust. Slot.
*   '''Holographic Decoy Launcher ($120k):''' Required for decoy projection. Grants +2 on Electronics checks and guided missiles have a 75% miss chance. Slot: 1 HP.
*   '''Fire-Control Matrix ($300k):''' Targeting computer. Grants +1 to attack d10s and ignores the -2 penalty when using Defensive Fire. Slot: 1 HP or 1 Cust. Slot.
*   '''Hull Nanite Repair Pods ($400k):''' External nano-welder pods. Allows Repair checks as a Move Action or grants a +2 bonus. Slot: 1 Cust. Slot.
*   '''Advanced Recycler Grid ($80k):''' Air/water filtration. Increases default passenger capacity by 25% and grants +2 to saves against infestations/toxins. Slot: 1 Cust. Slot.
